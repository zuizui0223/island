This checkpoint adds seven individually reviewed species-trait records chosen
from current support=2 `genus x trait_name` acquisition rules.  It combines
four reproductive records with three visible-morphology records and retains
English, Portuguese, French, and Spanish source text.

The recorded 232 potential cells are a planning diagnostic.  They are not
coverage until the shared all-evidence workflow applies source-lineage
deduplication, conflict resolution, dominance, masked validation, and
leave-one-lineage-out validation.  This directory contains no genus inference.

`autonomous_selfing_capacity=autonomous` row whose source says that the
species showed no appreciable autogamous selfing.  The exclusion is exact to
species, trait, value, source lineage, and URL.
