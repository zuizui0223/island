# High-yield mixed wave 9 checkpoint

This checkpoint adds nine individually reviewed, species-direct trait records.
It targets two `current_support=2` genus × `trait_name` rules with a theoretical
queue ceiling of 84 cells, but that number is not reported as coverage.  The
formal all-evidence rebuild determines realized direct, Low, and species-axis
changes.

The checkpoint also preserves counterevidence.  In particular, bagged seed set
for *Callistemon phoeniceus* is stored as `mixed_or_variable`, not autonomous,
because the original thesis says wind may be involved.  *Melaleuca
quinquenervia* keeps `self_incompatibility` and `mating_system` as separate
traits, and the *Spartina alterniflora* source is reconciled explicitly to the
accepted *Sporobolus alterniflorus* rather than fuzzy-matched.

All source rows include an exact supporting excerpt, stable URL, retrieval
timestamp, full downloaded-content SHA-256, and source lineage.  The three
*Triumfetta rhomboidea* traits share one DOI lineage and the two *Melaleuca*
traits share one FEIS review lineage; neither is counted as independent
replication across traits.  No family inference, global fallback, n=2 formal
inference, search-snippet evidence, or cross-trait substitution is emitted.
