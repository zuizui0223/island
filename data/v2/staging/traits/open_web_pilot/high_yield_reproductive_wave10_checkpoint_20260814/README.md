# High-yield reproductive wave 10 checkpoint

This checkpoint adds seven reviewed species-direct trait records from two
independent source lineages. It targets two `current_support=2` genus x
`trait_name` rules with a theoretical ceiling of 222 cells. That ceiling is not
coverage: only the common all-evidence rebuild may emit Validated Low after
dominance, masked-species and source-lineage validation.

The *Cirsium brevistylum* primary study explicitly compares autonomous
self-pollination and reports seed set without a pollinator. The source does not
show an emasculated apomixis control in its abstract, so that limitation remains
in the provenance rather than being silently erased.

For *Inga vera*, *I. striata* and *I. ingoides*, an official UFPE thesis reports
0/100 spontaneous self-pollinations and 0/30 manual self-pollinations per
species, with positive hand-cross controls. Autonomous-selfing absence and
self-incompatibility are therefore stored as separate traits. All six rows share
one original-study lineage; the later journal article is not counted as an
independent source.

All rows have exact master identity, supporting excerpt, stable URL, retrieval
timestamp, downloaded-content SHA-256 and lineage. No family inference, global
fallback, n=2 formal inference, search-snippet evidence, cultivar transfer, or
cross-trait substitution is emitted.
