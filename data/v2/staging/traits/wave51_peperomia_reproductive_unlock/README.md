# Wave51 Peperomia reproductive unlock

This frozen packet adds four species-direct autonomous-selfing records from the
original Unicamp doctoral thesis and two external-congener self-compatibility
records from distinct Huntington institutional species accounts.

The four thesis records share one source lineage and therefore cannot by
themselves establish a genus rule. The two Huntington records have distinct
publication lineages; together with the pre-existing direct record for
`Peperomia inaequalifolia`, they can establish only the trait-specific
`Peperomia x reproductive_assurance x self_incompatibility` rule. The
`autonomous_selfing_capacity` and `self_incompatibility` traits are not
interchanged.

The thesis PDF was retrieved through the official Unicamp browser session,
reviewed on pages 55-56, and pinned by SHA-256 and byte count. The repository
does not redistribute the copyrighted PDF and GitHub Actions cannot bypass the
repository's interactive access challenge. The formal artifact therefore
retains an explicit manual-browser receipt and this limitation, while the two
mutable Huntington pages are fetched and quote-verified in the run.

Family inference, global fallback, search-snippet evidence, and genus-by-axis
joins are absent.
