# Near-rule source acquisition (2026-08-09)

This wave starts from reviewed artifact Run `31267113911`, not from the full
106,295-species search queue.  The fixed baseline contains 116,658 resolved
species-axis cells and 57,519 direct High/Medium cells.  The acquisition target
is the 1,158 `genus × trait_name` groups that were one additional direct species
away from the production `min_species = 3` rule, plus source-scale direct rows
that were already downloaded but had not entered the formal ledger.

The package combines 13 providers while retaining the original lineage:

- Sinnott-Armstrong et al. flower-colour data (`10.5061/dryad.r4xgxd2sc`);
- PlantNET NSW Flora species treatments;
- NC State Extension Plant Toolbox structured flower fields;
- BIEN flower-colour records with their underlying USDA or INBIO citation;
- Goldberg et al. Solanaceae SI/SC data (`10.5061/dryad.1888`); and
- already reviewed Europe PMC full-text SI statements;
- Micheneau et al. primary breeding experiments for *Angraecum cadetii*
  (`10.1093/aob/mcp299`); and
- the IISc Flora of Peninsular India species treatment for
  *Elaeocarpus angustifolius*;
- Hartati et al. (`10.1590/0034-737x201966040004`), the Western Australian
  Museum, and the Leon Levy Native Plant Preserve for exact species morphology;
- Singapore NParks Flora & Fauna Web structured species treatments; and
- Ide et al. (`10.11519/jfsc.131.0_790`) for an exact-species hand
  self-pollination result kept as `self_incompatibility`, not autonomous selfing.

The package contains 1,831 evidence rows and 1,637 species-trait pairs not
present as direct evidence in Run `31267113911`.  It is generated by
`analysis/prepare_near_rule_source_packages_20260809.py`; the summary records
the SHA-256 digest and byte size of every input.  Structured rows are serialized
as exact supporting excerpts.  BIEN is a provider, not an independent lineage:
USDA and each cited INBIO book keep their original lineage identifier.
The single autonomous-selfing record is retained in the package but remains
below the ten-reviewed-record trait threshold, so it is not promoted to the
formal ledger in this wave.

## Fail-closed decisions

Only species in the fixed 106,295-species strict universe are eligible.  NCSU
is Medium and is limited to the explicit `Flower Shape`, `Flower Color`, and
`Flower Inflorescence` fields.  Its size field is excluded because it may refer
to the display or inflorescence rather than an individual flower.  PlantNET is
High, but size measurements must explicitly modify `flower` or `corolla`; leaf,
flower-stalk, spikelet, floret, cluster, and inflorescence measurements are
rejected.  Tube depth must have an explicit tube length, and calyx, bract,
involucral, scale, and fimbria measurements are rejected.

Flower colours and NCSU shape/inflorescence fields retain state sets separated
by `|`.  `pollen_vector_mode`, `reward_type`, and sexual-system-only records are
not mapped to the strict axes.  Family inference, global fallback, and
`min_species = 2` inference remain excluded.

The second wave adds ten exact-species floral-symmetry records. Nine come from
government species treatments and one from an expert botanical preserve.
*Uvaria* and *Scaevola* were selected because the formal queue identified them
as one species short of a trait-specific genus rule. *Tacca cristata* is
retained even though its bilateral value may invalidate an older radial genus
rule; conflicts are recomputed instead of being filtered for coverage gain.

The deterministic provider-by-trait audit uses the SHA-256 ordering of
`20260809|source_record_id`, selecting up to 40 rows per stratum.  It contains
431 reviewed records, the exact source record or quote, reviewer, timestamp,
and acceptance reason.  Promotion still goes through
`reviewed_source_package_evidence`, which requires at least 200 reviewed rows,
at least ten per approved trait, precision at least 0.95, cultivar
contamination at most 0.02, and package novelty at least 0.50.

## Rebuild

The final workflow chains from `reviewed-open-web-evidence-31267113911` and
passes these files to `island_v2.open_web_finalize`:

- `near_rule_incremental_evidence.csv.gz`
- `near_rule_source_audit.csv`

The workflow artifact is authoritative for coverage.  Local figures are used
only as a preflight check; coverage changes are reported after downloading and
independently validating the GitHub artifact.
