# Figure 1 specification — Chapter 1 H1–H5 hypothesis tree

## Figure purpose

Figure 1 should make the paper's inferential architecture visible before the reader sees any statistical result.

The figure must distinguish:

- observed plant-side hypotheses (H1–H4);
- pollination-associated floral concordance as an interpretation bridge;
- H5 as a mechanistic extension requiring independent pollinator evidence;
- the new H5a/H5b causal interpretation without implying that PR142 was retroactively re-fit.

## Figure legend draft

**Figure 1 | Hypothesis ladder and double-geographic-filter interpretation of island floral assembly.** Geographic separation is treated as a composite gradient of isolation, connectivity and source accessibility rather than a direct causal force on floral phenotype. The Chapter 1 analysis first tests whether increasing separation produces one universal floral/reproductive response (H1), then whether response vectors branch among biogeographic contexts (H2), at what source/taxonomic depth supported responses are expressed (H3), and whether continuous island area modifies the filter (H4). Fixed pollination-associated floral templates are evaluated only as a secondary concordance layer. H5 requires independent pollinator-side evidence. Under the double-filter interpretation, H5 can act in two ways: H5a, prospectively, if pollination-channel retention or disruption changes plant lineage entry or persistence and therefore generates H3 genus-level assembly; and H5b, corresponding to the frozen PR142 residual gate, if visitation and effective service explain plant responses that remain after source/genus composition is accounted for. Solid paths indicate relationships directly evaluated with Chapter 1 plant data; dashed paths indicate mechanistic extensions requiring independent interaction data.

## Claim-safety rules for the figure

Do not draw any direct solid arrow from `distance` to `Bombus loss`, `butterfly increase`, `bird replacement`, or `effective service`.

Do not draw pollination-syndrome labels as observed visitor states.

Do not make genus adjustment look like a nuisance correction that invalidates the original effect; it is itself an ecological result.

Do not label H5a as tested. It is a prospective mechanistic route generated by the H3 result and double-filter interpretation.
