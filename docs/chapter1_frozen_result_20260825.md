## Why this checkpoint was superseded

The earlier atomic analysis found many confirmatory within-context slopes in northern mid-latitude islands, but individual `isolation × context` tests had low power after multiplicity correction. That led to the provisional conclusion that confirmatory regional heterogeneity was not established.

That conclusion was **specific to category-by-category testing** and is not the current manuscript inference.

The later response-vector omnibus analysis tests the actual when/where hypotheses directly while retaining dependence among categories through spatial-block cluster-robust covariance. It shows that:

- isolation-associated filtering is confirmatorily detectable in both northern mid-latitude and tropical island floras;
- both signals persist in native non-endemic assemblages;
- the northern-midlatitude and tropical multivariate isolation-response vectors differ confirmatorily;
- northern high-latitude and southern-extratropical contexts remain unresolved at the confirmatory tier with current support.

## What remains valid from this checkpoint

The following atomic-level findings remain useful as **decomposition and guardrails**:

- individual floral/reproductive categories respond heterogeneously;
- the genus-composition-preserving M3 analysis does not recover one coherent confirmatory `generalized + plain + SC` syndrome in the main northern/tropical strata;
- Bombus or any other pollinator identity is not a primary Chapter 1 predictor;
- a significant effect in one region and a nonsignificant effect in another is not by itself evidence of a regional difference.

Atomic-category outputs from run `32833362756` may therefore be used to explain **which traits contribute to the now-confirmed regional response-vector difference**, but they must not replace the canonical when/where omnibus tests.
