# Chapter 1 H3–H5 causal hierarchy — 2026-09-09

## Why this clarification is needed

The frozen PR142 analysis correctly treats H3 source/lineage assembly before H5 pollination-channel mechanism. However, a purely sequential reading can be biologically misleading if H5 is interpreted only as a mechanism for a *residual that remains after genus adjustment*.

A pollination-channel filter can act **upstream of lineage assembly**. If loss or retention of a pollination channel changes which source-available genera establish or persist, the resulting plant-side signal will be absorbed by H3 genus composition. In that case, disappearance after genus adjustment does not argue against a pollination mechanism; it tells us the level at which that mechanism may have acted.

The double geographic filter therefore requires two mechanistic routes.

```text
                         geographic separation
                                  |
                 +----------------+----------------+
                 |                                 |
                 v                                 v
        plant dispersal filter            pollinator-channel filter
                 |                                 |
                 |                    source availability x mobility
                 |                    x establishment/persistence
                 |                                 |
                 +---------------+-----------------+
                                 |
                                 v
                       LINEAGE ASSEMBLY (H3)
                  genus entry / genus persistence
                                 |
                                 v
                      assemblage trait response
                                 |
                                 +----------------------------+
                                 |                            |
                                 v                            v
                         H5a upstream                  H5b residual
                 pollinator-mediated             within-lineage / beyond-
                    lineage filter                 genus channel effect
```

## H3 — what is directly identified

H3 asks **where in the plant assemblage hierarchy the distance response is represented**.

Latest PR142 result for the broad Palearctic two-axis response:

`observed 4/4 -> after family 4/4 -> after source-matched genus 0/4`.

This directly supports a genus-level assembly interpretation. It does **not** identify why those genera are differentially represented.

Allowed H3 claim:

> Increasing source separation is associated with differential representation of source-available genera, and this genus-level assembly accounts for the broad Palearctic floral/reproductive response.

Blocked H3 claim:

> Genus composition proves that plant dispersal alone caused the pattern.

Genus filtering can be generated by plant propagule limitations, habitat filtering, demographic persistence, interaction dependence, pollination-channel loss, or combinations of these.

## H5a — channel-dependent lineage filtering

H5a asks whether pollinator-side geography helps explain **H3 itself**.

Causal form:

```text
source pollination channel
        -> retention / disruption with distance
        -> effective service available to arriving plants
        -> differential establishment / persistence of plant lineages
        -> genus entry / representation
        -> assemblage floral/reproductive pattern
```

This is the strongest conceptual connection between pollinator mobility and the current H3 result.

The key estimand is not a floral-trait residual after genus adjustment. It is whether source-available lineages with stronger dependence on a disrupted channel are less likely to enter or persist as the channel is lost.

Candidate future model family:

`genus_entry or lineage_loading ~ source_channel_state x functional_dependency + plant/source covariates`

with source channel, retention/disruption, dependency and effective service measured independently of the focal floral outcome.

## H5b — beyond-genus residual channel mechanism

H5b retains the original strict PR142 residual logic.

Causal form:

```text
source/lineage composition fixed
        -> retained/disrupted pollination channel
        -> visitation
        -> single-visit effectiveness
        -> effective service
        -> residual floral/reproductive response
```

This tests whether channel change explains plant response that cannot be absorbed by family/genus composition.

A positive H5b result would support a stronger within-lineage or below-genus ecological mechanism. A null H5b result does not falsify H5a.

## Role of pollination-syndrome concordance

The fixed `large_bee_like`, `butterfly_like`, and `bird_like` scores are neither H3 nor H5. They are an **intermediate compatibility layer**.

```text
H2/H3 plant-side response
        -> sampled floral-architecture concordance
        -> candidate functional channel hypothesis
        -> H5a/H5b independent channel test
```

Thus:

- Palearctic large-bee-like / butterfly-like decline is compatible with loss or reduced representation of those architectures;
- tropical increases in all three templates primarily indicate a shared specialized/attractive architecture because ~87% of template variation is shared;
- none of these patterns identifies visitor mobility, retention, effectiveness or replacement.

## Pollinator mobility

Pollinator flight/dispersal ability belongs upstream of channel retention.

For channel `g`:

`E_poll(g,d) = f(source availability, distance, flight/dispersal ability, ocean crossing, establishment, habitat, realized community)`.

Different channels can therefore have different isolation-susceptibility curves. This predicts that the same geographic distance can create different channel states among regions, which can in turn produce different lineage filters and different floral architectures.

This is mechanistically compatible with H2 biogeographic branching and H3 genus-level assembly, but it is not directly identified by Chapter 1 plant data.

## Paper-level interpretation

The strongest current synthesis is:

> H3 identifies the plant-side level at which the major Palearctic distance response is generated. H5 remains the candidate pollinator-side explanation for *why* those lineages are differentially filtered and, secondarily, for any response that persists beyond genus. Pollination-syndrome concordance links the observed plant architecture to candidate functional channels but does not identify the channel itself.

## Frozen-contract boundary

The original `chapter1_progressive_analysis_v1` results are not redefined or re-fitted. H5b corresponds to the existing post-H2–H4 residual promotion gate. H5a is a prospective causal extension motivated by the double-filter interpretation and must be tested with independent channel and lineage-entry data rather than retroactively inferred from H3.
