# Chapter 1 H5 — global pollination integration

## Current decision

The redesigned H5 is now evaluated at two distinct levels.

1. **Plant-side consistency:** floral trait combinations are compatible with changes in pollination-associated architecture.
2. **Independent mechanism tests:** GloBI interaction breadth, biotic-vs-wind mode and channel heterogeneity must independently support a mechanism before pollinator causation is promoted.

The second level now includes a four-context, 3,252-island GloBI extension.

## Plant-side consistency retained

The predeclared direct-evidence predictions remain supported on native data:

- northern mid-latitude: large-bee-like floral architecture declines with isolation;
- tropical: butterfly-like floral architecture increases/does not decline with isolation.

However, more than 86% of covariance among large-bee-like, butterfly-like and bird-like templates is shared plant architecture, so these named templates cannot be treated as realized pollinator identities.

## Independent GloBI evidence is now global

The frozen GloBI genus-breadth predictor was applied to northern mid-latitude, northern high-latitude, tropical and southern extratropical island floras.

Primary all-observed coverage: **3,252 islands**.

The four-context distance-slope heterogeneity test was FDR-supported in **3/4** frozen source definitions and narrowly missed in the fourth. Therefore the result is classified as **source-definition-sensitive context heterogeneity**, not a promoted mechanism.

Descriptively:

- northern mid-latitude: weak positive breadth slopes;
- northern high-latitude: mixed near zero;
- tropical: consistently negative breadth slopes;
- southern extratropical: positive breadth slopes, with one source definition supported.

This is biologically compatible with the broader Chapter 1 result that interaction-associated assembly may differ among biogeographic contexts, but it is not robust enough to identify a pollinator filter.

## Area-conditioned GloBI mechanism remains unsupported

The redesigned plant-side H3 suggested area-conditioned response structure, but the global GloBI `distance x area` heterogeneity test was supported in **0/4** source definitions. Thus sampled source-genus interaction breadth does not explain the plant-side area pattern.

## Native-mechanism gate remains closed globally

Global native support is incomplete for the GloBI analysis. Northern high-latitude and southern extratropical native cells do not reach the confirmatory support threshold, while northern mid-latitude and tropical native slopes do not yield a robust source-definition-consistent breadth effect.

Therefore the global GloBI result is a contemporary-flora association and cannot be promoted to native colonisation filtering or historical pollinator loss.

## H5 synthesis

The current evidence supports the following hierarchy:

```text
floral architecture is pollination-associated
        yes
        ↓
named pollinator identity identified from flowers
        no — >86% shared architecture
        ↓
independent GloBI breadth gives a universal filter
        no
        ↓
independent GloBI breadth varies among contexts
        suggestive — 3/4 source definitions, not robustly promoted
        ↓
GloBI explains the area-conditioned plant response
        no — 0/4 area-heterogeneity tests promoted
        ↓
coarse biotic/wind or channel class identifies mechanism
        no
```

Final H5 claim:

> **Pollination-associated floral architecture changes with island isolation, and independent interaction breadth may itself vary among biogeographic contexts, but no source-definition-robust global pollinator mechanism is currently identified.**

This leaves a narrower mechanistic target for Chapter 2 or future global data: realized visitor turnover, effective service, and functionally specific plant-pollinator matching rather than coarse interaction breadth alone.
