# Chapter 1 H5 — global pollination synthesis v4

This document supersedes `chapter1_h5_pollination_global_integration_v3_20260916.md` by adding the frozen GloPL × reproductive-assurance functional-moderation test.

## Current evidence hierarchy

### 1. Plant response

Chapter 1 retains two partially separable response components:

1. reproductive assurance;
2. pollination-associated floral architecture.

The floral response is not reducible to measured `selfing_core`, so a compulsory `isolation → selfing → floral simplification` chain is already rejected.

### 2. Interaction and pollinator occurrence evidence

GloBI spans 3,252 islands and shows source-definition-sensitive geographic heterogeneity in interaction breadth, but it does not directly measure effective pollination service. Exact-island channel occurrence has insufficient identity-crossing overlap for a global North–Tropical causal interpretation; the strict within-island northern target is structurally non-identifiable.

### 3. Experimental pollen limitation

The full georeferenced GloPL frame provides a more direct reproductive-service measurement than occurrence proxies.

Frozen global-distance model:

- 2,969 experiments;
- 1,248 sites;
- 919 publications;
- global standardized distance slope = **+0.07937**;
- predeclared one-sided positive p = **0.01772**.

Classification: `global_pollen_limitation_gradient_supported_context_specificity_not_established`.

A post-hoc shape audit shows that this is not merely a mainland/offshore step:

- mainland → offshore level step: +0.13745, p=.1084;
- two-part within-offshore distance slope: **+0.19983, p=.01110**;
- offshore-only slope: **+0.23545, p=.005865**.

The shape decomposition is descriptive post-hoc evidence and does not alter the parent promotion.

### 4. The plant H2 regional branching is not reproduced by GloPL

The frozen North–Tropical full-scale model gives:

- North slope = +0.06170, one-sided p=.1073;
- Tropical−North distance interaction = **+0.14300**;
- predeclared negative-interaction p=.9119;
- implied tropical slope = +0.20470.

Thus general isolation-associated pollen limitation is supported, but the service gradient does not show the predicted stronger northern pattern.

## Current H5 interpretation

The evidence now separates three statements that previously risked being conflated:

1. **General service limitation:** supported. Experimental pollen limitation increases with geographic separation from major continental landmasses across the full GloPL sampling frame.
2. **Biogeographic branching of service limitation:** not supported. The GloPL North–Tropical interaction does not reproduce the plant H2 branching and points in the opposite direction.
3. **Functional buffering by existing reproductive-assurance traits:** not supported under the frozen family rule. Self compatibility is opposite the buffering prediction, autonomous selfing is suggestive but imprecise, and the explicit selfing-mating-system contrast lacks support.

Therefore the best current statement is:

> **Geographic isolation is associated with increasing experimental pollen limitation at global scale, including across offshore sites, but neither the regional structure of that service gradient nor species-level reproductive-assurance buffering presently explains the North–Tropical floral/reproductive branching in Chapter 1. Pollination-service limitation remains a supported general island correlate, not an identified common upstream mechanism of H2.**

## Claim ceiling

The current evidence does not establish:

- causal island effects on pollen limitation;
- pollinator abundance or visitation decline;
- historical pollinator loss;
- causal evolution of self compatibility, selfing, or autonomous selfing;
- mediation of Chapter 1 H2 by GloPL pollen limitation;
- absence of a selfing-mating-system mechanism where current matched support is inadequate.

The next mechanistic question is whether floral architecture itself modifies, tracks, or decouples from the isolation-associated experimental pollen-limitation gradient on exact species-matched support.
