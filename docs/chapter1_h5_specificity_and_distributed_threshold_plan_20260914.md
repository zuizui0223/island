# Chapter 1 H5 specificity and distributed-threshold extension — prospective plan

## Purpose

The failed N1 channel-heterogeneity gate is not reopened or rescued. This plan defines two new, orthogonal evidentiary routes that can strengthen or bound the mechanistic interpretation without relabelling the N1 result.

## H5c — independent pollination-mode specificity

Question: does the isolation-associated floral/reproductive response differ between plants independently documented as biotically pollinated and plants independently documented as wind-pollinated?

The pollination-mode label must come from `pollen_vector_mode` evidence external to the focal colour/structure/reproductive outcomes. Floral phenotype must not be used to infer the mode.

Primary contrast:

`distance × pollen_vector_mode (biotic vs abiotic_wind)`

for the frozen floral accessibility response, with the same source/context/stratum and spatial-block safeguards as the Chapter 1 analysis where support permits.

A stronger or qualitatively different isolation response in biotically pollinated plants would provide specificity evidence against a completely generic island-assembly explanation. It would still not identify realized visitor identity, historical pollinator loss, effective service, or causal replacement.

### Outcome-blind qualification gate

Observed H5c effects remain closed until a simulation/recovery audit using the realized island distribution, pollination-mode coverage, source/context cells, baseline covariates and spatial clusters demonstrates:

1. type-I error <= 0.10 under no mode interaction;
2. >= 0.80 recovery for a prespecified interaction of 0.5 response-SD per distance-SD, with recovery reported at 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 SD;
3. at least 50 response-supported islands in each compared mode within a confirmatory context/stratum cell;
4. at least 10 spatial blocks represented in each mode;
5. direct-only and all-analysis ledgers are assessed separately when pollination-mode evidence supports both.

If the gate fails, no observed interaction test is opened and H5c remains unresolved.

## H5d — distributed lineage thresholds

Question: can lineage-specific nonlinear entry/persistence thresholds generate the observed combination of strong genus-level attenuation and no universal assemblage breakpoint?

This is a generative identifiability model, not a post-hoc search for a significant global breakpoint.

For genus `g`, conceptual entry probability may have a lineage-specific threshold `tau_g` along the isolation gradient. If `tau_g` varies among genera, sharp lineage-level transitions can aggregate into a smooth assemblage-level gradient.

### Pre-observed identifiability audit

Before fitting any observed genus threshold distribution, simulate from the realized source-available genus/island design under:

- common smooth cline;
- common step;
- distributed steps with low, medium and high threshold variance;
- heterogeneous clines without steps.

The audit asks whether the design can distinguish at minimum `distributed_thresholds` from `heterogeneous_smooth_clines`.

Qualification requires:

1. held-out classification accuracy >= 0.80;
2. false distributed-threshold selection <= 0.10 under heterogeneous clines;
3. threshold-distribution scale recovered with <= 25% relative error in >= 80% of simulations;
4. no dependence on a single source definition or spatial block.

If this gate fails, H5d remains a conceptual bridge to `izu-core`; no observed distributed-threshold fit is promoted.

## Chapter 1 -> Chapter 2 handoff

Chapter 1 may establish macro-scale response direction, component decoupling, taxonomic depth, specificity or the absence of a universal breakpoint. `izu-core` resolves individual systems deeply enough to test whether effective service, reproduction and phenotype exhibit clines or thresholds at a local mechanistic scale.

A smooth Chapter 1 gradient and a threshold-like Chapter 2 response are compatible if lineage/system thresholds differ among taxa.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- do not reopen N1 or continue to N2 under the failed N1 chain;
- do not use flower phenotype to infer pollination mode;
- do not treat missing `pollen_vector_mode` as wind pollination;
- do not fit observed H5c/H5d outcomes before qualification;
- do not call distributed thresholds causal pollinator thresholds without independent interaction/service evidence;
- do not reinterpret the global 0/12 nonlinear result as evidence that local thresholds do not exist.
