## Scientific consequence

The Chapter 1 data can support the conceptual statement that lineage-specific thresholds, if heterogeneous, could aggregate into a smooth macroecological gradient. They cannot distinguish that generator from heterogeneous smooth lineage responses with the required reliability.

This makes the scale handoff to `izu-core` stronger rather than weaker: local systems with direct interaction/service/reproductive measurements are required to test whether particular biological channels genuinely show a threshold rather than a smooth response.
