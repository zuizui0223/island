# P1d paired spatial-block uncertainty result — 2026-09-15

## Why this test exists

The frozen H3 point estimates showed large Palearctic attenuation after source-matched genus adjustment, with the largest point-estimate drop between family and genus. P1d asks a narrower question: **how precisely is that incremental family-to-genus attenuation localized once existing spatial dependence is propagated through a paired resampling analysis?**

This is a post-baseline robustness extension. It cannot change the historical H3 classification.

## Exact reconstruction / common support

Before resampling, the frozen V2 taxonomic decomposition was independently reconstructed.

- direct-only maximum absolute reconstruction difference: `1.11e-16`;
- all-analysis maximum absolute reconstruction difference: `1.11e-16`;
- observed, family-adjusted and genus-adjusted stages use the same retained island-species support and the same `n_species` information weight within the decomposition.

Thus stage-specific sample loss is not a viable explanation for the observed attenuation.

## Paired bootstrap result

The existing `spatial_block` unit was resampled 2,000 times with seed `20260915`. Within each draw the identical resampled block multiplicities were used for observed, family-adjusted and genus-adjusted stages.

For the eight **direct-only** Palearctic source-mode × stratum profiles:

- observed total genus attenuation: `0.7881–0.8024` of the observed two-axis vector;
- lower bounds of the 95% interval for total genus attenuation: approximately `0.2765–0.4021`;
- observed additional family-to-genus attenuation: `0.5028–0.5467` of the observed vector;
- lower bounds of the 95% interval for that additional attenuation: approximately `-0.2095` to `-0.1193`;
- profiles with the additional family-to-genus attenuation interval wholly above zero: **0/8**.

Only `1/8` all-analysis profiles had that incremental interval wholly above zero. All-analysis is complementary and is not independent replication.

## Interpretation

The P1 result now has to distinguish two questions.

1. **Is the island response strongly structured by taxonomic composition?** Yes: observed genus-adjusted attenuation remains large, and the effect is not generated by stage-specific sample loss.
2. **Is the extra attenuation precisely localized to the family-to-genus transition under spatial-block uncertainty?** No: the paired interval for that incremental contrast crosses zero in every direct-only profile.

P1c remains informative because it asks a different question: whether **true genus boundaries** attenuate the response more than arbitrary within-family partitions with matched complexity. P1c can establish genus-specific structural information even if the exact magnitude of the family-to-genus increment is spatially imprecise.
