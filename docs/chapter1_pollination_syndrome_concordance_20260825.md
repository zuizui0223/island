That analysis establishes that:

- isolation-associated floral/reproductive filtering is confirmatorily detectable in both northern mid-latitude and tropical island floras;
- both signals persist in native non-endemic assemblages;
- the northern-midlatitude and tropical multivariate response vectors differ confirmatorily;
- northern high-latitude and southern-extratropical contexts remain unresolved at the confirmatory tier.

Therefore statements in the earlier audit such as “there is no confirmatory tropical vector” or “regional heterogeneity is not confirmatory” are **superseded**.

## What remains valid

The evidential boundary remains unchanged:

```text
frozen response vector
<-> literature-defined pollination expectation
= concordance / mismatch only
```

not:

```text
trait vector
-> inferred pollinator identity
-> causal historical mechanism
```

The following interpretation rules remain valid:

- Bombus occurrence is not effective pollination service;
- climatic compatibility is not realized occurrence;
- opportunistic nondetection is not historical loss;
- colour or floral form alone cannot identify a pollinator guild;
- bird-, butterfly-, moth- or bee-associated syndrome labels require multivariate concordance and remain interpretive;
- the genus-composition-preserving M3 result still prevents promotion of a broad `generalized + plain + SC` package into a demonstrated classical syndrome.

## Current priority

Chapter 1 is currently focused on **when / where** rather than mechanism. Any revised pollination-syndrome audit should be performed only after the confirmed northern-versus-tropical response vectors are decomposed on a common response set and should not modify the frozen when/where inference.
