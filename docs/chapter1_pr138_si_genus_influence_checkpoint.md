# PR138 exact-SI leave-one-genus influence checkpoint

Status: **lineage influence diagnostic / claim-boundary checkpoint; not a causal genus model**.

Run: `32987896970`  
Artifact: `pr138-si-genus-influence-32987896970`  
Digest: `sha256:c8b1673aa775f2ff93c05a4996d0464334183866515b33fec8966de8e47bbcca`

The genus-fixed + source-pool null shows that the exact-SI Palearctic attraction/accessibility slope does not exceed the between-genus expectation. This checkpoint asks whether that assembly-level result is nevertheless driven by one or a few individual genera.

## Design

Every scored exact-SI genus is removed once. For every omission:

- island species membership is otherwise fixed;
- floristic status is fixed;
- GIFT mainland species membership is fixed;
- the four outcome-blind source assignments are fixed;
- island and mainland syndrome scores are recomputed after the omission;
- source-region eligibility is recomputed;
- the source-adjusted `attraction_shift = (-large_bee_like + generalized_accessible) / 2` is refit with the same distance, area, climate and spatial-block model.

No genus is selected from an effect size or p-value. This is an exhaustive influence audit.

## Baseline reproduction

The no-omission all-analysis rows reproduce the frozen PR138 exact-SI Palearctic estimand across all four source modes and both primary floristic strata. The baseline observed slopes are `+0.0643` to `+0.0788`.

## All-analysis-eligible evidence

There are **420 scored exact-SI genera** in the union of the two attraction/accessibility axes.

Across 420 genera x 4 source definitions x 2 strata = **3,360 leave-one-genus-out fits**:

- **3,360 / 3,360 slopes remain positive**;
- **3,360 / 3,360 remain FDR-supported** within their four-source families;
- the minimum fitted slope after any genus omission is **+0.03750**.

The most influential omission is `Potentilla`:

- mean absolute slope change: `0.02478`;
- maximum absolute slope change: `0.02903`;
- slope range after omission: `+0.03750` to `+0.05799`;
- all 8 source-mode x stratum scenarios remain supported.

The next strongest omissions are `Silybum`, `Olea`, `Viburnum`, `Misopates`, `Erucastrum`, `Hirschfeldia`, `Lathyrus`, and `Papaver`. None reverses or removes the all-analysis signal.

Therefore the between-genus Palearctic SI pattern is **not a single-genus artifact**. It is distributed across the observed genus composition.

## Direct-only sensitivity

There are **375 direct-only scored exact-SI genera**.

Across 375 x 4 x 2 = **3,000 leave-one-genus-out fits**:

- **3,000 / 3,000 slopes remain positive**;
- the minimum fitted slope is **+0.02542**;
- `Potentilla` is again the largest coefficient influence, with mean absolute change `0.02915` and maximum change `0.03150`;
- deleting `Potentilla` still leaves all 8 scenarios FDR-supported.

One inferential sensitivity is visible in the smaller direct-only layer: deleting `Silybum` leaves all 8 slopes positive (`+0.03235` to `+0.04264`) but removes FDR support in those 8 scenarios. No other direct-only genus omission loses support across the full eight-scenario set.

Thus direct-only evidence confirms the **directional** distributed assembly signal, while also showing that statistical detection at the smaller evidence layer can depend on individual genera even when the direction does not.

## Scientific interpretation

The sequence of attacks now gives a clear hierarchy:

1. The Palearctic attraction/accessibility shift is an observed assemblage pattern.
2. It persists in exact-SI taxa, so measured selfing capacity is not necessary for the observed pattern.
3. It does **not** exceed a genus-composition-preserving source-pool null, so the current data do not support an additional beyond-genus / within-genus floral filtering component.
4. Exhaustive genus deletion shows that this lineage-level pattern is not generated by one exceptional genus; the direction is distributed across genus turnover.

The strongest supported ordering is therefore:

`isolation -> biogeographic / lineage assembly -> floral syndrome composition`.

This does **not** show why those genera are filtered. A pollination-channel process could act on colonization or persistence of whole lineages, but PR138 has not identified that mechanism. Bombus loss, relaxed attraction selection, and alternative-pollinator replacement remain outside the causal claim boundary.
