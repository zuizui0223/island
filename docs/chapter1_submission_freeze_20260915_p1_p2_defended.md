## Current paper-level thesis

The paper begins with the island-biogeographic question: why should island flowers become generalized or reproductively self-reliant with isolation?

The defended answer is not one universal adaptive syndrome. Instead:

1. the formal North–Tropical plant response differs jointly under common observation support;
2. the strongest Palearctic within-context syndrome is strongly structured by non-random genus composition;
3. observation-process stress tests and several simple mechanistic alternatives do not trivially erase or explain that plant-side pattern;
4. the causal interaction mechanism remains unidentified.

The broader inference is reached from the island case:

> community-level trait syndromes can be carried by lineage composition rather than one repeated organismal response, so lineage structure and its uncertainty should be established before mechanism is assigned.
