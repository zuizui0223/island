# Chapter 1 V5 trait-resolution MNAR tipping-point checkpoint

## Decision

V5 is complete for the Wave36 snapshot as a **post-baseline prospective
falsification test**. It does not rewrite the PR #141 baseline. The result is
mixed:

- the Palearctic plant-response vector survives every predeclared finite MNAR
  scenario in both the all-analysis and direct-only ledgers;
- the reproductive-assurance component and the North--Tropical direct vector
  contrast can lose FDR support under finite state-dependent resolution shifts;
- no baseline-supported focal slope changes sign on the finite grid;
- unrestricted all-unresolved-focal/nonfocal bounds can erase or reverse the
  reproductive result, so MNAR is not eliminated as an alternative explanation.

The correct H2 update is therefore **pattern retained, trait-resolution
robustness bounded rather than established**. The correct H5 update remains
unchanged: these plant-side sensitivities cannot identify a pollination channel.

## Question

Could the currently observed regional response branches, especially the
low-coverage reproductive-assurance branch, be generated by trait resolution
depending on the unobserved reproductive state itself?

## Estimand

For unresolved reproductive-assurance cells, the sensitivity parameter is

```text
OR_R = odds(resolved | selfing_core) / odds(resolved | non-selfing_core).
```

At each predeclared OR, the model completes the expected focal-state prevalence
but retains the observed resolved-species count as the regression information
weight. It therefore changes the expected score without treating imputed states
as newly observed precision.

The refitted outputs are:

- within-context distance slopes;
- within-context response-vector omnibus tests;
- North--Tropical direct response-vector differences;
- Palearctic and Neotropical reproductive-assurance branches.

All tests retain the frozen support tiers and the existing global branching FDR
families.

## Alternative explanation being attacked

The existing observation-selection adjustment is conditional on measured
predictors of trait resolution. V5 attacks the stronger alternative that the
probability of resolution also depends on the unobserved selfing state, either
globally or differently between the focal regional contexts.

## Prospective implementation

The grid and scenario families were committed before the formal run:

- fixed OR grid: `0.1, 0.2, 1/3, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10`;
- shared state-dependent resolution;
- symmetric North-versus-Tropical differential resolution;
- symmetric Palearctic-versus-Neotropical differential resolution;
- two deterministic partial-identification bounds per family;
- all-analysis and direct-only ledgers run separately;
- OR = 1 required to reproduce the observed score;
- the grid is an ordered sensitivity analysis, not an added p-value family;
- a gain of support is not used to promote a claim.

For a differential scenario, the first context receives `+0.5 log(OR)` and the
second receives `-0.5 log(OR)`. Thus the reported OR is the full contrast in the
state-dependent resolution shift.

## Trait-resolution audit discovered during the formal run

Wave36 reports 219,733 of 318,885 species-axis cells as filled (68.91%), but
37,513 `Low` cells have no materializable `trait_composition`. They are retained
as source-reported coverage and excluded from analysis rather than converted to
a biological zero.

| Axis | Source-reported fill | Analysis-usable fill | Low cells without composition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| flower colour | 77.50% | 52.59% | 26,478 |
| floral structural complexity | 86.03% | 86.03% | 0 |
| reproductive assurance | 43.19% | 32.81% | 11,035 |
| all axis cells | 68.91% | 57.14% | 37,513 |

This correction was made fail-closed before interpreting V5. It lowers the
analysis-usable denominator but does not change the predeclared MNAR grid or
support thresholds.

The Neotropical reproductive slope was not baseline-supported in direct-only

direct-only multivariate vector is supported at OR = 1 but loses support under
some finite scenarios; this does not restore a reproductive-specific claim.

### Vector-level result

- The Palearctic within-context universal plant-response vector remains
  supported throughout the finite grid for all natives and native
  non-endemics, in both evidence scopes.
- The North--Tropical native-nonendemic vector difference is less robust. It
  loses support at a differential OR of 10 in all-analysis data but already at
  OR 2 in direct-only data; under a shared mechanism it loses support at OR 0.1
  and 0.2, respectively.
- No focal baseline-supported within-context slope changes sign within the
  finite grid. Detected focal breaks are support losses, not direction
  reversals.

### Partial-identification bounds

The deterministic extremes do not point to a single robustly identified
reproductive branch. For example, in all-analysis Palearctic all-native data:

- all unresolved nonfocal: slope `-0.0177`, q = `0.559`, unsupported;
- all unresolved focal: slope `+0.0425`, q = `9.5e-5`, supported.

The corresponding direct-only extremes are `-0.0148` (q = `0.654`) and
`+0.0433` (q = `6.62e-4`). Extreme differential assignments can likewise erase
several regional vector contrasts. These are deliberately adversarial bounds,
not plausible prevalence estimates, but they show that the missing states do
contain enough uncertainty to determine the reproductive conclusion.

## Verdict

1. **Palearctic broad plant-side branching is not explained away by the finite
   reproductive MNAR grid.** Its multivariate vector is robust because the
   accessibility component also contributes.
2. **The reproductive-assurance details remain provisional.** Some all-analysis
   branches lose support at OR values of 2, 3 or 5, and the direct-only
   North--Tropical contrast loses support at differential OR 2.
3. **Direct-only Palearctic reproductive slopes are unusually stable on the
   finite grid**, but unrestricted bounds still include an unsupported negative
   result. This is robustness over the declared grid, not identification under
   arbitrary MNAR.
4. **Neotropical reproductive assurance is the weaker branch.** It is supported
   only in the broader analysis ledger and has finite MNAR tipping points; it is
   not independently recovered by direct-only reproductive slopes.
5. Additional trait acquisition remains consequential. The strongest data need
   is still direct reproductive-assurance evidence, not further proxy expansion.

## Claim ceiling

V5 supports saying that a broad Palearctic plant-response branch is not removed
by a large, prospectively fixed finite grid of state-dependent trait-resolution
bias. It does **not** support saying that reproductive assurance is missing at
random, that the branch is immune to arbitrary MNAR, or that a missing
pollination channel caused the observed floral architecture.
