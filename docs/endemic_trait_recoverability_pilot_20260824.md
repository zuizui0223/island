## Recoverability diagnostic

GBIF record count is used only as an observation-effort / recoverability proxy. It is not treated as evidence that a trait source exists.

For each below-pilot stratum, progressively looser record thresholds were tested. The strictest tested threshold that can *theoretically* reach 30 islands is:

| regime | outcome | current | strictest threshold reaching 30 | minimum new-island gap |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| northern mid-latitude | generalized form | 20 | >=50 GBIF records | 10 |
| northern mid-latitude | self compatibility | 12 | >=50 | 18 |
| tropical | generalized form | 27 | >=200 | 3 |
| tropical | self compatibility | 27 | >=100 | 3 |

The diagnostic also shows that 50 islands are theoretically reachable only after substantially relaxing the recoverability threshold for northern outcomes. This is **not** a license for a confirmatory-scale acquisition campaign.

## Pilot-only acquisition rule

Below-pilot strata are allowed to acquire only enough direct evidence to reach 30 islands. They must then be re-analysed before any decision to continue toward 50.

The current minimum pilot queue contains 34 stratum-specific rows but only **25 unique species**, because nine species contribute to both form and SI/SC support.

- tropical generalized form: 3 species / 3 new islands;
- tropical SI/SC: 3 species / 3 new islands;
- northern generalized form: 10 species / 10 new islands;
- northern SI/SC: 18 species / 18 new islands.

The queue is generated by `src/island_v2/endemic_trait_recoverability.py`. It chooses the strictest tested GBIF-record threshold capable of reaching 30, ranks the already frozen status-aware candidates, and stops as soon as the pilot gap is covered.

## Acquisition execution

`.github/workflows/acquire-endemic-trait-pilot.yml` runs one bounded review-only search over the 25 unique species using the existing multi-provider shard engine. It extracts only floral-form / SI-SC-relevant candidates and never promotes evidence automatically.

Manually verified direct evidence can enter the same audited formal-evidence pipeline used by PR #132. Two high-confidence pilot rows have already been prepared there:

- `Lysimachia remyi`: Smithsonian Flora of the Hawaiian Islands, corolla explicitly **campanulate** -> `floral_form = bell_campanulate`;
- `Medusagyne oppositifolia`: controlled hand-selfing experiment produced germinating offspring -> `self_incompatibility = SC`.

They are promoted only if the standard candidate-ID, ontology, provenance, excerpt-hash, lineage-fingerprint and manual-audit gates pass.

## Stop rules

1. Do not acquire any more endemic colour data for northern or tropical regimes: both are already at 50 islands.
2. Do not acquire southern endemic traits to solve the current limitation: the ceiling is status coverage (13 islands).
3. For endemic form / SI-SC, stop at 30 and re-run the status- and lineage-aware model before considering another acquisition round.
4. If a queued species is unrecoverable, do not weaken evidence rules. Re-rank another endemic species that supports the same unsupported island, and relax the GBIF-record threshold only after the stricter tier has genuinely failed.
5. No Bombus occurrence campaign is justified solely to rescue the previously unsupported hypervolume pathway.

## Scientific interpretation

The data problem is now outcome-specific rather than a global 100k-species completion problem. Colour is identifiable at the predeclared island-support target in northern and tropical endemic floras. Form and SI/SC remain below pilot support but have measurable acquisition headroom. The next decision is therefore whether a small, high-recoverability pilot changes the residual isolation signal after floristic status and genus composition are controlled—not whether the remaining global trait matrix can be filled.
