# Repository ownership — user decision, 25 November 2026

This supersedes the theme-based proposal in island_fd_manuscript_claim_boundary_20261125.md. Data origin, not analytical technique, determines ownership. All analyses consuming the project GBIF island roster, integrated floral/reproductive trait evidence, OR derived aggregates belong to **island**. This includes mixed GIFT mainland/animal/external observations plus own inputs, even when their historical report says GIFT4505. External-only means no own plant/occurrence inputs, directly or transitively. External GloBI catalogs without focal plant or roster inputs may qualify even if their source preparation was in island. Shared mathematical methods/scaffold are not ecological findings.

The former fd main and all 70 PR HEADs are frozen in config/fd_data_ownership_20261125.json. Exact public tracked research files will be copied to a content-addressed archive in research/fd_own_db with path, hash, source commit and restore utility. External-only candidate paths are separately indexed, not counted as island-owned analysis. Unreviewed or mixed content stays archival and is not automatically promoted or executed; archived historical prose is not authoritative. External-only candidate promotion to fd requires input-chain and dependency verification. No protected artifacts, original raw records or licensed external files are newly downloaded/copied. Keep old history and source links, no force pushes, no deletion of unverified original sources. Frozen GEB remains unchanged.

New research with own DB belongs in island; fd active branches must not load own DB or public aggregates. Existing mixed fd PRs are migration candidates; no merge of legacy mixed code into an external-only main. Do not treat an archived result as new evidence or republish it in both papers.
