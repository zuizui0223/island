## H1–H4 evidence integration
- H1 existing traitwise isolation: SC positive in four regions in broad and WCVP sensitivity; other atomics differ. Extension: on self-DB, profile concentration, local/between-island differentiation, nonrandom pairing, discrete metric support; original 928-flora marginal/joint Q versus candidate mainland pools. The same-parent 4505 analyses permit per-trait >=5 support, joint architecture and directional isolation gradients. Separate isolation slopes from island-minus-source means.
- H2 existing selfing-adjusted floral access response: north high All and tropical All supported, tropical Direct not FDR-supported. Extension: common-carrier SC vs recorded mating system, genus/lineage-conditioned composition, source/trait forecast hierarchy. Different genus dependence does not imply identified selection. Original all-recorded flora cannot be promoted as native flora. Study-specific selection, annotation quality, native-status uncertainty and repeated species matter.
- H3 existing GloPL full and offshore slopes: independent experimental source, not measured pollinator loss. FD external animal and literature bridge results document opportunity and/or conditional co-occurrence; no qualified five-guild regional retained/nondetected contrast (0/20) and current bird/Bombus/bat associations fail key specificity/support gates. Network removal is simulation on observed links, not real loss.
- H4 existing exact-species reproductive assurance and generalized accessibility lower PL (post-hoc), not historical mediation. FD direct island-level Q/PL model 30 islands had all 3 intervals crossing zero, supplementation-only 19 fails support, zero exact same-island same-species overlap. Preserve negative result.
