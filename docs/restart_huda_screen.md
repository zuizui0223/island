# Huda original-source screen

Screen date: 2026-09-07. Research-only note; no ledger or promotion changes.

## Outcome

No species-specific self-versus-cross experiment result was recovered for any of the nine assigned targets. Full text/tables remain unavailable in this bounded public-source search. This is an access/evidence limitation, not a biological negative or proof that the papers lack the results.

## Primary sources inspected

1. Huda MK, Wilcock CC (2008), *Impact of floral traits on the reproductive success of epiphytic and terrestrial tropical orchids*, Oecologia 154:731–741, [publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00442-007-0870-4), DOI 10.1007/s00442-007-0870-4. The readable abstract concerns natural fruit set and floral/population traits across 23 species. The publisher explicitly labels the page subscription-content preview; full methods and tables were not visible. Natural fruit-set associations must not substitute for controlled self/cross results.
2. [University of Aberdeen institutional record for the 2008 paper](https://abdn.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/impact-of-floral-traits-on-the-reproductive-success-of-epiphytic-/). Metadata and abstract available; the surfaced access link is the DOI, not an accessible deposited manuscript.
3. Huda MK, Wilcock CC (2012), *Rapid floral senescence following male function and breeding systems of some tropical orchids*, Plant Biology 14:278–284, [publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1438-8677.2011.00507.x), DOI 10.1111/j.1438-8677.2011.00507.x; [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21972891/). The publisher abstract is readable through web retrieval even though the parent task encountered HTTP 403. Clicking its ordinary full-text link returned an internal retrieval error in this screen. No access-control circumvention was attempted.

The 2012 abstract reports self-, geitonogamous and cross-pollination treatments in 14 epiphytic and two terrestrial species. It names six unspecified Dendrobium species plus Pelatantheria insectifera as SI, and eight other species as SC. Four named SC species have larger/heavier outcrossed than selfed capsules: Phalaenopsis cornu-cervi, Eria pubescens, Cleisostoma appendiculatum and Arundina graminifolia. None is an assigned target. The abstract cautions that capsule formation alone does not establish full self-compatibility. Its seven SI plus eight SC assignments do not enumerate all 16 studied species, so an unnamed remainder must not be inferred. Source: 2012 publisher abstract above.

## Bounded search and stopping point

Public search covered exact article titles, DOI strings with PDF/full-text terms, author/title combinations, targeted species terms, and an Aberdeen thesis route. No legitimate readable full-text copy or experimental table surfaced. The publisher bibliographies identify Huda's 2000 Aberdeen thesis, *Diversity, ecology, reproductive biology and conservation of orchids of south-east Bangladesh*, but no readable institutional thesis copy was obtained. The thesis is a future retrieval lead only, not evidence used for coding.

Next legitimate gate: obtain publisher-authorized full text through existing institutional access, a lawful author-deposited copy, or a user-supplied PDF, then verify exact species rows, treatments, denominators, fruit/seed endpoints, and the paper's own criteria. No requests were sent to authors and no purchase or account action was taken.
