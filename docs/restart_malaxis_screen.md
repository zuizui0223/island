## Original source and admission

[Calderon-Quispe et al. 2025](https://plecevo.eu/article/164210/download/pdf/), DOI [10.5091/plecevo.164210](https://doi.org/10.5091/plecevo.164210), Plant Ecology and Evolution 158:476-492. Original 17-page PDF, 11,198,491 bytes, SHA256 `c884a1e38fab57c6c9140a44ae35cc196313619e58f369d4321aff435c4fd0f8`. CC BY 4.0. Publisher HTML failed but its public linked PDF downloaded normally; no access control was bypassed.

Full methods pp.481-482, Table 3 p.483, results p.484 and discussion p.488 were inspected. The entire Table 3 page was rendered and visually checked. Ten individuals per species received intact, emasculated, hand-selfed and hand-crossed treatments under tulle bags. M. parthoni had 50 flowers per treatment; M. excavata had 30. Intact, emasculated and selfed treatments all produced zero fruits. Outcrossing produced 45/50 and 23/30 fruits, respectively. These are flowers nested within ten plants, not 50 or 30 independent plant replicates.

Admit separate High species-direct self_incompatibility=SI and autonomous_selfing_capacity=absent for each named species, following explicit author interpretation and positive outcross controls. Four species-trait records resolve two species-axis cells. Do not infer mating_system or transfer to congeners. Existing colour and structure rows remain untouched. The precise sample sizes and limits are retained in each evidence receipt.
