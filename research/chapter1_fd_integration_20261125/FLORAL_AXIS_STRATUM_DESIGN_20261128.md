# Tropical symmetry–depth contrast: fixed-margin attribution and coherent null

Post-outcome diagnostic of island PR354 (original H1 run 45579499069), not a new prospective discovery. Source-first ownership: island. Frozen GEB selector/manuscript unchanged. PR352 already partitioned joint Rao Q; this design instead targets the actual conditional difference in radial prevalence used in the Chapter 1 author-review candidate.

## Fixed estimand
For each original eligible island let A be radial symmetry and D deep tube, and use precisely the same paired single-state Direct species roster, distance, area, climate, coverage and carrier-count covariates. Let N, D, S, R and X be total species, deep carriers, nondeep carriers, radial carriers, and radial-deep carriers. Observed contrast = X/D - (R-X)/S. For each island-local stratum g, the exact random-pairing expectation of the radial-deep count is R_g D_g/N_g. Sum across strata and substitute the expected X into the contrast. Decompose the observed slope on the identical OLS design as:

- genus-only local-margin expectation;
- incremental change when conditioning on joint symmetry/depth evidence grade within genus;
- remaining within-genus-and-grade association.

The terms sum exactly, per island and on the fitted slope. This is algebraic accounting, not a fraction of causal mediation, a phylogenetic model, or a mechanism of establishment.

## One targeted primary test
The 257 tropical broad-recorded islands (>=10 pair carriers, >=5 per depth group) are the sole primary cohort. Permute the radial/zygomorphic label once per species within genus × both evidence grades, retaining the deep label and entire incidence matrix; apply the same permutation to every island, recomputing island-local fixed-margin expectations and the remaining association. 1,999 draws, fixed seed, two-sided Monte Carlo p with +1 correction, no cellwise or endpoint search. Exchangeability is an explicit biological assumption; global stratum margins are fixed but island margins vary in the null. This is not a simultaneous island-fixed-marginal permutation. Report local-cluster t estimates as descriptive context, the permutation result separately. The parent slope must reproduce to 1e-10 or fail closed.

Retain the >=30 broad, High-only and literal-native cohorts as nested sensitivity panels. All use original support rules (>=50 islands, >=10 blocks, full-rank design); no threshold relaxation. No pooling regions or claiming a confirmed cross-region interaction. High-only and native sensitivity outcomes remain visible even when unsupported.

## Editorial decision criteria
- If observed slope is reproduced largely by the genus/grade expectation and residual null is unsupported, describe a composition-associated pattern; do not claim changed within-lineage coupling.
- If remaining association has positive slope and rejects the coherent null, describe an exploratory residual association on the broad recorded roster only. No promotion to confirmatory or native-flora inference without the grade/native checks.
- If null has no informative exchangeable support, report it as unidentifiable, not evidence of no association.

No animal identity inference, no new H4 model, no public raw data, no changes to the existing GEB publication lock. Output is anonymous aggregate summaries only.
