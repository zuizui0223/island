# Tropical paired floral profile transport — fixed diagnostic

Follow-up to PR354 and PR362 (257 documented tropical islands, conditional symmetry-depth contrast +0.0276202067; local genus-margin reconstruction exact but zero exchangeable strata). Prior archived 13-endpoint two-stage forecasts target categorical loss and prospective geography→genus forecasts; they do not establish held-out prediction of this exact four-cell conditional contrast and its isolation slope. This is a new outcome-motivated diagnostic, not independent confirmation.

Freeze target cohorts, original paired species-Direct single-state ontology, original H1 OLS design and source SHA256. Primary: broad n>=10 with >=5 carriers per depth group; nested n>=30, High-only and literal-native support retained. Use only one target (tropical) and this exact morphology pair. For each held-out spatial block:
- Exclude every accepted paired-trait species recorded anywhere in the block, including species also occurring on other donor islands. Use unique donor species, never occurrence weights. No test trait states used in donor estimation.
- Primary donor pool: all eligible non-tropical recorded islands, excluding the held block and its species. Sensitivity: other tropical blocks with identical full-block taxon embargo. Require at least 100 unique donor species, 10 donor blocks, and support in both depth classes. No replacement from held-out traits.
- Fit genus-level joint four-cell frequencies with fixed smoothing alpha=5 toward the donor-only four-cell prior (Jeffreys 0.5 per cell). Unseen genera use the same blind prior and their weight is reported.
- At prediction time supply only the actual documented paired-carrier genus roster. Compare donor-only blind, equally weighted represented genera (entry representation), and species-number-weighted represented genera (loading representation). These are **known-genus conditional predictions**, not forecasts of genus entry or colonization. The distinction is equal versus observed within-genus representation, not a historical process. Target genus counts and carrier/eligibility selection are observed; test labels are used only for evaluation and the inherited outcome-based eligibility mask.
- Estimate the observed and predicted conditional radial contrast using each four-cell vector; the slopes on the same H1 design telescope into blind, entry-minus-blind, loading-minus-entry and observed-minus-loading. Nonlinear ratios are formed before regression; no causal partition. Require original cohort and slope replay before prediction; if some donor folds fail, retain attrition and do not silently compare reduced support.
- Primary performance comparison: block-equal paired squared error of the four-cell category-frequency vector (equivalently Brier gain on common observed carriers) for loading versus blind. Additional entry versus blind and loading versus entry, conditional-contrast squared error and log-loss, all reported. Fixed 499 block-bootstrap sensitivity ranges with folds frozen; correlated folds and shared test taxa preclude calibrated independent CIs. Also report unique target species-equal and genus-equal proper-score gains, without claiming formal phylogenetic independence. Prediction slopes and residual slopes are descriptive.
- No first-source group exclusion is available in this specific pinned H1 geography; do not claim equivalence to earlier stricter block+source experiments. Other-region training is a held-out source test, not independent original biological measurements. No test of named pollinator channels or native establishment. No changes to frozen GEB or previously rendered author-review package.

If prediction improves on the blind baseline with positive held-out slope, the taxonomic composition pattern has transportable conditional information. If no gain or wrong slope, retain failure: the observed local algebra cannot be promoted to transferable genus profile prediction. A positive loading increment is descriptive contribution from species representation, not evidence of a causal loading process. High-only and literal-native limitations remain.
