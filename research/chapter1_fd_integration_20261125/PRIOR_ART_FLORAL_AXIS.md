# Targeted prior-art positioning of the floral-axis extension

25 November 2026 | scope-limited review, not a systematic literature search or proof of first discovery.

| Prior work | Established contribution | This extension's distinct estimand |
|---|---|---|
| [Zell et al. (2025), New Phytologist, doi:10.1111/nph.20234](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.20234) | Species-level island occurrence with breeding system, lifespan, symmetry and arrival opportunity; traits and their interactions already used in island-colonization research. | Continuous continental isolation and within-assemblage depth-conditioned symmetry prevalence, not species-level probability of occurrence. |
| [Trethowan et al. (2024), Journal of Biogeography 51:1659–1669](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.14751) | Analysed floral size, openness and colour in 14 Malesian islands; openness increased with isolation in their occurrence models. | Do not claim novelty of tropical floral-isolation gradients; test the paired depth-conditioned symmetry contrast. |
| [Keasar & Bodner (2025), American Journal of Botany 112:e70125](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajb2.70125) | Phylogenetically controlled comparative work associates tube length, symmetry and their interaction with nectar production. | The new result is the isolation association of the combination, not discovery that symmetry/depth interact or a measurement of floral rewards. |
| [Jirgal & Ohashi (2023), The Science of Nature 110:19](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00114-023-01845-w) | Artificial-flower experiments separate orientation and symmetry effects on bumblebee entry-angle consistency. | Do not interpret radial symmetry as a direct measure of reduced directional constraint or visitor breadth. |

**Defensible claim:** in the recorded tropical island assemblages with both depth groups represented, the association of radial prevalence with isolation is stronger among deep than shallow/open flowers, with the deep component better supported by the joint taxon/geography sensitivity. Individual reproductive-state adjustments do not absorb the contrast. High-only and native support unresolved; no H4 joint functional estimate. The study adds assemblage-level geographic evidence for context-dependent floral combinations, not a new universal pollination syndrome or proof of a functional mechanism.
