## B. Other positive evidence and counterexamples

- Pairing, profile overlap and conditional predictability: registry 002–006, 013–015, 018–021, 045–048. The original exact-score experiments must be reported separately from PR363 (which targets a different outcome). Local concentration and Jaccard contrasts are scope-dependent; recurrence is not above-null convergence. Known-genus predictive skill is not prospective colonization. Geographic and species/genus-equal sensitivity failures are material.
- Historical uncorrected gradients 030–031 are useful provenance (tropical zygomorphic decrease; SC residual +.297 pp, Holm .0476; autonomy residual unsupported), not substitutes for corrected H1. Do not use them as primary estimates. Registry 051 positive colour-documentation support is a coverage association, not a biological effect.
- Pair-atlas/plant-defined syndrome (PR351): 17/64 covariance and 10/64 phi retrospective same-sign screens, zero endpoints passing both origin scopes, 0/72 colour-size joint genus-weight supports. Retain all plant proxy results and robustness failures; no named pollinator identification.
- Joint diversity on conditional cohorts (PR357): climate-disjoint joint Q +.02483 north / +.01738 tropical in n264/201, but broader cohort -.03111/+ .00652. North conditional positive robust across genus/group sensitivity; tropical not. PR352 local exact margins and PR358 common-carrier contrasts show why marginal change and genuine coupling must be separated. Four-axis and six-pair primary Holm families all 1; four-axis morphology contrast reverses direction. These are essential sensitivity/counterexample results, not a universal FD effect.
- Animal-channel work (023–025, 027): 0/20 qualified local guild comparisons; hypothetical all-bee network removal +9.36 pp on 76 networks/11 islands/4 blocks, not observed decline; bat tubular residual has only four blocks and adjusted p=.273. Bird nested raw positives lack strong conditional support. No guild-specific causal interpretation, no claim of a direct floral-diversity–pollen-limitation bridge. Preserve in an explicitly exploratory/negative SI section or linked author inventory.
- Source readiness, failed gates and incomplete ancestry are not positive biological results. Two ancestry holds (028,033) and external-only fd pilot must not be promoted. The full JSON retains their status.
