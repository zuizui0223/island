Do not infer a licence from an unrelated reference or free access. Preserve flags for notices concerning third-party material. The public workflow emits only aggregate counts. To allow a private item-level review without disclosing the exact source roster in a public repo, it also seals the minimal citation and licence metadata (no full texts, abstracts, trait values or taxa) using a one-time custodian public key, RSA-OAEP-SHA256 plus AES-256-GCM. Private key is not in GitHub. A sealed artifact is not a public release of the source roster. Any plaintext readback must stay in the owner's authorized private storage.

Even explicit article permissions do not establish the original acquisition version, the licence of all underlying data, the scope of specific trait evidence, or completion of attribution.
