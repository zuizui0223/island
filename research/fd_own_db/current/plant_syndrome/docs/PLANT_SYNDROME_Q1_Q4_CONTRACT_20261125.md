## Fixed morphology proxies

Use only genuine same-accepted-species, single-state high/medium species-Direct records. Core axes: primary colour C, form F, symmetry S, tube-depth class D. The database combines red/pink and yellow/orange; no extra-long tube category, nocturnal opening, scent, nectar production, or actual pollen delivery is inferred. Size Z is a separate complete-five-axis sensitivity, not filled when missing. A dash means the axis is not used for that template; it is not a mismatch. Multiple templates may match one plant. No template fit is proof of a pollinating animal.

Colour groups overlap by design. Generalist-like does not identify flies; tropical regions may have specialised bee guilds. Specialist templates are operational hypotheses, not fixed biological laws. The strict red-only bird sensitivity drops yellow_orange, and size sensitivity uses the same five-axis cohort for comparisons with the core score. Freeze these before the new output is viewed. Include all other valid single states as observed nonmatching states.

## Four primary estimands (one directional test per question)

For plant i let x_ikj be the 0/1 compatibility of observed axis j with template k. The score s_ik is the mean of its specified axes; H_i = mean of four specialist scores minus open-generalist score. This is a morphology proxy, not realised degree of specialization.

- **Q1 composition.** Primary: island-minus-candidate-mainland mean H on the same four-axis Direct carrier definition; negative predicts relative enrichment of open-generalist morphology. Also report each template score, strict all-axis matches and unclassified proportions, regions separately, no forced assignment.
- **Q2 coherence (implement first).** For each template, average signed same-species sample covariances of all pairs of its specified axis indicators; average five templates equally. For a pair use n/(n−1)[E(x_j x_l)−E(x_j)E(x_l)]. The own-marginal independence reference is zero. Primary delta C = C_island−C_source on the paired complete-core roster. Negative predicts less excess matching of the syndrome-compatible states. Signed coherence is NOT generic mutual information, and can be affected by marginal variance; a prespecified common nonconstant-axis phi-correlation sensitivity checks scale dependence. No synthetic expansion of multistate profiles. Decompose each covariance exactly into weighted within botanical genus×four-axis evidence-grade pattern and between-stratum covariance, both with the same n/(n−1) factor. Report both on the same roster, plus genus-only sensitivity. An observed difference in between-stratum coherence is compatible with lineage sorting, not proof of introduction of a fixed evolutionary module. Within-stratum difference is not within-species evolution.
- **Q3 reproductive assurance.** On the same genuine four-axis + autonomous_selfing_capacity Direct records, use autonomous vs absent (exclude delayed/facilitated/mixed for primary). Primary island-minus-source difference in within-genus-grade covariance between H and autonomous selfing indicator, predicted positive. SC vs SI is secondary, not equivalent to autonomous selfing. Isolation moderation secondary.
- **Q4 roster inclusion filter.** In the inclusive candidate-mainland pool with four-axis Direct records, compare H of accepted names listed in the focal native island flora vs not listed; primary paired within-genus-grade H difference, predicted negative. Require at least 5 carriers in each outcome and at least two overlapping classes within genus; use source-pool and island group dependence. All-recorded sensitivity separate. Nonlisted is not proof of absence or failure to colonise, and never discard shared species in Q4. Historical establishment/within-population adaptation cannot be identified from species-wide traits.

## Cohorts and uncertainty

Primary Q1/Q2/Q3: all recorded island taxa; inclusive geo_k5 native mainland candidate pool, at least 10 genuine scored species per target and source; equal island weight within each geographic 10-degree block, equal block weights; paired block bootstrap and paired first-candidate-source group bootstrap (1,999 draws each). Require >=30 independent operational groups and >=200 eligible islands under both grouping definitions, nondegenerate intervals, and >=90% valid draws; grouping is not proof of independence. Require both intervals in predicted direction, use the worse directional bootstrap tail for Q1–Q4 family and Holm across four primary questions (missing or unrun primary assigned p=1). Bootstrap tail is an exploratory sign diagnostic, not an exact permutation p-value. In interim Q2 output use conservative 4×p bound and never claim completed Holm family. Report all sensitivity panels: climate10, disjoint donor excluding whole focal flora, native-only, all-axis high-quality, 5-axis size and strict red-only; matched cohorts for inclusive/disjoint and size comparison. Regional panels descriptive (north midlatitude, north high latitude, tropics, south extratropical), not four independent primary tests. Also report within-genus/grade and between components; no causal variance partition. Repeated accepted names across islands and donors remain a limitation; taxon-sensitive inference is subsequent, not independent evolutionary replication.

## Biological interpretation and later guild connection

The five proxy sets are coarse hypotheses. Pollination-syndrome predictive performance is contested (Ollerton et al. 2009; Rosas-Guerrero et al. 2014). Realm-based guild opportunity is a later contextual sensitivity using independently curated biogeography; do not assign blanket tropical Bombus absence (montane exceptions) or infer bird pollination only from hummingbirds. Region and guild opportunity are confounded. A flower's morphology does not establish its effective pollinator. No geographic contrast proves adaptation, pollinator switching or within-island trait evolution.
