## A2. Cross-benchmark triple-gate: just two independent source information gains

The original 78-cell result's nine positive all-recorded improvements versus LOCAL region-blind flower frequencies do not all mean the model is genuinely more informative than alternate source priors. As an **explicit post-hoc strengthening**, require each geography→genus→flower result to beat **all three predictions on the EXACT SAME source carrier plants**: (i) target-region genus-BLIND categorical frequency, (ii) OTHER-region genus-BLIND categorical frequency, and (iii) simple POOLED other-island regional GENUS-mix flower prediction. For EACH baseline separately, require positive 95% physical-block and first candidate-source-group intervals for BOTH proper Brier/log losses. This retains **2 of 39 all-recorded** macroregion×endpoint source cells:

All other **37/39 all-recorded** endpoint×region cells fail this strict all-comparators criterion, including three source-ineligible cleistogamy cases. **0/39 original-native** source cells pass, with no Southern-native independent source support. This is a more defensible **information-increment shortlist** than the broader 9/39 local-blind-positive count; it is still post-hoc, exploratory, not multiple-testing adjusted, and NOT a measured causal environmental/seed source process.

**Important counterexample in reproductive assurance:** Tropical autonomous-selfing-capacity original stage-2 model improves Brier over its local-region genus-blind source by **+0.163841** (n606), but has Brier **−0.0004 approximately** versus the external genus-blind source and no supported increment over the pooled GENUS model. This is evidence about **regional source-frequency transferability**, not a demonstrated island selfing adaptation mechanism or pollinator scarcity.

The regionally positive Southern colour and tropical symmetry are specifically strong predictive source signals, but none licenses interpretation as a historically observed island trait transition or a causally identified pollinator-shift effect.

## C. Real categorical Rao/Gini–Simpson diversity is NOT equivalent to category prediction

In a separate source-replicated, preselected **2-endpoint** analysis, predict nominal categorical floral diversity Q=1−Σ_k p_k² (unit distance between **all different categories**), from the same reference genus-blind and non-oracle two-stage category vectors. Compare matched original Direct carriers to (i) plug-in observed Q and (ii) finite-sample unbiased observed Q_hat = n/(n−1) × (1−Σ(n_k/n)²), n≥5. Use *paired model absolute error* improvement and source 499-resample physical-block/first-source group intervals. This is **nominal trait-category Simpson diversity**, NOT a multivariate morphology-distance hypervolume and not flower adaptation.

**Main diversity result:** **0/6 all-recorded region×endpoint panels** show group-replicated improvement under the finite-sample corrected Q absolute-error benchmark, even though many proper Brier/log-loss source floristic category predictions are more accurate. **2/4 supported native-only diversity panels** are positive for corrected Q, but neither represents global native/southern independent support. In the all-recorded Southern same-species colour×form endpoint, good categorical Brier/log-loss forecasts coexist with **worse plug-in Q absolute-error skill** than the simple regional-blind comparator. Thus **predicting which floral categories occur/frequencies and predicting observed functional-diversity magnitude are different empirical questions**.

Finite-sample n/(n−1) correction handles random sampling of species from a fixed category distribution; it does NOT repair nonrandom published trait data, GIFT checklist omissions, survey effort, historical changes or actual selfing/flower-guild adaptation. At n≥5 the adjusted estimator itself can be noisy. Both source group intervals are exploratory and category Q absolute error is not a proper categorical scoring rule. This negative outcome limits the inference about "island functional diversity" while preserving earlier positive category-composition predictive results.

## D. Strongest supportable macroecological conclusions and next discriminating original tests

1. **Geographically heterogeneous floristic information transfer** is a repeated descriptive/predictive result for original same-species Direct flower traits: local vs external blind priors reverse north vs south [previous direct reference audit](../20261119_stage2_name_disjoint_floral_forecast/PAIRED_LOCAL_EXTERNAL_BLIND_ORIGINAL.md); 13-endpoint successes are restricted to tropics and southern extratropics; no globally uniform three-region source transfer gain against strong local categories.
2. **Source taxon/documentation availability matters**, as shown by the [external-only congener addition](../20261119_stage2_name_disjoint_floral_forecast/EXTERNAL_ONLY_CONGENER_SENSITIVITY.md); allowing more external genus trait documentation improves exact same-target scores in 3/6 earlier source panels, without a new tropical win over strong local blind.
3. **Do not treat robust trait prediction as direct functional diversity prediction**, because the original unbiased nominal Rao Q source test is not positive in all-recorded cohorts. Further original analyses should estimate categorical richness/effective number of categories, continuous mixed-trait Gower/PCoA functional diversity if actual commensurate trait records permit, and correct for Direct trait documentation effects.
4. **Direct ecology to test, not presume**: joint floral architecture × reproductive assurance within SAME botanical accepted species, and independently studied pollinator community changes; present WOL/GloBI/EuPPollNet island-local visitor data do not supply sufficient independent fitness/guild turnover measurements. Similar flower morphologies do NOT prove bird, butterfly or moth pollinator syndrome changes.

**Nonclaims:** no confirmed within-species island adaptation, animal guild switching, new mainland ancestor floral sample, geographically causal colonization, category-frequency adaptive convergence or globally decreased floral diversity. Area/distance/territorial broad regions are observational correlates; external other-island trait donors are different named species, not confirmed ancestors. Avoid claims of multiple-testing corrected scientific significance, since the source intervals are exploratory.
