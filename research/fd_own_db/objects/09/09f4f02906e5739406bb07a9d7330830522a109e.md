## Main claim: geographically contingent reassembly of floral functional repertoires
Use the established full 4,505 GIFT botanical-island parent, with endpoint-specific Direct evidence support. The strongest results are botanical assemblage patterns, not confirmed directional pollinator-mediated evolution.

1. **Functional recurrence despite species/genus turnover.** In 2,324 floras with >=5 true same-species Direct colour × form records, mean category-frequency TV overlap across 2,699,326 island pairs is .386901. Across 842,058 pairs sharing no accepted species, overlap remains .247183; across 155,569 strictly genus-disjoint full-flora pairs, .214879. Descriptive recurrent categories, not excess convergence against a shuffled null.
2. **Region-dependent joint architecture.** Original source-relative actinomorphic × deep-tube proportions: tropics +10.989 percentage points (666 islands, block interval +8.958 to +13.313), N midlatitude +2.580 (1443), N high latitude -2.704 (135). This is nested descriptive reappearance, not independent replication. Candidate sources not observed ancestors.
3. **Genus-structured assembly.** Tropical marginal-corrected covariance excess +4.01790 pp decomposes into +4.05652 between-genus and -.03862 within-genus. Different estimand from +10.989 pp joint frequency difference. Composition, not inferred within-lineage evolution.

Both exploratory grouped intervals positive, but no jointly labelled oceanic-connected cell; cannot separate the effects.
5. **Trait-specific functional diversity.** Among 39 all-recorded trait × macroregion panels, 13 show improved corrected nominal Q prediction over local category prior, 9 improve category proper scores, 6 both. Strong strict benchmark examples tropical symmetry n925 Q-error gain +.008968, southern primary colour n184 +.016851. Predictive evidence, not a universal diversity increase/decrease; no validated continuous KDE volume.

## Proposed paper spine
**Geographically contingent reassembly of island floral functional repertoires.** Main story: (i) recurrence across taxonomic turnover, (ii) region-dependent joint floral morphology, (iii) genus mixture and geographic history organize those differences, (iv) floral categorical diversity/predictive structure is trait dependent. Pollinator composition and diet specialization are independent exploratory mechanistic candidates, not the established causal explanation. Separate colonization/retention, floral resource access and pollen transfer. Do not equate recorded tubular form with true depth, actual bird/butterfly visitation or pollen effectiveness. Keep original species-wide traits, candidate source comparison, and incomplete native status explicit.
