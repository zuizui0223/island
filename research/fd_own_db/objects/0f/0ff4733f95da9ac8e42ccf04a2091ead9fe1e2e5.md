## Crucial distinction: group-bootstrap alone did NOT exclude nearby flora from model TRAINING

The earlier original [region-exclusive genus transfer CI #43700248327](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/43700248327) genuinely excluded all accepted plant **species names of the focal island** and any species names shared between local/external regions, and selected equal numbers of distinct SOURCE accepted species per same botanical genus. However other accepted species documented on different GIFT islands in the SAME 10° original physical block, or whose first GIFT candidate mainland-source **entity** was shared with the focal island, could still influence both region-specific genus profile parameters and global reference category probabilities. Original spatial/source-group-resampled uncertainty summaries did not remove those plant names from training.

The new original source study freezes **three** outcome-blind model re-fit modes:
1. **block_out:** delete from BOTH region-exclusive botanical genus donor sides and the external genus-blind prior every accepted species NAME ever documented on any original GIFT island in the focal physical 10° block.
2. **first_source_out:** delete all accepted names from every original GIFT island assigned the SAME first geo_k5 candidate mainland source entity as the focal original island.
3. **block_and_first_source_out:** delete UNION of both original source sets; recompute every botanical genus trait distribution AND the external source prior from the remainder.

Missing source labels remain missing, **no fallback**. Each focal island's entire original accepted species roster is removed from every model. Each local vs external region-exclusive genus classifier uses the SAME outcome-blind sampled training species count for each eligible botanical genus (max20). Only original focal Direct species in botanical genera supported by BOTH old and strictly refitted models are evaluated: the before/after difference is an **exact SAME accepted target plant species** comparison, not a changing plant assemblage sample. Null/unscored source groups are counted as observation support failures.

## The *independent mainland ancestor* boundary remains important

This stricter **training location** holdout is valuable in its own right. However all local/external training donor species still come from **other GIFT island floras** and are assigned species-wide published Direct trait states in the SAME original literature ledger; the focal island's observed GENUS composition is GIVEN as a predictor oracle. The separately source-protected original GIFT 748 candidate mainland-source region audit found **ZERO** directly scored source colour×form candidate species names outside all accepted GIFT 4,505 island floras and just **9** after the narrower 2,324 island test-cohort name holdout (with zero variable candidate source genera). Thus a successful stricter training-excluded prediction does NOT independently prove ancestral mainland phenotype or colonization/phylogenetic evolution.

**Main macroecological interpretation:** genus-associated floral form and real joint colour×symmetry information can remain geographically informative across original *distant* island floras, but the relative relevance of the local source species subsets is continent/latitude-contingent. This is a supported **conditional floristic information transfer**, plausibly shaped by genus-specific starting repertoires and species-pool sorting; actual plant local adaptation, tropical bee→bird/butterfly/Sphingidae pollinator shift, pollen effectiveness and causal island geological history are NOT established. Preserve 4,505 original botanical floras as main parent, share endpoint status masks and independently grouped source uncertainty. Detailed new method and private underlying whole-flora source lists stay DRAFT.
