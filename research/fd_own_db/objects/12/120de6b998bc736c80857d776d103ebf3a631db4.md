## Primary research claim (observed, not causal)

> Global island floral assemblages repeatedly occupy recorded combinations of floral forms despite strong botanical species turnover, but the prevalence and pairing of those combinations are geographically heterogeneous and largely structured by botanical genus composition. This is **floristic functional-repertoire reassembly**, not demonstrated directional pollinator-syndrome evolution.

**Population accounting:** all **4,505** GIFT botanical island floras are retained as the source study population, even when one particular directly recorded endpoint has substantially fewer eligible islands. **2,324** floras have at least five accepted species with truly *same-species* Direct flower-colour × floral-form observations; **2,699,326** unordered pairs are compared. The historical 928-island 3-trait complete cohort and 425 independently geology-labelled flora islands are *separate selected subsets*, not the global population.

### 2026-11-18 verified next-analysis result: repeated flower categories even across disjoint botanical genera

The original **same accepted-species high/medium Direct colour×form** global 2,324-island, 2,699,326-pair study is unchanged. A *new original source-CI completed test* certifies **155,569 island pairs with NO shared accepted botanical genus across either island's entire recorded flora**; their mean observed exact category-frequency functional overlap is **0.214879** (across different 10° geographical blocks: 155,566 pairs, overlap 0.214880). This is **direct empirical recurrence of recorded floral character combinations across distinct botanical genera**, not statistical proof of evolutionary convergence: observed squared category-frequency distance **0.280641** is **higher** (more differentiated) than the fixed-masks within-genus shuffled-trait reference **0.256538**. The global zero-shared-species reference remains 842,058 pairs with overlap 0.247183. Certification deliberately quarantines unknown genus records, so strict genus-sharing categories do not partition all zero-species-sharing pairs.

Regional all-recorded strict genus-disjoint support is tropical **321 pairs/139 islands/54 physical blocks** (overlap 0.262854), northern midlatitude **10,120 pairs/942 islands/75 blocks** (0.227232), and southern extratropical **56 pairs/42 islands/19 blocks** (0.166336). Strict source-native global genus-disjoint evidence is **2,106 pairs** (mean overlap 0.234340), with **no eligible tropical source-native genus-disjoint pair**; do not interpret that as ecological absence. This source test has **no geography/coverage-caliper matching, first-GIFT-source bootstrap, island-local visitor observations or independently proven adaptive pollinator syndrome shifts**.

### 2026-11-18 original matching and geography falsification (completed)

**Source original-data CI succeeded**, not a simulated ecological result: [matched #44293839934](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/44293839934), [stricter independent-physical-block #44295181899](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/44295181899) and [complete 2×/4×/strict-native audit #44296122505](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/44296122505). All preserved global **4,505** GIFT floras, same-plant Direct colour×form eligibility **2,324** and original status-native **327**; all original source SHA256 and tests passed. [Full analysis and immutable numerical source descriptions](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fd/blob/analysis/genus-disjoint-functional-beta-20261118/results/20261118_genus_disjoint_functional_beta/MATCHED_KM_EFFORT_BLOCK_STRESS.md) stay in DRAFT PR #18.

For each original eligible focal island, one full-flora **genus-disjoint** comparison donor and one independently **species-disjoint but genus-sharing** comparison donor were matched without reading the flower outcome, in the same broad region and in different physical blocks. Predefined donor calipers covered inter-island km, true Direct flower records, total flora species/genus counts, Direct fraction and area; climate PC1 and all-three-physical-block distinction were run separately.

With 2× matching, **1,112** focal islands /139 physical focal blocks qualified globally: recorded colour×form overlap G0 (no genus sharing) **0.215415** vs G1 (genus sharing) **0.233675**, focal-equal difference **−0.018260**. After equal focal physical-block weighting the contrast is **−0.006463** with exploratory 95% focal-block resampling interval **[−0.021426,+0.008933]**. In the tropics 128 qualified focals, the corresponding focal-equal contrast was **−0.005147** (focal-block-equal −0.007543, exploratory interval [−0.042162,+0.027060]). With 4× donor calipers, tropical n139 focal-equal becomes **+0.007939**, reversing direction, while focal-block-equal **−0.002098** remains near zero.

The source-matched focal+donor geographical block graph has **ONE connected component**, so thousands of matched focal edges are NOT thousands of independent source histories. In 199 outcome-blind three-block-disjoint numerical reselections, only **19–26** globally block-disjoint matched triplets remain per draw (median 22) and **8–13 tropical** (median 11). Their observed contrast spreads cross zero: global **[−0.06299,+0.04528]** and tropical **[−0.05844,+0.07493]** (5%–95% *reselection ranges*, NOT confidence intervals). Under strict known-native, only **71 matched focal islands** and **median 3** block-disjoint triples remain, all northern midlatitude in the surviving original matching; there is no interpretable tropical native matched contrast.

**Scientific consequence:** repeatability of recorded floral categories across nonshared genera is the supported global positive descriptive finding. There is **no stable extra botanical functional similarity** specifically associated with genus-disjointness after covariate and geography sensitivity. But the small physical-block-independent support and unavailable first-GIFT-source donor clusters also prevent strong rejection of subtle ecological convergence, selection, historic colonization or actual island-local pollinator guild replacement.

## Important falsifications retained alongside the positives

1. **Not a global convergence claim:** category profiles recur despite no shared species, yet the same no-shared-flora Hellinger comparison was **0.3933 observed vs 0.4184 under within-genus shuffled trait states** (199 draws; exploratory lower tail). Absolute functional resemblance does not mean excess resemblance beyond availability/lineage nulls.
2. **Not a global oceanic island effect:** 37 historically paired origin comparisons reduce to only five conservative spatially connected components; newly geography/source-disjoint matching does not retain BH/BY-adjusted geological effects [main 2026-11-09 reanalysis](../20261109_geography_independent/README.md).
3. **Not a within-genus adaptation result:** same-variable-genus source comparison is nearly absent for the focal tropical phenotype; a structural zero from no variation is **uninformative**, not evidence of stabilizing selection.
4. **Not a documented pollinator switch:** positive visited-plant associations do not measure independent non-visits, effectiveness, pollen deposition, local island versus mainland visits, or actual historical transitions. In genus-equal within-study DoPI tests, the classic Bombus–Syrphidae morphology contrasts largely attenuate [study and genus audit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fd/blob/analysis/discrete-functional-hypervolume-20261113/results/20261117_original_dopi_gift_guild_motifs/GENUS_EQUAL_WITHIN_STUDY_AUDIT.md).
5. **Not a continuous 17D hypervolume:** the former narrow Gaussian bandwidth kernel produced near-profile-count scaling. Keep finite categorical functional beta, Rao Q, exact true species-level pairs and genus accounting instead.
