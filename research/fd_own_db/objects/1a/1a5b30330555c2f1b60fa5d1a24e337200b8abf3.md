## The scientific estimand is the ACTUAL floral composition difference, not model accuracy

Original global frame = **4,505** accepted botanical island floras: **2,205 northern midlatitude, 1,558 tropical, 317 southern extratropical, 424 northern highlatitude, one geographically unresolved** (retained in population denominator, never assigned an invented island or source region). The previous all-plant origin/endemism status check found **83.65% of original island×accepted-plant records with unresolved native/introduced status**; strict native-known and two-trait-known floras are therefore **selected documentation cohorts**, not randomly sampled 4,505 floras.

On each eligible original island and real Direct trait:
- Island-side actual plant set: strict source-defined **native AND nonendemic AND original floristic status native_nonendemic**, AND accepted species name present on one of the island's five native GIFT candidate mainland checklists, AND actually high/medium directly recorded category.
- Mainland-side original possible source set: high/medium directly recorded actual plant species from those SAME nearest-five native GIFT checklists, whose accepted name is **absent from the island's ENTIRE recorded original botanical flora**. This is an original checklist unrecorded species, **not a confirmed failure of arrival/establishment**.
- Both sides are matched **within the same island** across fixed plant original *GIFT source native mainland listing breadth* bins (number of distinct GIFT mainland source regions listing species: 1, 2–3, 4–10, 11–30, ≥31). For each bin, original weight is `min(n_native,n_candidate)`; require >=5 original native Direct plants, >=10 Direct candidate-source plants and matched effective source breadth count >=5. No genus matching is imposed in this FIRST STAGE, because genus composition is a genuine component of the observed species assemblage difference.
- The result is the same island's **observed sector share in native plants minus actual source-only candidate plants**, not any predictive score, category source extrapolation, mainland ancestral plant phenotype, pollen-fitness assay or independent evolutionary event.

## Exactly which difference is genus turnover vs actual state differences within a shared genus?

For original island i and available GIFT breadth bin b, let `w_Ng` and `w_Sg` be the actual proportions of high/medium Direct plants in genus g in island-native and source-candidate groups; `q_Ng`, `q_Sg` are the ACTUAL observed Direct 0/1 category shares within that genus in each group. Then:

`Δ_ib = Σ_shared[(w_Ng−w_Sg)(q_Ng+q_Sg)/2] + Σ_shared[(w_Ng+w_Sg)(q_Ng−q_Sg)/2] + Σ_native_only[w_Ng q_Ng] − Σ_source_only[w_Sg q_Sg]`.

The four terms are, respectively: **shared-genus relative abundance/composition**, **shared-genus interspecific Direct category difference**, **genus recorded only on native-island side**, and **negative source-only genus term**. The last two terms are huge and can cancel, and their **sign must be preserved**. The breadth-weighted combined components EXACTLY reproduce the prior original source-breadth-balanced direct trait difference on EVERY qualified island (tolerance 1e−12). Physical 10° region-block and nearest-original-GIFT-source-group equal summaries also preserve the four-term identity exactly. All components are noncausal taxonomic-category accounting; particularly, within-genus state differences between DIFFERENT accepted species do **not** identify in-situ island flower evolution or ancestral state.

### Why the combined taxonomic contribution must not become a causal "filter percentage"

Much of the source-category opportunity is in **candidate species/genera not recorded on the island**, and a large part of the observed native flora is in different genera. On the exact matched tropical source islands:
- **Zygomorphic**: the mean effective fraction of original direct island-side native plants belonging to actual shared genera is **37.4%**, but just **10.5%** on the much larger candidate mainland source side; on average only **0.70** matched *bin×shared-genus* comparisons per island show genuine different category states among actual named plants.
- **Tubular/bell**: same-breadth effective plant shares in matching genera **31.6% native vs 8.1% source**; 3.02 actual state-variable shared-genus bin units per island, much higher variation potential than symmetry.
- **Autonomous**: **18.8% native vs 6.0% source**; only 1.81 actual variable-shared-genus bin units on average per original island.
- **True same-plant bilateral×tube/bell**: **26.1% native vs 8.5% source**, with just **0.19** variable shared-genus bin units per island. A near-zero within-shared-genus contribution for this sector may therefore reflect VERY LITTLE actual comparable variation, not evidence against real adaptation.

Thus enormous signed **native-exclusive and source-exclusive** terms (+73.47 and −71.39 pp in tropical autonomy, for example) largely cancel by algebra. They cannot be declared percent of taxa biologically blocked at dispersal, successful colonization, selection or historical extinction. Candidate source regional lists can be far more taxonomically extensive than each island's observed flora; the source genus set may include many species that never had any realistic dispersal opportunity to the exact island. Literature trait categories, native provenance and range reporting effort are additional drivers.

## Scientific outcome and next identifying stage

**Positive direct ecological observation:** The shape of the recorded floral assemblage differs between strict native nonendemic island and potential mainland candidate taxa in a **region- and category-specific direction**. Its signed difference can be mechanically reconstructed largely by the composition and observed availability of botanical genus categories, while within genus category differences among ACTUALLY distinct source-listed species are much smaller and frequently information-sparse. This unifies the earlier original 4,505-island genus beta/source prediction results with an actual observed floral composition target, without confusing generic predictor R² with measured ecological assembly.

**Cannot conclude:** the source-only genera actually tried and failed to colonize an island; selection rather than dispersal/detection caused their absence; the matched source genus population is an observed historical mainland ancestor; a common genus category difference is real endemic island evolution; plant visitors switched from bee to moth/bird; source interaction availability, pollen transfer and seed set changed; or the 83.65% original botanical flora native/intro unresolved records have comparable true status. To distinguish species supply and establishment, independent propagule abundance/range/arrival data and naturalized introduced histories are needed. To identify genuine adaptation, genomic/phylogenetic independently sister comparisons and island-vs-mainland local flower phenotype measurements are needed, especially for actual trait-variable genera. Original source native mainland GIFT breadth matching is a modest source-listing/detection availability control, NOT identification of ecological selection.
