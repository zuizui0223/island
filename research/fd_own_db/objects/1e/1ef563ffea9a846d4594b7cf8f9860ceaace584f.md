## Exact per-island decomposition; NOT a causal variance partition

Let p_geo be the original geography-predicted genus mixture transformed through other-region source congeneric flower profiles, and q_direct the empirical species-Direct category frequency of genuine scored accepted plant names in one target island flora. Use actual target botanical genus composition ONLY AFTER source predictions are complete, to construct two audit-only intermediate predicted floral distributions:

- p_whole = external source congeneric flower model applied to the **true whole recorded island flora** genus mixture.
- p_direct = the same source external congeneric flower model applied only to the genus mixture among **actual Direct-documented target species**.

The category frequency residual vector obeys an EXACT telescoping identity:

\[
q_{\rm direct}-p_{\rm geo}
=(p_{\rm whole}-p_{\rm geo})
+(p_{\rm direct}-p_{\rm whole})
+(q_{\rm direct}-p_{\rm direct}).
\]

The terms are (1) **genus composition prediction residual**, (2) **Direct trait documentation/source sampling residual**, and (3) **documented flower-category mismatch relative to externally trained congener profiles**. The third term may arise from congener biological heterogeneity, source annotation practices, source domain shift or species-Direct sampling, and is NOT a measured island within-species phenotypic change.

The original observed multiclass Brier also exactly satisfies Brier(p_geo)=Q_plugin(q_direct)+||q_direct−p_geo||². The original source code asserts both this and the signed projection identity:

\[
\sum_{j=1}^{3}\langle e_j,e_{\rm total}\rangle=||e_{\rm total}||^2.
\]

**Squared component norms ||e_j||² DO NOT generally sum to the total ||e_total||².** The signed projections may be positive or negative because vector components can cancel, and must NOT be converted to positive ecological causal selection variance shares. Reporting the *largest norm* is a descriptive source model-error diagnostic, not proof of a particular ecological process.

## Research meaning and ceiling

The next most discriminating source-quality/biological step is to assess how much of the external congeneric trait-profile transfer mismatch is due to genus-to-species Direct documentation, mismatch of trait labels across sources, within-genus functional heterogeneity, or actual independent local island floral phenotypes. The present observations cannot distinguish these without newly measured or independently validated species trait states, ideally repeated in comparable island/mainland populations and linked to effective pollinator visits/pollen deposition/fitness.
