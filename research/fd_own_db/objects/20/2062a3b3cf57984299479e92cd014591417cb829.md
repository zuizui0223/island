## Why compare named independent text corpora?

The preceding worldwide source-only analysis uses publicly released NHM `combined.csv` without a source/provider column. We now retrieve the **actual BHL OCR** and **Flora of China** individual text-corpus CSVs and separately reconstruct each species' single unambiguous original `flower colour` state. These were the two largest named source corpora for *primary whole-flower colour* under the previously outcome-independent source audit. Other NHM original corpora (Flora of North America, Flora of Pakistan, Ecoflora) remain documented in [five-corpus source audit](INDEPENDENT_NHM_FIVE_CORPUS_PROVENANCE.md), not secretly discarded because of their test outcomes.

Original qualified unambiguous, **outside ALL 4,505 observed island-flora taxon names and GIFT native-mainland-listed** source species:
- BHL source-only **1,377 flower-primary-colour species**, from 106,433 BHL text-description rows, available in 8,264 original geographic candidate GIFT source assignment units (which are NOT 8,264 botanical floras).
- Flora of China source-only **1,111 flower-primary-colour species**, from 23,622 text description rows, available in 4,503 geographic candidate source-assignment units.
- No external NHM text phenotype is independent new herbarium/field *mainland population* flower-colour observation; no primary flower shape or two-trait complete filter is required for the original target main analysis.

The target species outcome is the ORIGINAL high/medium species-Direct whole-flower primary colour in the five interoperable categorical states, requiring ≥5 directly recorded target species only for this endpoint. **All 4,505 original botanical island floras remain the baseline; main eligible colour targets 3,119 all-recorded island floras, separate original native-only 395 floras, and the historic selected 215 paired-trait-endpoint floras only a secondary sensitivity.**

## Original fixed SAME-island and SAME-placebo-source control

For EACH included focal island, require both BHL and Flora of China to have genuine name-disjoint source-only measured flower-colour categories in its own candidate five GIFT mainland source entities. Select TWO other original GIFT five-source assignment sets, from the SAME broad regime but a DIFFERENT 10° physical geographical block, with original source-entity-set Jaccard <0.8 and BOTH exact corpus source-donor SPECIES COUNT and corresponding distinct GENUS COUNT within 0.5–2× of the respective actual source. Select those TWO identical pseudo-source assignments across BHL and Flora of China using only source-support metadata, **never looking at the actual island floral colour outcome**. Keep every focal original category target species, actual source assignment and two pseudo-source identities fixed across the two corpus scores.

Within a corpus, model the source-only externally documented flower-category frequency with original fixed pseudo-count `alpha=20` towards the global same-corpus GIFT native source-only donor category prior. Report improvement `pseudo-source proper loss minus actual geographically closest source proper loss`; positive means closest source predicts better. The two corpora have different original species and textual provider coverage, even when source counts/genus counts are matched. Therefore their score difference may reflect descriptions and documentation, **not independent biological effects**.

Uncertainty comes from 1,999 conditional **equal original 10° physical block** and separately **equal original first-ranked GIFT native mainland source** resamples, NOT independent botanical evolutionary histories, source phylogenies, causal p-values, or 1,965 individual genuinely independent archipelago histories.

## Interpretation and nonclaims

1. The earlier combined-NHM, whole-4,505-island global main result **remains**: recorded plant flower-colour assemblage is quantifiable, but global geographically nearest GIFT source **is not independently proven more accurate** than source-opportunity matched geographically alternative assignments.
2. The new named NHM provider test shows that original BHL and FloraChina text categories do not share a globally robust positive source signal under *both* cluster frames. The provider pair contrast on selected strictly original native floras is positive for BHL vs China under both conditional groups, yet neither text corpus is a separate direct mainland locality measurement, and the comparison was post-hoc among overlapping nested cohorts.
3. Even a positive source-specific proper prediction is NOT a proof of plant dispersal/colonization, native establishment, within-plant floral evolutionary change, selection by different pollinator assemblages or fitness compensation. GIFT source regions are potential botanical opportunities, not historical ancestors; the original Direct colour states are globally fixed per plant species.
4. **Do not narrow the 4,505 island population** to 202/215 or native-only for manuscript main claims, and do not revert to the old 928 colour+form+symmetry complete-case as a global gate. Each original species-Direct single trait and two-trait phenotype endpoint retains independent eligibility.
5. To connect to the core floral colour×FORM question, the next substantive check is quantify independently source-only **both primary flower colour and flower shape on the SAME botanical species** in named original NHM corpora. Report the source-only donor count and original 2,324 paired-floral island support, and abstain from causal guild/syndrome claims if the paired external donor universe is too sparse.
