## Why this is a separate response

**Self-compatibility (SC) does NOT imply autonomous self-fertilization**, and a pollinator-free floral form cannot be derived from either. The original source `self_incompatibility` code distinguishes SC versus SI; from **6,857 high/medium originally Direct labelled accepted species**, exactly 4,845 are SC and 1,881 SI. The **131 original mixed_or_variable records** are excluded from this strict binary response, NOT coded as SI. Original `autonomous_selfing_capacity` has **1,025 autonomous**, **434 absent**, **132 mixed_or_variable** and **2 delayed** source Direct species; the last two groups are excluded, NOT forced to absent. Only **983 distinct accepted original species** have BOTH strict reproductive binary categories documented; island-wide simultaneous trait availability does not mean plant-by-plant categories were measured together.

The primary paper still has **4,505 global original botanical island floras**, each trait's own ≥5 recorded single-species Direct eligibility without an artificial n30/all-three-floral completion requirement. This companion is exploratory and does NOT supersede the six independently frozen *flower MORPHOLOGY* directional tests in [the original floral space analysis](DIRECTIONAL_FLORAL_ISOLATION_GRADIENTS.md).

## Falsification by genus, original status, and GIFT donor group

The original large tropical increase of autonomous selfing capacity, despite a strong raw signal in 580 Direct source floras, is strongly attenuated by regional within-genus original observed plant category prevalences, **+0.058 [−0.211,+0.334] percentage points**. It is compatible with **taxonomic genus replacement of documented selfing ability labels** under isolation, not proven within-genus transition from SI to selfing.

Tropical **SC** is a different result. In the original all-recorded 775-island sample it retains a small genus-relative positive slope **+0.297 [+0.101,+0.505] percentage points** by geographic blocks; an alternative equal-first-GIFT-source grouping with 81 source groups shows genus-relative **+0.386 [+0.115,+0.679]**. This is a robust-to-two-observed-cluster-choices **conditional known-source floristic category association**, not evidence of actual autogamy or pollen release.

**Crucial native-source caveat:** the strictly source-original native-known tropical subset has only **116** islands with n≥5 source-Direct SC/SI status (42 geographic blocks), giving original raw SC +0.867 percentage points **[−1.728,+2.700]** and genus-relative +0.505 **[−0.108,+1.025]**, both crossing zero. The native botanical status selection, group/history imbalance and direct documentary source coverage prevent a generalized native-island evolutionary self-compatibility claim. For native-known tropical autonomous-source subset, only **93** floras/36 blocks qualify; its source-genus-relative +0.861 [+0.064,+1.550] is exploratory, nonrandomly selected and not an independent confirmation of the all-recorded category response.

The high-latitude SC genus-relative apparent +0.549 remains positive under physical blocks but its 18 first-GIFT-source groups show genus-relative +0.292 [−0.053,+0.767], so the sign is **not** independently robust to GIFT source-history weighting. Some original geographic blocks share native botanical source and genus histories.

There are **578 tropical islands** with n≥5 strictly binary Direct SC and n≥5 independently Direct autonomous labels, allowing a future **matched-same-island** correlation audit. But different plants may supply the two categorical measurements; we have NOT demonstrated a plant-level joint SC+autonomous adaptation or mediation through actual pollinator scarcity.

## Implication for original island floral functional-diversity hypotheses

This is the requested additional *reproductive functional axis* beside the categorical morphology-space analyses, not a morphological hypervolume dimension:
- In the **same named tropical region**, recorded zygomorphic floral proportion decreases with isolation (original morphology/Holm source effect) while original SC and autonomous selfing capability documentation fractions increase with isolation. The flower-symmetry and autonomous sector raw associations largely disappear under in-region genus-conditioning; tropical SC retains a small but limited, original native-sensitive residual.
- Such co-occurrence of island macro patterns is **compatible with** lineage/genus turnover and some residual floristic filtering; it is NOT evidence for an evolved pollinator shift, reproductive assurance gain, or genetically inherited change within source plant species.
- Missing or ambiguous original source trait states, plant native-status uncertainty, source genus taxonomic distribution, differential GIFT botanical survey effort, shared 10° source blocks and phylogenetic nonindependence must be carried forward.
- Strict same-species SI/SC and autonomous capacity records plus island-specific pollen limitation, effective visitor observations and independent mainland native plant phenotypes are required before proposing functional mediation, ancestral origin or selection.
