## Exact estimator and double source-group support gate

For each true species-Direct endpoint (one of ten single category traits or three genuine same-species pair traits), compare **Brier/log loss of LOCAL region genus-blind prior − Brier/log loss of EXTERNAL region genus-blind prior** on the identical held-out target plants. Positive is better *external* transport; negative is better *local*. Original 499-resample exploratory intervals were computed **DIRECTLY from the paired island source-reference score differences** (not by subtracting independently bootstrapped marginal model intervals), once by source 10° physical-block-equal grouping and once by first candidate mainland source-entity-group-equal grouping.

An **external-better** label requires both Brier and log loss 95% interval lower bounds >0, under BOTH independent groupings (four directional interval checks). **Local-better** requires all four upper bounds <0. Any other supported result is **ambiguous**. A missing cluster identity / insufficient source donors is explicitly **unsupported**. These 95% intervals are exploratory and **not multiple-testing adjusted**, and different flower categories and pairs are **not evolutionarily independent biological replicates**.

## Strongest interpretational brake: tropical selfing baseline reversal

Tropical recorded autonomous-selfing-capacity original geography→genus→Direct trait model previously had a large **+0.163841** Brier improvement relative to LOCAL genus-blind trait frequency. The newly directly bootstrapped **local minus external blind reference** is **+0.164218 [0.149755,0.179828]** (n606), and external-blind prediction is already slightly better than the source two-stage model. Thus the large local-referenced score is **explained numerically by a reference-distribution mismatch**, not by identified island-evolved reproductive assurance or a measured pollen-limitation event. The exact loss identity is statistical; do NOT call this a causal fraction mediated by regional environment or source pool.

Biologically testable follow-up is whether this baseline reversal covaries with **original geographic source-trait record coverage, source region overlap, trait-category annotation differences and original independent pollinator-guild availability**, with appropriate species/genus phylogenetic holdouts. Current records do **not** identify mainland ancestry, true colonization, island adaptation or local pollination transition.
