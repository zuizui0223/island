## Strict nature of the test: taxon NAME-exclusive other GIFT islands, focal genus oracle

For each original GIFT geographic region, select botanical genus-conditional donor species present ONLY in that geographic region versus different accepted donor species appearing ONLY in OTHER regions; taxa occurring in both regional source sets are removed from both. The entire focal island's accepted floristic species names are excluded from both training sets, not just directly scored floral carriers. Original donor species matching is same documented genus, balanced number of source species per genus and selected by deterministic outcome-blind botanical name hash; an unseen genus uses a shared source-blind prior. Both predictors are scored against the same original focal direct flower states, using proper **Brier and log-loss** and source-original 999 block-resampling summaries. This is a valuable check on **within-genus geographical portability of species-level flora traits**, but the focal island's realized genus composition is PROVIDED to both models: it is a conditional-oracle test, not prediction of which genera colonize an island. The source region-specific training plants here are derived from **other GIFT ISLAND floras**, not independently phenotyped mainland ancestors.

**Sign definition:** Brier gain = [predictive squared loss with externally region-exclusive same-genus trait donors] MINUS [loss with local region-exclusive same-genus trait donors]. Positive means geographic-local source donors offer better prediction, negative means **other-region donors are better**, conditional on the same observed island genus composition. Values are Brier loss units, not percentage-point flower frequency differences, fitness or guild shift.

## Ecological contribution, limitations and manuscript target

**New main positive pattern:** the degree to which a botanically observed *genus* predicts its own different species' floral characteristics is **geographically contingent**. Local region-trait information matters strongly for many northern-midlatitude flower traits. In tropical floras, some plant traits, including floral form and actual colour×symmetry joint categories, are more transferable from other regions, although other botanical flower aspects still benefit from local source data. This is compatible with a **starting taxonomic pool × region-specific floral trait configuration** framework; it does NOT establish common convergent pollinator guild shifts, within-species trait evolution, localized pollination efficacy, or ecological adaptation.

**Main manuscript decision:** retain 4,505 original botanical floras as parent, original observed trait-specific n>=5 evidence windows, exact same-species paired categories, genus/source/macrogeography, strict source-focal accepted-name holdout and BOTH spatial/first-candidate source uncertainty summaries. Treat entire test as CONDITIONAL botanical association using GIFT other-island floras, not independent phylogenetic confirmation. Interregional transfer results are **strong descriptive-predictive complements** to the original all-island functional-category beta and region-dependent true flower architecture, not a substitute for independent mainland phenotype records.
