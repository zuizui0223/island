## Interpretation and next gate
Bird-side dietary label specificity beyond family composition is now supported in some nested public phenotype cohorts, but not beyond genus composition. This is distinct from the plant-side genus×quality permutations on original GIFT4505, which had 0/500 BH support. The most informative next targeted test is *both sides of the mutualism on the same exact cohort*: frozen original Direct tubular outcome, original common bird incidence, family/genus-constrained diet permutation and plant genus×grade null, simultaneous testing with original native and high-quality restrictions; additionally compare min5/min50 on identical island cohorts rather than changing the sample. No merging into confirmed main evidence at this stage.
