## What the new residual does and does not measure

For an eligible pair of original island botanical floras `i,j`, `p_i(c)` denotes its within-island frequency of **directly observed paired flower-colour/form category c**. The primary scalar is:

`R_ij = sum_c[p_i(c)-p_j(c)]^2 - E_genus{sum_c[p_i(c)-p_j(c)]^2}`.

The expectation is analytical under the original **whole-category-profile permutations within botanical genus**, preserving **the original named-species incidence mask**. It is a *GLOBAL* genus reference: it is NOT stratified by finer climate, exact island source/ancestry, phylogenetic tree, botanical native status, trait-provider or regional mainland species availability. The eligible focal dyads are within the same broad region, across distinct 10° physical spatial blocks, and disjoint in accepted species **within the currently chosen floral-status scope**. The global/all-recorded test therefore excludes any original whole-flora shared accepted species. The native-scope exploratory test **initially excluded only species shared within the native-source status subset**, NOT every species shared across the two complete original all-recorded floras. This substantive identity restriction is corrected explicitly in the second, stricter follow-up. The first code's output provenance wording was amended in [source audit commit 12e27e2](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/commit/12e27e2bf60163dd10c81092049216f01a47c943) without changing its underlying calculations.

The outcomes are **frequency distances between recorded floras**, not within-species flower evolution, pollinator effectiveness, floral niche volume, convergence or historical guild replacement. Dyads reuse island endpoints and spatial source histories; their counts are NEVER inferential replication counts. As descriptive dependence checks, report pair-equal, island-equal and physical-block-equal residuals and the range after deleting each entire physical block. Leave-one-block min/max is NOT a 95% interval or a randomization p-value.

## B. STRONGER exact same-island and exact same-pair native-source comparison

To remove the most serious previous confound, the second original-source run first requires **at least 5 original accepted native-labeled Direct colour+form plants in each focal flora** (327 initial islands, full botanical universe still 4,505). Before reading morphological outcomes it selects **identical focal island pairs in both status scopes**, same broad region, different physical 10° blocks, full original **all-recorded-flora accepted-species disjointness**, and valid independent coordinates. This is much stricter than the earlier native-only no-shared-native-species pair mask.

## What remains a justified research claim

1. Recorded island plant floras show quantifiable floral functional category frequencies and shared profile repertoires even with zero shared accepted species, distinguishable from direct plant species turnover.
2. Genus composition, botanical provenance and spatially separated sampling frames explain considerable sensitivity of regional floral beta residuals; the original global within-genus shuffling is only one source-blind reference, not an ecological counterfactual.
3. The supplied direct botanical categories have no within-species population measurements or same-plant effective flower-visitor observations. Neither adaptive flower evolution, historical channel-switching, reproductive assurance benefits nor a universal mainland/oceanic floral syndrome follows.

**Next confirmatory gate:** cross-source independent native botanical opportunity and effort on many independent archipelagos; finer geography/region-local mainland-genus donor calibration; predeclared phylogeny/family constraints; positive within-region near-distance support; and actual plant-specific pollination service when addressing guild loss. Broader 4,505-island trait-specific macro-ecology remains the primary frame; strict native and matched subsets are sensitivity/identification analyses, not a replacement for the full study population.
