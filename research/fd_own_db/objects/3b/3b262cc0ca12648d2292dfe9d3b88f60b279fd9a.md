# Global GIFT floral repertoire without the focal-island genus oracle: seven conditional traits collapse to one source-pool transfer signal

**Source-verified original-data prediction and explicit counterexample, 19 November 2026.** This study uses the full **4,505 GIFT accepted botanical island floras** as the original macroecological population, ten high/medium species-Direct floral endpoints and three genuinely SAME-accepted-species two-axis phenotypes, **four** independently assigned GSHHG macroregions, **two** nested all-recorded/native documentation scopes, and **three** fully source-trained geographical blacklists. It is a **conditional original data predictive analysis**, not a claim of observed colonization, ancestral mainland flower phenotypes, effective pollination, or independent convergent evolutionary changes.

## What is genuinely new compared with the earlier 7/13 result?

Earlier [source-verified 7/13 strict geographically held-out genotype-genus category prediction](../20261119_strict_training_geo_source_holdout/GENUS_OVER_BLIND_TWO_PROPER_SCORES.md) finds that **KNOWING the actual genus identity of each focal GIFT island plant** helps predict its Direct floral phenotype from a different accepted species of the same genus documented in OTHER macroregions, after fully excluding plant names from the focal and adjacent island floras. That conditional result remains valid. It does NOT show that a model could forecast the focal island's genus composition or its functional flower composition prospectively.

The new model deliberately **does NOT read the focal island genus roster when constructing predicted category probabilities**. Instead, it:

1. Learns **regional source species-pool genus weights** from the count of differently named, High/Medium trait-scored plant species in OTHER original GIFT island floras of the TARGET broad macroregion. All species names appearing on ANY GIFT island in the focal 10° block, the focal first geo_k5 candidate mainland source group, or their union are excluded from learning. The focal species names are additionally excluded as a cross-validation source integrity condition; their genus roster contributes **no predictive weight**.
2. Learns original floral trait frequencies from entirely different accepted species in OTHER broad GIFT regions conditional on genus, with the same original outcome-blind donor-name count matching, smoothing \`ALPHA=5\`, a correctly held-out external-category source prior, and exact TRUE same accepted-species paired states.
3. Predicts the **whole Direct-carrier floral categorical profile of the focal original island**, including genera for which no external congeners are documented (they default to external genus-blind source prior). No favorable source-genus-matched focal carriers are selected after observing outcomes; the minimum original Direct documentation is five plants per island.
4. Evaluates THREE genuinely distinct predictions on **the same observed focal accepted plants**: (i) externally pooled **genus-BLIND** category source prior, (ii) an **in-region** pooled trait-category source prior (itself geography/source-held-out), and (iii) source-region inferred **GENUS MIXTURE × other-region genus-specific floral traits**. A fourth explicitly labelled **focal-genus ORACLE** comparator measures the gain from knowing every target genus in advance, but its true genus input is not made available to the new model.

The inferred genus weights are **counts of documented Direct-state source species by genus**, **not actual island-specific colonization probabilities, direct climatic SDM genus occupancy, or observed within-island genus frequencies**. This is one deliberately simple and transparent no-oracle model, not a proof that every possible oracle-free prediction model must fail.

## Original support/coverage: all 4,505 parent island floras are retained

The full study has 4 original regions × 13 Direct endpoints × 2 nested plant provenance scopes × 3 geographical donor-group blacklists = **312 source support panels**. Exactly **187** panels have >=30 original focal Direct-scoreable island floras AND >=10 physically different original 10° blocks plus >=10 first source entity groups, following additional explicit constraints: >=20 distinct trait-scored source species in EACH target-region and other-region source, >=2 botanical genera with external congeners, and >=10% of region-local source Direct botanical species belonging to source genera with other-region congeners. An additional **84** have no eligible source-pool genus-mix and matched original >=5 Direct focal flora support, and **41** have scored floras but insufficient original independent block/source groups. Original geographically classified 4,504 island floras plus one original unresolved flora remain in the parent, not a 187-panel ecological population replacement.

**Original group bootstrap:** 499 outcome-blind whole-original-cluster resamples, separately 10° block-equal and first candidate mainland-source-entity-equal. All results require *both proper multiclass Brier AND log-loss* intervals to exclude zero in the SAME direction for both grouping modes. Clusters, endpoints, regions and source plant observations are NOT independent evolutionary replicates, and source 95% bootstrap intervals are exploratory, NOT multiple-testing-adjusted predictive uncertainty for new evolutionary histories.

## Important regional and functional counterexamples

For **all-recorded, strongest union training holdout**, there are 12 source-qualified trait categories in each of northern-midlatitude, tropical and southern-extratropical (only 3 at northern high latitude); the no-oracle source mixture vs external blind category prior has **8 positive /2 negative** in northern-midlatitude, **5 positive /2 negative** in tropics, and just **2 positive /5 negative** in southern extratropical source data. Thus the model is NOT a universally positive global floristic source predictor. Against the stronger regional blind categorical source prior, northern midlatitude has **ZERO positive /7 negative**, tropics 3 positive /2 negative, and southern extratropics 6 positive /2 negative; regional taxonomic trait occurrence, annotation and genus-weight mismatches matter.

The genuine **flower form×symmetry** TRUE same-accepted-species category gains against external blind in north+tropics but is **inconclusive/negative** in southern extratropics. Direct floral symmetry, floral colour, inflorescence display and flower size also fail a globally consistent signed no-oracle three-region source result, despite having been valuable when the actual focal genus was known. Original TRUE colour×form has mixed region/cluster source results. **Autonomous selfing capacity is a negative no-oracle source-genus prediction relative to external blind in northern midlatitudes, tropics and southern extratropics**, but this is only prediction, not absence of heritable selfing or source geographic differentiation.

**Strict known-native-only flora subset** has no three-region supported endpoints for either no-oracle baseline because southern extratropical original Direct native trait/geo source group support drops to zero. This is insufficient independent sampling support, NOT a negative demonstration that native plants lack genus-linked flower traits.

## Ecological interpretation and scientific ceiling

**What changed:** source original phylogenetically/taxonomically associated flower categories remain **predictive WITH the observed local botanical genus community available as input**. Simply replacing that true local genus composition by taxonomic occurrence weights learned from other original GIFT island floras removes six of the seven three-region source signals against an external blind prior and ALL seven against a locally documented trait-frequency reference. This identifies a practical **functional assembly / taxonomic source composition forecasting bottleneck**: how much of the appropriate genus repertoire arrives or becomes documented on each island matters for using genus-associated flower trait information as predictive ecology.

**What it does NOT prove:** It does NOT establish evolutionary trait adaptation, geological colonization filtering mechanisms, bird/butterfly/hawkmoth pollinator shifts, a globally universal absence of oracle-free floristic predictability, an independent mainland ancestral floral pool, or a theoretically optimal maximum predictive power. The source genus weights are a **simple bias-prone count of distinct trait-documented region-exclusive accepted plant names**, not a predictive SDM trained on original island area, climate, distance, geological age, propagule pressure, or actual mainland occupancy; a better trait-observation-adjusted **genus occurrence/colonization model** might perform differently.

Main botanical study remains the full **4,505 GIFT island floral functional-category diversity, region-dependent true joint floral architecture composition, and phylogenetically/taxonomically structured species-pool reassembly**, with the seven-conditionals and oracle-free sensitivity as separate, highly informative predictive results. Next real macro-ecological test should explicitly model **which botanical genera are available and established** from island geography, past continent connection, ecology and phylogenetic group under source training exclusion; do not force animal guild causal inference from the sparse/aggregated WOL, GloBI or EuPPollNet positive-only network evidence.
