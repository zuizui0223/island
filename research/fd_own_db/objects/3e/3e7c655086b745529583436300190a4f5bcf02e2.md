## 1. The actual external source and provenance limitations

The original NHM `combined.csv` had **59,369 unique exact binomial plant names**, 59,369 row descriptions, of which the original source provider column is **ABSENT**. The provider-name column in an individual `ecoflora.csv` source is not present in the combined table; the original parser explicitly labels the combined rows `combined_source_unresolved` instead of inventing independent provider citations. Thus the **combined table cannot alone provide independent per-publication/individual mainland provider holdout**. A separate original five-corpus (BHL, Ecoflora, Flora of China, Flora of North America, Flora of Pakistan) source-file recovery and attribution audit is required.

Only directly described, exactly matched, unambiguous one-category flower states are accepted. Text with multiple different colour families, OCR-numerical contamination, unresolved names or multi-rank taxon labels is excluded rather than imputed as a single state. Flower shape and symmetry are independent endpoints; no mandatory complete three-flower-trait screening was restored.

## 3. Does this new source opportunity cover the ORIGINAL global plant assemblages?

For **original directly measured flower-colour target plants** (not triple-complete floral state cases), the original source universe has **3,132 eligible GIFT island floras**, representing **420,617** island×colour-Direct plant carrier records. In the candidate native mainland source lists for these island floras, **2,250/3,132 islands** have at least one genus with an outside-all-islands NHM primary-colour-known GIFT native mainland species, but only **19,107/420,617 = 4.543%** of original Direct plant carrier records share a genus with such a source-only externally described nearby mainland species.

On the original posthoc **215 northern-midlatitude island endpoints** of the same 456 species-disjoint island-pair test, **185/215 islands** have at least one congeneric outside-all-islands NHM primary-colour direct donor in their five nearest mainland candidate source regions; however, only **709/23,475 = 3.020%** of the target plants' Direct colour-plant carrier records have such nearby congeneric donor support.

**Key distinction**: newly measured external mainland-listed plant trait source category opportunity can be large in absolute unique species (`n=2,751`) yet have poor **focal island genus-conditional coverage** because botanical island plants and GIFT source trait literature sample different sets of genera/recording contexts. A near-zero source condition is NOT evidence of no true mainland source genus or local pollination niche: it is an evidence bottleneck.
