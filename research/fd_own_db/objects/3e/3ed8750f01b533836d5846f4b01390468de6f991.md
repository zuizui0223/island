## Main distinctions and falsifications

**Source-conditional candidate**: Tropical recorded source-flora SC representation has a small positive continent-distance gradient after regional genus-category expectation, +0.297 percentage points per mainland-isolation doubling (blocked 95% [+0.101,+0.505], Holm8 0.04755). High-latitude recorded SC also has a positive genus-conditioned directional source result (+0.549 percentage points, Holm8 0.04755), but because high latitude is a nested geographical sample, these cannot be called independent geographical replications. Neither records direct individual reproductive assurance experiments or observed island plant-population evolutionary change.

**Species compositional replacement remains a strong rival:** Tropical autonomous-selfing fraction shows a larger raw +1.053 percentage-point gradient but collapses to +0.058 after genus-reference subtraction, its interval spanning zero and adjusted posthoc p=1.0. The much smaller native-known tropical subset includes **116 SC island floras and 93 autonomous island floras**, with both strict native trait raw isolation block intervals spanning zero; some genus-adjusted raw intervals differ in sign/size. Both source status and botanical island inclusion change, so this is not an introduced species treatment effect.

**No common reproductive-architecture same-taxon syndrome:** A recorded SC label is not a recorded autonomous-self-fertilizing flower. Counts for two independent marginal traits on the SAME ISLAND are NOT proof those traits cooccur on the SAME PLANT. Only 983 originally accepted source species worldwide have both strict labels. Real joint/multivariate reproductive assurance and actual field tests would be required before inferring a compound island breeding syndrome.

**No new causal evidence of pollinator decrease:** Neither SC nor autonomous reproductive trait observations include effective pollination visitors, pollen supplementation fitness results, time series of island pollinator loss, verified native/endemic ancestral morphology, nor ecological dispersal selection mechanism. The marine island geological type is typed in only part of the botanical universe and is not inferred from distance to continents.
