**Conservative falsification and positive controls**:

Thus it is the **configuration of flower characters**, not a single deep-tube label, that is a plausible pollination-system access hypothesis.
- In DoPI the same pair traits' positive-study associations become much smaller and mostly have intervals including zero if the positive visited plant species are restricted to botanical genera shared across observed guilds. The original GIFT island genus four-part decomposition (especially relative genus weights and exclusive recorded genera) and original source-only mainland botanical leave-one-out both show a large source/lineage component. Therefore do NOT interpret cross-dataset sign consistency as independent adaptive convergence.
- DoPI European butterflies/hawkmoths are not a tropical bird syndrome validation. Typical bird, butterfly and hawkmoth profiles are nonexclusive in colour/shape; the original Direct ledger cannot separate red from pink, yellow from orange, UV spectra, anthesis or true pollinator pollen performance.
- DoPI confirmed effective pollination grade1 source evidence is extremely sparse for butterflies and Sphingidae; original DoPI×GIFT study source supports zero grade1 multiaxis guild-pair contrasts at the required >=10 studies. Even European visitation results are not verified pollination efficiency.
- The GIFT profile counts differ across the three source-relative contrasts. The European DoPI study counts are also profile-specific. DO NOT average the three effects or subtract their percentage-points, call 3 directions three independent evolutionary shifts, or fit an effect-size meta-analysis from them.
