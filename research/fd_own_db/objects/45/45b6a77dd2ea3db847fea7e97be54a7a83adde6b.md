# Next source-qualified FD analysis: genus-disjoint functional repertoire overlap

**Frozen analysis proposal — 2026-11-18; original-data result PENDING.** This is a falsification of the *lineage/repertoire* interpretation, not a search for a significant pollinator effect.

## Main question

Recorded colour × floral-form categories recur between islands with **no common accepted species**. Does any overlap remain between original floras with **no common accepted botanical genus**, after comparable geography, Direct evidence support, genus source availability and island separation? A positive absolute functional overlap alone is not a claim of convergent evolution, and an observed excess/deficit against a null depends on the null's conditioning.

## Immutable denominators, paired comparison tiers

- **Parent population:** all 4,505 original botanical GIFT island floras. Do not require 3/10/17 trait complete cases.
- **Primary measured endpoint:** only true SAME-accepted-species high/medium species-Direct colour × form; >=5 directly described species per island. The source-verified eligible cohort is 2,324 floras; its 2,699,326 undirected pairs are a finite pair-population, **not** 2.7 million independent evolutionary replicates.
- **Tier S0:** all eligible island pairs.
- **Tier S1:** **zero accepted species shared across the entire original floristic roster**, not just observed Direct carriers. Original S1 denominator **842,058** and mean TV overlap **0.247183275** are required reconciliation controls.
- **Tier G0 (primary):** **zero accepted botanical genus shared across the entire original floristic roster**. Also calculate the looser genus-disjoint requirement using *only directly phenotyped species* as a separate sensitivity (never substitute for the primary).
- **Source-status:** all-recorded; separately strict original GIFT native-known accepted species. Unknown native status is not nonnative or functional absence.
- **Regions:** global counts and within-region estimates on independently assigned northern-midlatitude, tropical, southern-extratropical, and northern highlatitude regimes; report unassignable geography as missing.

A genus token is a taxonomic identity based on a source-backed accepted species/binomial and accepted botanical genus, never a family proxy, spell prefix from unresolved species, or an inferred phylogenetic tip. Reconcile CoL accepted genus/mapped identity when available; quarantine ambiguously resolved taxa and rerun as sensitivity.

## Responses and noninterchangeable metrics

For one pair of island category compositions p_i, p_j, **TV overlap** O=Σ_c min(p_ic,p_jc). Also report Hellinger affinity H=Σ_c sqrt(p_ic p_jc), exact category-set turnover/nestedness, Direct species supports, original entire-flora species and genus intersections, geographic distance (km), GIFT first-source entity, Direct fraction and evidence quality.

Do not compare differences in mean O between S0/S1/G0 as if they were treatment effects: the sets have different geography and floristic richness. No mixed marginal product may manufacture an unobserved true flower pair. Use the same accepted species-state map and unchanged coverage masks throughout.

## Outcome-blind geographic matching and partial identification

1. For each focal island before inspecting any floral states, draw candidate G0 donors from the same broad region in a nonidentical independent geographic 10° block, and S1 but **genus-overlap** donors as candidate controls. Match on inter-island great-circle distance, both islands' Direct species n, full-flora genus richness and Direct fraction; use fixed support calipers set from eligible covariate distributions. Source/archipelago exclusion and geographic distance differ and must be independently reported. No matches => NOT_IDENTIFIABLE.
2. Define primary matched outcome ΔO = mean_focal[ O(G0 donor) − O(S1, genus-overlap donor) ] with one within-focal aggregate, not independent pairs. Report support counts: focal islands, independent physical blocks, first-GIFT source regions and archipelagos, before computing inference.
3. Source/genus conditional benchmark: freeze the same original island rosters, genus intersections, matched pair graph and Direct support, and reassign **whole observed same-species colour×form profiles** globally or within accepted botanical genus under a deterministic per-replicate seed. At least 199 full trait-label permutations, permitting 999 where support; if no mutable within-genus profiles, flag structural null uninformative rather than claiming adaptation absent.
4. Two uncertainty units independently: resample entire original 10° geographic blocks, then entire first GIFT candidate-source clusters. Repeated pair edges are never treated as independent observations; leave-one-archipelago out as sensitivity. Use pre-existing meaningful support gates >=30 distinct focal islands, >=10 geographic blocks, >=10 source clusters for a regional claim, or explicitly mark unsupported.
5. Restrict to 2–3 pre-specified endpoints: joint colour×form primary, independently scored symmetry × depth secondary, and category genus-turnover negative-control; correct a pre-specified family when reporting exploratory intervals. With 0/low independent support, report the denominator instead of chasing a threshold.

## Predictions and discriminating outcomes

- G0 observed O>0: recurrent coarse trait categories even without shared genera, **descriptive cross-lineage representation**.
- G0 O exceeds geography/trait-mask and genus-profile conditional prediction: candidate *beyond source/genus availability* functional similarity, NOT an independently verified selected syndrome.
- G0 O is at or below conditional reference: compositional resemblance can be explained (or overpredicted) by the available documented trait pool; no universal convergence.
- G0 count or matched blocks/source clusters insufficient: **identifiability limit**, not an ecological zero.

**Irreducible mechanism gap:** no original effective island-local visitors, ancestry/within-species phenotype shifts, observed arrivals or seed set are measured. Independently verified mainland sister taxon/local visitation data are a separate future gate.
