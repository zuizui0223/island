# Frozen extension: potential partner composition × plant functional traits (2026-11-25)

Status: design and source-eligibility gate only, written before reading new Bombus / fruit / bat effect estimates. Existing PR #66 nectar-bird primary contract remains unchanged. These tests use plant and animal occurrence, not interaction observations.

## One primary outcome per animal group

| ID | Geography | Partner predictor | Plant endpoint (species-Direct high/medium) | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FRUIT_BIRD_CONTROL | tropics | log1p fruit diet >0 species, control log1p zero-fruit species | fleshy fruit vs non-fleshy fruit, measured direct fruit morphology (not inferred from dispersal mode or animal presence) | positive E2 |
| BOMB_NORTH | original northern_midlatitude | recorded native Bombus present (1) vs checklist-qualified nonrecord (0), control log1p other native bee species count | TRUE same accepted species zygomorphic × deep tube, among species with both known states | positive E2 |
| NECTAR_BIRD | existing PR66 tropics | log1p ≥50% nectar-associated minus partial coefficient, zero diet control | tubular form | positive E2 (unchanged PR66) |
| NECTAR_BAT | tropics | log1p ≥50% nectar-associated minus partial coefficient, zero diet control | TRUE same-species large/very_large × (open_radial, bell_campanulate or brush_puff) | positive E2 |

The fruit control is run first when source rights and direct fruit data allow. Positive fruit control does not establish equal power, absence of confounding or causal matching for floral endpoints; negative control cannot distinguish biological absence of a relationship from low power. Growth form is an exploratory cross-trait environmental-confounding diagnostic, not a proven causally unrelated negative control. Do not switch to dispersal syndrome if direct fruit morphology is missing. Do not select an alternate fruit/flower endpoint after viewing outcomes.

Bombus: use Marshall et al. original island checklist with corrected accepted name, NonNative=0 and Uncertain=0, exact genus token Bombus, source-native certainty, source checklist reconciliation to total native richness (before removing uncertain rows); positive = at least one certain native recorded Bombus. For primary, require source coverage Cov in [0,1], ≥0.90, model native richness reconciled with all native named records, at least one certain native bee, no uncertain native Bombus entry (even on positive islands), source unique geometry and ≥90% mutual polygon intersection; this deliberately conservative source-qualified zero means no certain native Bombus record, NOT confirmed biological absence. Do not infer absence for unmatched/uncovered islands. Archive support counts regardless of result. No outcome-dependent threshold relaxation. Compare higher/lower sample support and all native records as *descriptive* separately if explicitly planned before outcomes. Geographic group is original bee source Archipelago (blank/ambiguous excluded), not island name. Positive count/other bee richness and source list are independent of plant data. For genus-defined Bombus labels, animal-side within-genus label permutation has no scientific meaning; do not promote a degenerate p. Use plant-side null + group bootstrap; group/realm consistency is a distinct sensitivity, not independent phylogenetic replication. A subgenus analysis requires a separate vetted Bombus phylogeny and fixed labels before effects are examined.

Bird/bat range incidence: shared geometric intersection functions in PR66; require per-source release, exact attribute definitions and redistribution rights. IUCN standard attributes are PRESENCE/ORIGIN/SEASONAL, current name sci_name, but completeness and spatial resolution vary by assessment and taxon. No assumption that every species has a range map. Use source-verified extant/probably extant native/reintroduced resident/breeding polygons and 50% island overlap, islands ≥1 km², as in PR66 only after codebook audit. Range zeros are map noncoverage, not verified absence. Treat unknown seasonality as unavailable for primary. A nectar bat is a diet class from exact EltonTraits mammal table; need validated name/family/genus crosswalk, 5% unmatched ceiling and clades independently defined before effects. No maps or derived incidence redistributed.

## Common estimator and gates
E1 = coefficient contrast on island trait share; E2 = identical contrast on observed share minus expectation under fixed distinct regional species plant genus × evidence-grade block means. Pool defined before animal model, on region flora roster with known trait, not weighted by repeated island occurrence; species conflicts or duplicates fail closed. Use group-demeaning with equal group weights. Common covariates log(area), log2(1+mainland distance), log2(scored n), abs(latitude); partner counts enter as above. Require ≥200 eligible islands, ≥30 informative independent groups (nonconstant focal predictor), and ≥4 residual degrees of freedom, full rank, finite design. Failed gates produce support-only output; no estimate or p. Primary bootstrap 1,999 equal-group resamples and ≥90% valid fits, plant-side 1,999 coherent distinct-species state permutations within genus × grade with fixed roster, plus (diet mode) 1,999 coherent diet label permutations within *present* animal species genus, and independent joint permutations. Need movable plant blocks, movable animal blocks for diet and ≥90% rank-valid fits. One-sided p with +1 correction. For new diet tests, intersection-union primary p=max(plant-only,animal-genus,joint); require positive E2 lower group percentile bound plus adjusted p. For Bombus plant-only p; not equivalent strength to both-side diet test. Original PR66 primary decision remains as frozen; when compared across pollinators include its genus-null p only if original E2 interval positive, otherwise p=1 (conservative additional gate).

The pollinator family is fixed as NECTAR_BIRD, BOMB_NORTH, NECTAR_BAT (Holm across all three, unavailable/unsupported p=1 in family accounting, never a biological null). Fruit control is separate calibration. Do not add moth/reptile to this family post hoc; new prospective family requires independent source and precommitted endpoint. Self-compatibility (SC) and autonomous selfing separately are secondary, directional loss/assurance predictions; no confirmed absence or Baker's law mechanism from checklist zeros. Keep them out of primary family and freeze the secondary testing family separately before running. All source and support failures retained.

No effect fitting until source eligibility is certified. Current archived flora ontology is flower/mating only. Check GIFT 3.2 trait metadata and protected Direct ledger for genuine fruit morphology and growth form; do not infer fruit type from floral form or genus, do not assign missing trait 0. Source metadata counts are not matched island support. GIFT primary references and access restrictions must be checked. No raw protected plant identifiers leave authorized island runs.

## Shared clade replication adapter
For diet-defined groups (including fruit), clades must be fixed before any effects, with one clade label per animal genus. The generic estimator restricts the focal dietary count to each predeclared clade, puts remaining focal-diet animals in a separate control, and retains the same plant block pool, support, rank and null gates. Require actual within-clade animal genus label variation before interpreting diet specificity. Fruit labels are binary positive vs zero before permutation. Holm across the full declared clade list, including unavailable slots; overlapping clade/archipelago estimates are not independent replications. Bombus subgenera not run until a taxonomically validated grouping and separate contract exist.
