## 2. Biological question and rival explanations

Do native resident nectar-associated phyllostomids and hummingbirds partition floral trait space across Caribbean island assemblages beyond genus/evidence composition, or does the bat–tubular signal reflect shared geography or broad access traits? Three possibilities are retained:
- H1 differential matching: relatively greater bird association with red/narrow tubular traits, relatively greater bat association with white/wide-aperture traits.
- H2 shared access/generalization: both associated with enclosed or nectar-access morphologies, yet may differ in aperture, anthesis, rewards or actual pollen placement. A shared floral category is not proof that both guilds pollinate the same species.
- H3 lineage/environment/coverage explanation: marginal associations attenuate after conditioning on the other guild, shared controls, or plant genus × evidence grade. Attenuation does not uniquely identify confounding or mediation.

Antillean Gesnerieae directly motivate the contrast: Martén-Rodríguez et al. (2009), *J Ecol*, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2745.2008.01465.x (tubular red/yellow flowers primarily hummingbird, campanulate pale flowers primarily bat, subcampanulate mixed visitors); Martén-Rodríguez et al. (2010), *New Phytol*, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-8137.2010.03330.x (specialization, generalization and reproductive assurance transitions). Muchhala (2007), *Am Nat*, https://doi.org/10.1086/512047 experimentally demonstrates opposite effects of aperture width on bat and hummingbird pollen delivery in an Ecuadorian system; Muchhala (2006), https://doi.org/10.3732/ajb.93.8.1081 supports the same distinction in Burmeistera. These provide motivation, NOT a Caribbean-wide result. White/red are coarse proxies; tubular membership does not measure aperture width or tube depth.

## 4. Estimands and fixed analysis order

A. Coverage-only first: count joint eligible islands, both guild count ranges, both guild within-group variances, numbers of groups/islands with joint variation, residualized bird/bat correlation, singular values/condition number, variance inflation factors, common trait coverage, leave-group rank, number of distinct movable species in both endpoints. Unknown/no overlap remains separate. If one guild has no independent within-group variation, or joint fit is rank-deficient, do not substitute separate-cohort regressions. Stop at a descriptive support result.

B. Primary coarse specificity contrast on the common roster: use species-level direct color indicator red (R) and white (W); r_it = observed island trait share - genus×grade expected share, using same common roster. Within independently assigned group, group-equal OLS on r_R and r_W with both animal predictors and the identical fixed design; log1p bird and bat counts separately standardized by common-cohort pooled within-group SD (freeze scaling, no fold-specific scale). Include log area and log1p mainland distance as core covariates; pre-specified expanded sensitivity includes original climate PC1–4, Direct coverage/log scored richness, latitude and other bat/bird counts if identifiable. No outcome-guided control dropping; if core model cannot be fitted, mark unidentifiable; expanded model failure stays recorded. Full and reduced models use same islands/controls/scales. Coefficients have proportion change per within-group SD units.

D_R = beta_bird,R - beta_bat,R; D_W = beta_bat,W - beta_bird,W.
S = D_R + D_W.

Expected D_R>0 and D_W>0, with beta_bird,R>0 and beta_bat,W>0. S>0 alone is not a double dissociation. Joint resampling uses the same group draws across all outcomes and all coefficients. Neither cross-coefficient is assumed zero. If discussing negligible cross-association, define an explicitly provisional equivalence margin of ±0.02 share per within-group SD, report interval relative to it, and sensitivity ±0.01/0.05; failure to reject a cross effect is not equivalence. With tiny K do not claim equivalence.

C. Discovery-focused tubular diagnostic, secondary and not confirmatory: on the SAME joint roster/cohort, fit bat-only plus fixed controls, bird-only plus fixed controls, and both guilds plus fixed controls. Report raw, genus-grade expected and residual slope for both, plus paired attenuation beta_bat,reduced - beta_bat,joint; calculate joint VIF and covariance. To bridge to original #348, first reproduce the original focal-minus-other phyllostomid contrast on the original data, then on the regional/common-cohort restriction, then on the joint model with other-family/nonfamily controls where estimable. Keep each stage separate; scale differences must be explicit. No percentage attenuation when reduced coefficient is near zero. Compare tubular and true deep tube separately; a closed tube and deep nectar access are distinct. Bell/campanulate vs tubular same-species form contrast, red×tubular vs white×bell joint memberships only if exact state schema and true paired observations support them; all secondary, preserve all endpoints even if no support.

D. Optional external morphology/interaction validation: select independent published Caribbean species observations using geography and taxonomic scope, not coefficient direction; blind outcome extraction to island bird/bat richness. Measure aperture width/flower length, anthesis and nectar timing where available; classify effective pollination by pollen deposition/fruit-set exclusion evidence, not visit alone, and allow mixed guilds. Antillean Gesnerieae published study is already known and thus biological calibration, not blind confirmatory holdout. A separate unexamined family/region/source is required for independent validation. Report focal-family removal (Gesneriaceae and other influential families using outcome-blind abundance rankings), plant species-/genus-equal weights, and geographic block vs archipelago sensitivities without counting as replication.

## 5. Null, uncertainty and claim gates

Use coherent permutations of each unique accepted plant species' complete observed color×form state vector within genus × combined evidence grades (preserve pair covariance and synchronized occurrences across all islands); for depth or other endpoints use the corresponding predeclared joint carrier roster and frozen matched stratum. Recompute observed/residual and all contrast estimates per draw; keep animal geography, covariates, species incidence and evidence fixed; 9,999 draws seed 20261125, report two-sided conditional label-null p and directional descriptive statistics. Species permutations test a botanical conditional association, not independent geographic replicates, randomized animals or phylogenetic contrasts. Holm across primary D_R,D_W and declared secondary family separately; no resetting prior 21-panel correction. The actual joint estimates must retain raw and expected components and null-centering checks.

Group-equal leave-one-group and paired group bootstrap as diagnostics only; report all leave-group results, valid draws, group-specific partial associations and cohort counts. Global gate remains >=30 informative groups / >=200 informative islands, at least 30 movable scored species per primary contrast, full-rank fits, >=90% valid resamples and all leave-group fits. With the anticipated four blocks, even exact group sign-flip (if exchangeable and appropriate) has only 16 assignments; minimum one-sided p=1/16, so do NOT use species permutations or island bootstraps to manufacture geographic significance. Real archipelago count may differ and must be measured, not assumed. Regional diagnostics: core joint fit full rank, positive residual degrees of freedom (at least 4), both within-group predictor SD >0, VIF<=5 and standardized design condition number<=30 as provisional interpretability thresholds; <= thresholds not proof of causal identification; all leave-group ranks and signs reported, no confirmatory p-value claim. If the regional thresholds fail, output descriptive association/unidentifiable, not a relaxed model chosen by signs. Cluster intervals with few groups are descriptive and conditional on resample validity; no formal double-dissociation confirmation without independent sampling or adequate geographic replication.

| Outcome pattern | Allowed reading |
| --- | --- |
| Both diagonal directions and D_R,D_W positive, stable under controls/leave-group/quality | regional differential matching candidate, not established pollination or selection |
| Tubular bat slope attenuates, bird positive, joint fit identifiable | consistent with shared bird–bat distribution explanation, not proof of confounding |
| Both guild tubular slopes positive | shared coarse floral architecture candidate, not verified dual pollination |
| Bat tubular remains positive, bird contribution distinguishable | conditional bat-associated morphology candidate; aperture/interaction data needed |
| Cross-effects or red/white predictions fail | no classic double dissociation; retain data and alternatives |
| Joint variation, coverage, fit or independent group gate fails | not identifiable / insufficient support; no biological absence conclusion |

Functional diversification is a distinct secondary question. For true paired trait vectors, estimate observed joint-state Rao Q / effective joint-category richness relative to fixed marginals and genus×grade joint null; additive marginal Gower Rao Q cannot identify pair association. Co-occurrence of both guilds or positive S does not show greater diversity or complementarity. A separate frozen interaction contrast and enough common geographic support would be required. No causal evolutionary, competition, pollinator replacement or island-wide effective-pollination claims from the present design.
