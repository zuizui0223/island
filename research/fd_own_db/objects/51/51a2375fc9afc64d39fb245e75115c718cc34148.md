# GIFT 4,505 botanical floras — strict spatial and first-source TRAINING-exclusion contract (19 November 2026)

**Exploratory / original-data validation required.** This test is a direct next step from the source-verified global [13-endpoint interregional genus transfer report](../results/20261119_global_region_genus_transfer/README.md), and explicitly tries to falsify prediction that derives from floristically related nearby ISLAND community rosters rather than transferable genus traits. It does not establish independent mainland ancestral phenotype observations or pollinator-mediated selection.

## The subtle leakage problem in previous valid observational work

Previous source-original other-region/local-region taxon-name-exclusive genus models removed the focal island's *entire accepted species roster*, ensured region-exclusive donor species names and balanced donor number *within the same botanical genus*. Those are necessary controls, but because the global literature phenotype ledger is assigned to accepted plant species rather than independently measured within-island flower populations, **different species from other islands in the SAME 10° physical geographic block, or same first original GIFT candidate mainland source region, could still appear among the region-exclusive genus donors**. Previous group-equal block and first-source bootstrap intervals applied only to the final prediction scores; they DID NOT remove the same-group botanical source plants from model training.

The strict training-disjoint sensitivity therefore excludes all original accepted plant species names documented in **ANY** original GIFT island flora that shares the focal original physical block and/or the focal original first geo_k5 candidate mainland source entity, even if those accepted species are also documented on more distant islands. This is conservative, and may cause severe donor attrition. Distinguish absent source support from negative plant ecology.

## Fixed source analysis, all 4,505 floral community parent retained

- **Original 4,505 GIFT accepted botanical island floras**, every full species name including flower-trait-uncoded plants, with original exact SHA-pinned 2026 specimen/canonical accepted species roster and independent 8264-island GSHHG geographic source. Both all-recorded and known-native are **nested** study scopes, not separate ecological history experiments.
- **13** genuine species-Direct endpoints: ten original species high/medium single traits plus three directly recorded SAME accepted species two-trait pairs. No 928 all-trait-complete or 17D Gaussian KDE selection, no invented combinations or synonym conversions.
- Exclusion conditions: (1) physical ten-degree **block_out**, (2) first candidate GIFT source entity **first_source_out**, and (3) **block_and_first_source_out**. Missing block/source group cannot be silently replaced with the unblocked model. Do not use original genus or trait categories to select held-out source groups.
- All source donor training profiles **and** external genus-blind category prior are re-estimated after source deletion. Local and external donor names must remain strictly exclusive of each OTHER broad geographic region, matched SAME genus, same outcome-blind donor species n per genus; no species listed in focal flora or held-out other GIFT island floras may remain in either training arm.
- In the BEFORE-versus-AFTER comparison, score the **same focal accepted species in the same focal island** under both models, restricted to genera for which BOTH donor arms have eligible original true-Direct species after strict source removal. This is an exact common-support comparison, not a change in which floral traits were sampled. Preserve both positive and negative predictive gains.
- Report all 4 broad original regions × both statuses × all 13 endpoints × all 3 training-exclusion modes (**312 potential panels**) and a full population/source support waterfall. Require >=5 same original Direct focal carriers in matched donor genera, >=30 original focal botanical islands, >=10 physical blocks and >=10 distinct original first GIFT source groups for both uncertainty views.
- Use original proper **per-species multiclass Brier and log loss**, for both externally versus region-locally exclusive genus predictors and an independently refitted external genus-blind source reference, with 499 group-resampling exploratory 95% intervals and delete-one-original-group sensitivity. Both original source geographic groupings are diagnostics, not phylogenetic independence or preregistered family-wise adjusted inference.
- When asking whether the previously positive NORTH-vs-TROPICAL region contrast survives, require both original proper scores and BOTH geographic cluster modalities to retain the SAME sign under the strict source removal. If support drops below eligibility, the correct statement is **NOT IDENTIFIABLE after conservative training exclusion**, never proof of no biological trait transfer.

## Expected scientific interpretation ceilings

A sign-stable after-strict-blocking genus trait prediction pattern would indicate **geographic transfer of botanical GENUS-CONDITIONAL source-floral information beyond same original 10° block and/or candidate mainland source group rosters**. It would NOT identify colonization probabilities (observed focal genus composition is KNOWN), independent mainland ancestor traits (the donors are species in OTHER GIFT island floras), independent evolutionary replications (the 13 endpoints and often geography overlap) or ecological pollination mechanism (source-original WOL/GloBI/EuPPollNet effective pollen or true nondetection remain insufficient).

If predictions reverse or lose support, preserve the earlier correct observational **8/13** original all-three-region genus-over-blind transfer and five north-vs-tropical SOURCE-local/external reversals as the *less conservative taxonomic source association*, but revise any suggestion that the patterns generalize to spatially held-out or fully source-entity held-out island communities.
