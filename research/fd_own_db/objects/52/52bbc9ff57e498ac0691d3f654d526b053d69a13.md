## Sampling/evidence and causal limitations

1. Source DoPI is primarily **European**. No global island-local visitor survey or true guild absence exists in the linked data, and no tropical nectarivorous bird-vs-butterfly/hawkmoth mechanism has been identified. Do not equate GBIF "adequately nondetected" to extinction/absence.
2. Unreviewed source original interactions include 595,076 grade-3 reports (visitation), 57,142 grade-2 pollen carriage, **7,513** grade-1 directly confirmed pollination, **125** grade-4 nonpollinating/robbery, 14,867 NA and 2,177 nonstandard/unresolved codes, which are excluded from graded matching. These are original RECORDS, not replicated pollination mechanisms.
3. Same-publication controls do not enforce same field sites, sampling periods, local flower availability, or identical observational protocols. An observed positive plant-guild link does **not** convert the guild's absence from another flower into a documented true nonvisit.
4. Original GIFT flowering trait descriptions are species-wide, not matched island vs mainland population measurements. Independent morphology source publications may overlap DoPI source studies or GloBI indexed bibliographies, and genus-conditional subsets are nonrandom. Publication-level cluster CIs are exploratory and **not multiplicity-adjusted** for 540 correlated profile contrasts.
5. The strongest pattern is the classic mechanical access signal: Bombus positive visitor plants are relatively more zygomorphic, Syrphidae and butterflies relatively more open/radial. However much of that signal accompanies plant lineage composition and/or observational effort, and it cannot independently establish floral convergent evolution, pollinator regime shifts, nectar reward, realized pollen deposition, or reproductive fitness.
