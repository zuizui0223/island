# Architecture-dependent floral reassembly: a working biological hypothesis

25 November 2026 JST. Re-expression of existing evidence only, no new models.

## The surprising observation
A deep tube is not a single floral syndrome. The tropical candidate-source contrasts depend on what other architecture accompanies that trait. The original four-region radial/deep comparison and three tropical deep combinations are retained in Figure 2. Exact archived results:

| Pair | n | Island−source joint frequency (pp) | Covariance difference (pp) | Between genera | Within genera |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Radial × deep | 666 | +10.98858 | +4.01790 | +4.05652 | −0.03862 |
| Bilateral × deep | 666 | −1.368 | −3.4253 | −3.6390 | +0.2137 |
| Tubular/bell × deep | 598 | +5.172 | −1.0973 | −1.6341 | +0.5367 |

Covariance intervals: radial [+3.1737,+4.9234]; bilateral [−4.3149,−2.5685]; tubular/bell [−1.9404,−0.2753]. Exploratory conditional geographic-block intervals, not simultaneous hypothesis tests. The non-focal joint-frequency values are point estimates rounded as in the original source report. Radial and bilateral are symmetry states; tubular/bell is a form category and may overlap both. Do not sum all three, infer the conditional frequency P(radial | deep), or claim net depth prevalence without the full joint table. No same-variable-genus support for tropical radial/deep; W is not a local evolutionary change.

For the focal radial/deep cohort, ΔJ = Δ(p_A p_D) + ΔC, with 10.98858 = 6.97068 + 4.01790 pp. The latter = 4.05652 − 0.03862 pp. All are descriptive, signed components, not proportions of a causal pathway. The between-genus term depends on weights AND genus-level marginal trait frequencies. It does not isolate colonization or turnover.

## Connection to the independent animal results
The same 30-island protected common cohort gave plant-genus×grade residual specialist-minus-partial coefficients for source-relative depth +0.195580/+0.187680, overall tubular form −0.093692/−0.074534, and joint form/depth −0.035551/−0.030217. These endpoints are not the global symmetry/depth outcome. The exploratory residual contrast makes it worthwhile to separate flower access dimensions and animal diet classes, but no 160-test Holm pass and no stable native result mean it cannot explain the observed geographical floral pairing. The 11/11 nested discovery tubular contrasts and 6/24 bird-family BH passes remain reported alongside their failures under more stringent nulls. Bird diet is not a measured pollination syndrome. Butterfly documented host breadth and HWI are separate exploratory data, not a demonstrated pathway.

## Three contrasting prospective predictions (not tested here)

1. Uniform loss model: independently measured resource-access restrictions and visitor approach restrictions are both depleted as the available pollinator functions decline. Do not substitute the broad radial/deep category for a validated restriction measurement.
2. Architecture-dependent assembly model: even with no change in species-local phenotypes, the full symmetry-by-depth category distribution of a flora differs from candidate pools; the marginal product and covariance components can respond independently and the latter can arise from the taxonomic mixture. Test complete contingency tables with predeclared denominator, all states, and matched geography/source support.
3. Interaction-specific extension: independently measured local partner availability, approach behavior and reachable nectar depth explain prespecified conditional floral contrasts beyond both assemblages' taxonomic expectations, then independently predict visitation/pollen transport. If those extra predictions fail, the descriptive floral assembly result remains but the proposed interaction mechanism is unsupported.

A test of P(radial|deep) would require jointly scored depth and symmetry margins, not just the three ΔJ values. A test of direction of specialization would require functional measurements, not categorical stereotypes. Retain the current observational result as the main contribution; do not launch another post-hoc search for significant subsets.
