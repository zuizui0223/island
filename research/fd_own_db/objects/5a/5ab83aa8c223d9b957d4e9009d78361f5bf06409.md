### Selected original native status and biological mechanism limitations

**Strict native-status warning:** The original GIFT native_nonendemic classification itself was partly corroborated by **an earlier WCVP native TDWG-L3 status** on a selected original status-covered island subset. In the 2026 official WCVP v16 recoding here, the original strictly native/nonendemic GIFT source-listed focal species have virtually **100%** positive WCVP native L3 evidence (whereas source-only GIFT candidate species have ~95%). Thus an apparently fully complete WCVP match on STRICT island focal taxa is NOT independent biological validation of their nativeness; this is PROVIDER/STATUS-SELECTION nonindependence and must be explicitly disclosed.

WCVP TDWG-L3 region counts are broad **documented native region extent**, NOT independently measured species native geographic AREA in km², nearest edge-to-island distance, realised propagule dispersal probability, seed size, dispersal vector, ocean-current access, island colonisation chronology, nor observed plant effective pollinator visits. A named GIFT candidate source region can contain plant records very far from the island's population edge; native WGSRPD L3 alone does not locate a species occurrence at fine scale.

The botanical source genus-sharing deficit surviving WCVP-native-regional-range-bin sampling is **a source-specific observational original floristic nonexchangeability result**. This supports island plant taxonomic assemblage bias beyond mere species count, original GIFT candidate source region count, source-to-island region km, and one independent broad native-range extent proxy **for the species that survive the triple-stratum gate**. It is not direct causal evidence that genus-specific plant propagules reached an island and failed establishment, that natural selection selected against non-native source taxa, that endemic island flower morphologies evolved, or that effective pollinator guilds shifted.

**Next identifying evidence:** independent native species occurrence geometries plus actual closest native distribution-edge distance to islands, plant dispersal syndromes/seed size and propagule pressure, island flora census/detection controls, independently sampled native and endemic population flower phenotype states, and direct plant-pollinator effectiveness/pollen-limitation/seed set observations. None is supplied by WCVP region-count metadata alone. Do not represent this source-native-range test as fully identifying island colonisation processes.
