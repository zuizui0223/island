## Why this is a DIRECT physiological-floristic test, not a new predictive model

Earlier [global original 4,505 native/endemic/introduced first-stage direct comparison](DIRECT_SOURCE_NATIVE_ENDEMIC_INTRODUCED_FLORISTIC_FILTERS.md) found distinct, signed percentage-point botanical category frequencies among strictly native nonendemic plants on islands versus plausible geographically nearest native GIFT mainland candidate species NOT recorded in each island's full flora. Potential mainland source list is a possible plant taxonomic **opportunity**; its presence is not arrival, and its nonrecording on the island is not actual propagule/establishment failure.

Because a range-widespread plant can appear in many GIFT checklists, original DIRECT plant trait profile differences may be associated with source-breadth availability and floristic literature effort. Thus one more **observational documentation balance** is applied without conditioning on genus (genus turnover is the first-stage target mechanism):

- Only original **strict native AND nonendemic** Direct accepted species that are ALSO present in one of their island's original five geographically nearest **native GIFT mainland source lists** are compared to distinct same-trait Direct mainland GIFT candidate plants **absent from the island's ENTIRE recorded botanical flora** (not just high/medium flower-state taxa).
- Original GIFT number of DISTINCT native mainland source entities containing the name is calculated over its 748 original native continental GIFT region records. This is not actual biological distribution area or proven dispersal probability.
- For both actually observed native and unrecorded-candidate donor groups classify accepted plant names into **five previously fixed source-list breadth bins:** exactly 1, 2–3, 4–10, 11–30 and ≥31 mainland source entities. When a bin contains both island-side and candidate-source-side independent Direct named species, the difference in observed actual binary category **frequency** for that bin is calculated. Its within-island aggregation weights each bin by `min(n_native_in_bin,n_candidate_in_bin)`. Require ≥5 source-list breadth effectively matched species and original >=5 directly measured source-listed strict native names and >=10 candidate-source names before qualified island effect.
- For the **SAME eligible physical island** show BOTH unadjusted strict source-listed-native minus candidate source composition difference and breadth-matched difference. 999 original equal-10° physical block conditional resamples and separate first-GIFT-mainland-source-group resamples are computed. No p-value is inferred from repeated island/plant names, and 95% windows are NOT multiplicity-corrected across 7 traits×4 geographical regimes or other source-status panels.

All 4,505 original botanical island floras remain the source frame: 2,205 northern-midlatitude, 1,558 tropical, 317 southern-extratropical, 424 northern-high-latitude and one independently geography-unresolved. The original legacy 928 three-trait complete/n30 island sample is not used. We test the seven genuinely species-direct morphological/reproductive sectors individually, including original true SAME-accepted-species tubular/bell+zygomorphic combination, not hypothetical flower profile combinations.

### Was breadth matching itself possible, or did it silently drop almost all donor species?

The source-original match had genuine overlap in multiple predefined GIFT source-listing breadth bins in most source-comparable tropical floras: zygomorphic **96/98**, tubular/bell **85/88**, autonomous **60/72** and real bilateral×tube/bell **81/83** original eligible islands each had at least two bins with BOTH directly labelled native/nonendemic and GIFT candidate-only plants. Mean number of potentially exchangeable category carriers under `Σ_b min(n_native_b,n_candidate_b)` was respectively **111.4**, **55.5**, **21.3**, and **35.5** species per eligible island, not just the nominal floor five. The average fraction of the actual source-listed native direct carriers in at least one GIFT source-breadth-overlap bin was **100.0%, 100.0%, 99.6%, 99.8%**, respectively; candidate GIFT source trait-carrier bin coverage was **94.7%, 94.6%, 89.5%, 94.5%**, respectively. This supports that the numeric breadth-matched contrast is not driven solely by one selected source bin or exclusion of almost the entire native sample; it **does not** establish matched actual seed arrival, botanical range size, independent source phylogeny or native provenance completeness.

## Scientific conclusion

**The most direct current source-positive observation** is not predictive accuracy: after an explicit original mainland source-list breadth balance, tropical documented native/nonendemic island plants differ from their geographically plausible candidate mainland source plant assemblages in a **nonuniform combination** of real architecture and autonomous capacity states. It is a measurable regional floral **assemblage sorting pattern**, not a scalar functional hypervolume contraction or proven specialisation loss. Separately, introduced floristic composition differs again, especially tropical floral architecture. The complementary endemic same-island genus comparison remains **underpowered**: tropical zygomorphic differences found in eight island×genus units represent only **five DISTINCT variable genera**; a within-genus estimated zero would be non-identifiable. This is why "sorting versus evolution" remains a properly bounded **hypothesis** rather than a fully resolved causal contrast.

The next most useful data are explicit native/introduced/endemism source verification, original well-documented mainland and island congeneric flower morphology, location-specific population phenotype and establishment/visitor effectiveness records, ideally from multiple phylogenetically independent genera or archipelagos. Current original names and source taxa come from the same literature-conditioned Direct trait compilation, so a source balance reduces but cannot eliminate sampling bias.
