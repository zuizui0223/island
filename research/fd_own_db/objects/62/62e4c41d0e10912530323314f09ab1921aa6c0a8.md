## Three different nulls represent three different questions

The true structural category reference comprises **actually observed** original `floral_form × floral_symmetry` phenotypes. The predeclared specialist sector is the exact two original configurations `(tubular, zygomorphic)` and `(bell_campanulate, zygomorphic)`; the open-architecture comparator is the actually observed `(open_radial,actinomorphic)` category. These categories are morphology, NOT observed effective Bombus, Lepidoptera or bird visitors.

For each source island with >=5 directly documented same-plant form×symmetry carriers, let `k_i` be its actually distinct observed complete category profiles; `n_i` is the Direct carrier species count; `R` is the number of region-available specialist joint profiles; `m_i` is how many specialist joint profiles are documented for the island. Define excess specialized-region absence `(E_null[m_i] − m_i) / R` (positive = island is missing more specialized profile states than null predicts). Separately, the probability that **NO** specialist category is documented is compared to an analytically expected zero-sector probability.

**Null 1: uniform fixed-k** draws k distinct category profiles from the region's K actually documented reference categories. Exact expected specialist coverage = kR/K. It preserves observed **number of different profiles k** but NOT category prevalence, individual Direct carrier count, taxonomic genera or geographical source opportunity. This is a weak negative-control baseline, not ecological inference.

**Null 2: original frequency/carrier count** samples n accepted source Direct species without replacement from the region's documented unique plant species, preserving region-level real category frequencies; the probability any category is absent is the exact finite-population hypergeometric probability.

**Null 3: region genus-exchange** preserves each original focal island's named-genus number of observed Direct species and analytically exchanges the complete actual two-trait phenotype profiles **without replacement within each genus** from the in-region union of Direct species. This fixes original joint flower phenotype pairing and observed genus carrier totals; expected category coverage and complete-sector absence are calculated exactly, not randomized 199 times. The reference is an **in-sample ORIGINAL observed species/region pool**, NOT an independently measured ancestral mainland donor or a causal selection model.

Each of the three null residuals is regressed separately against original **log2(1+ distance_to_continent_km)** within the same source region, controlling log island area, known trait documentation share, log Direct carrier count and absolute latitude, with one equal vote per physical original 10° source block and 999 geographical block-bootstrap sensitivity resamples. This is cross-sectional floristic documentation, NOT a dynamic local extinction timeseries.

### Positive observation that survives a restricted exploratory check

The OPEN comparator `(open_radial,actinomorphic)` on northern midlatitude **1,593** floras has original genus-relative excess missingness *negative* isolation slope **−0.002794 [−0.005664,−0.000397]** per doubling (raw exploratory block t p≈0.0338). This is consistent with isolated floras showing LESS absence of that widespread open structural profile than one within-region genus null would predict. However it is one of **16 regional/source-scope/sector** analyses, is NOT part of the predeclared six marginal category gradients, and is NOT multi-contrast corrected. It therefore stays a **posthoc source-relative hypothesis**, not a confirmed adaptive opening of floral morphology under pollinator loss.

For tropical open radial×actinomorphic, 697 original island floras have 12.77% no recorded open profile versus genus expected 12.54%; the genus-relative missingness slope is +0.001430 [−0.001144,+0.004390], not a supported difference.

## Consequences for the spatial ecological theory

- The **measured positive result** is *region-specific floral functional assemblage structure* and categorical state-space occupancy among different original botanical species; this works at the full GIFT 4,505 population frame and does not use invalid 17-dimensional KDE volume.
- **A fixed-k-only null is not enough** to infer directional ecological “specialist niche loss.” The actual original 2-trait case demonstrates potentially dramatic attenuation once each species' genus and the region's observed available phenotype states are fixed.
- A recorded specialist state missing from a species-Direct checklist is NOT proven absent on the real island; nor is morphological sector occupancy proof of actual pollen-effective pollinator guilds, a historical bee-to-moth/bird transition, organism adaptation or pollen limitation. Original trait labels are fixed per species worldwide.
- The 1,593/697 eligible subset is the *particular two-Direct-trait measurement opportunity*, NOT a substitute for the 4,505 botanical flora study population. Native-only, regional mainland candidate source, physical-block independences, rare source genus opportunity and category annotation biases still bound causal interpretations.
- The requested *directional near-versus-far island pair nestedness asymmetry* is a separate cross-sectional statistic; no historically oriented phylogenetic or temporal extinction inference has yet been calculated. Even a future positive directional nestedness difference would require controlling k, category prevalence and physical/source block reuse.
