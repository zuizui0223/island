# Novelty audit and manuscript positioning — 24 November 2026

## Decision

The defensible contribution is **geographically contingent joint floral reassembly and explicit two-sided tests of its possible correspondence with potential partner diets**, not the first recognition of island syndromes, trait correlations, trait-dependent island colonization, dietary generalism or pollinator matching. "Combinatorial floral reassembly" is descriptive working terminology, not a new verified syndrome or evidence of a universal mechanism. The prior draft's "Beyond pollinator loss" framing has been replaced by a more precise title, since this study does not establish a causal effect of pollinator loss.

## A testable formulation for subsequent independent work

For a prespecified pair of floral traits A,D and candidate source S, define `J = E[A D]` and `C = J − E[A] E[D]`. The source-relative `ΔJ` differs from `ΔC`. For each roster, `C = Cov_G(E[A|G], E[D|G]) + E_G[Cov(A,D|G)]`. These standard exact identities define the main estimands rather than new methods. An interaction-related prediction is that an independently measured potential-partner functional composition is associated with a specified access-trait outcome beyond geographic/taxonomic expectations and that a separate local observation verifies actual floral use and pollen transfer. The current data do not establish this prediction. Do not repeat post-hoc searches for positive subsets; any new test needs independent groups, provenance and measurement.

## Refinement: architecture-dependent reassembly (25 November 2026 JST)
The earlier draft emphasized a single positive tropical joint category and omitted the southern extratropical regional row. The original source report supports all four radial/deep regional rows and contrasting tropical deep-tube combinations. In particular, bilateral/deep joint frequency has a negative point contrast (−1.368 pp), and its covariance difference (−3.4253 pp) contrasts with radial/deep (+4.0179 pp). Tubular/bell × deep shows positive joint frequency (+5.172 pp) with negative covariance (−1.0973 pp). These distinct estimands substantiate the question of architecture-dependent assembly; they do not establish a directional shift in measured pollination specialization. Figure 2 and Results now retain these contrasts. No new ecological fit.
