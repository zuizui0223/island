## Interpreting these floral architectures in relation to real pollination syndromes

Distinct candidate pollinator mechanisms can generate overlapping architectural motifs:

- **Radial/deep:** bird, butterfly, hawkmoth, long-tongued bee or others with access; requires measured visitor family/behaviour/pollen body placement to separate.
- **Zygomorphic/deep:** mechanical entrance/posture and contact filtering, often compatible with large bees but NOT uniquely bee pollination; some birds, moths and generalists can also access.
- **Coloured tubular/deep:** bird attraction versus **bee avoidance** are competing visual mechanisms; broad GIFT red_pink/yellow_orange cannot test bee visual colour-space/UV, scent, nectar, anthesis or orientation.
- **Open radial/shallow:** potentially generalist access, but neither actual visitor range nor effective pollen service is quantified.

**Existing animal evidence:** [original GBIF source/guild support gate](../20261116_source_guild_identification/README.md) qualifies only 3 of 125 guild×flower×region comparisons, all same tropical bird record channel, and all mixed-block CIs span zero. [GloBI real botanical same-species island visitors](../20261117_GloBI_real_flower_visitors/README.md) have sparse bird/undivided Lepidoptera citations; all deep-profile bird/Lepidoptera positive documentary support disappears after globally largest archive-source omission. [Web of Life and Mangal island network audit](../20261117_independent_network_site_preflight/README.md) confirms a few positive site-local visit links but insufficient direct deep flower×bird/Sphingidae and no effective pollen. GBIF non-detection or GloBI missing records are NOT ecological guild absence.

A potential new independently released plant–flower visitor compilation, [DoPI Version 2.0 DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22876365](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22876365), offers European bee/butterfly/hawkmoth positive records with literature; it does NOT identify tropical bird associations or guild-level true absences and some sources overlap with indexed GloBI. DoPI source metadata/file intake audit is separately running; do not claim it has already been validated and linked to original GIFT species.

**Defensible one-paper framing:** Taxonomic turnover and region-specific reassembly of floral functional profiles across global island floras; multiple pollination-mode hypotheses but NOT inferred effective syndrome shifts. The global parent remains 4,505 islands, categorical Rao Q and functional beta, source-relative real morphology and exact signed genus accounting; the triple/four-axis motif screen is an explanatory sensitivity, not a required triple-complete gate.
