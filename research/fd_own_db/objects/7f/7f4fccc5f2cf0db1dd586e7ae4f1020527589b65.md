# Pre-result contract — original GIFT genus geography → floral repertoire (2026-11-19)

This is an analytical **implementation and prespecification**, not a newly observed positive ecological result. The existing GIFT 4,505 botanical-island parent and each Direct endpoint's actual same-accepted-species scored subset stay unchanged. This addresses the unverified transition in main between (i) 7/13 prediction with the *actual focal genus supplied*, (ii) 0/13 oracle-free improvement over a strong local blind baseline when using generic regional genus weights, and (iii) small geographically predicted genus-composition gains.

## Leakage fix must precede trait-stage claim

The original geography→genus implementation excludes islands in the same 10° physical block or first GIFT candidate source entity as the focal, but its original genus matrix includes accepted plant names shared with the focal on **other** donor islands. This is appropriate for some other-island source-community prediction questions, but is insufficient to describe a strictly *full-accepted-species-name-disjoint* forecast. Refit the stage-1 donor genus counts after excluding **every accepted species name in the focal GIFT full botanical roster** from all donor island lists. Apply the full focal-block and first-source *accepted-name* exclusion to **both** stage-1 genus donors and stage-2 external genus flower donors, not merely to their scored Direct subset. Re-normalize every donor island after filtering; drop empty/insufficient donors and recompute eligibility rather than assigning artificial zeros. The code-level audit rejects overlaps. Retain a separate original stage-1 published-scope sensitivity, clearly labelled and compared on matched focal test islands.

## Fixed two-stage model and scoring

For each original focal island i and endpoint c, use the region-equal, geographical-nearest-40, environmental-nearest-40 and environment+ranked-source-similarity predicted genus distributions from other *permitted* GIFT botanical islands. Preserve the stage-1 shared 0.02 unknown-genus mass in a **single explicit unknown bin**, never replacing it with the target's real genus label. For each Direct endpoint, learn external-region P(c | genus) from different accepted plants after the same full-accepted-name, physical-block and first-source exclusions; use the previous ALPHA=5 shrinkage against its own external genus-blind category prior. A genus not present among eligible independent external flower donors and the unknown-bin mass both use that same external blind flower distribution.

P_i(c | geography) = sum_g P_i(g | geography) × P_external(c | g). Score only the same **actual Direct plant carrier cohort** across all candidate models, on proper multiclass Brier and log-loss. Compare directly and paired on the **same island-level outcomes** against (1) outside-region genus-blind, (2) local-region genus-blind, (3) original generic regional-genus-weight source model, (4) geography-only nearest40, (5) environment-only nearest40, and (6) environment+source-nearest40. The *focal actual-genus oracle* is only a separately labelled upper-information comparator, never model input. Include both all-recorded and literal-original-native with missing-data support gates; do not require unrelated trait completeness.

Primary macroregions: northern midlatitudes, tropical and southern extratropical; northern high latitudes are reported regardless of lack of power. Report ALL signed gains, not only significant positives; for each endpoint/region/training mode, paired bootstrap intervals with **original physical 10° block** and **first GIFT candidate source group** separately, equal weight per source cluster (>=30 evaluable islands, >=10 clusters for each); require both proper scores with both grouping 95% interval lower bounds >0 for exploratory positive qualification. Report negative intervals, groups, unseen-genus weight, donor attrition, supported/unsupported endpoints and cohort-size changes. Treat these 95% intervals as exploratory and explicitly discuss 13×region×scope×mode testing multiplicity.

A strict three-region claim requires the same endpoint and positive direction across all three primary regions and all heldout-source training modes; also report each region's zero/negative results. A stronger functional-assembly claim additionally requires *improvement over the local-region category-frequency blind*, not just weaker external blind.

## Mechanistic interpretation ceiling

Positive scores would support observational predictability of floristic category composition conditional on GIFT geography and other-island floras; they do not directly identify dispersal, establishment, within-lineage adaptation, pollinator syndrome shifts, animal guild choice or seed set. The donor trait profiles are species-wide literature records from **other GIFT islands**, not measured ancestral mainland plants. Source similarity in ranked *candidate* mainland geography does not prove propagules arrived. Source name holdout is a prerequisite for stronger independent-donor claims. Keep raw plant/genus/island IDs and exact coordinates in protected source analysis; export only anonymous region/endpoint/gain/group summaries to fd main **after real-source CI succeeds**.

Implementation: src/fd/two_stage_floral_forecast.py is a tested numerical scoring/guardrail module. The stage-1 refit on original species-level GIFT source data and its real-data results are **not executed by this module**. A separate source-backed runner and workflow are still required.


## Exact diagnostic partition (evaluation only; no adaptation claims)

On the SAME focal island, distinguish (1) geography-predicted genus frequencies W_pred from actually recorded WHOLE-flora genus frequencies W_whole; (2) observed genus frequencies W_Direct among Direct-scored species from W_whole; and (3) observed Direct flower categories from externally predicted congener categories. With externally trained genus→flower matrix F, the **signed category frequency residual** has the exact identity

q_direct - W_pred F = (W_whole - W_pred) F + (W_direct - W_whole) F + (q_direct - W_direct F).

The three terms respectively diagnose **floristic genus forecasting**, **nonrandom availability of Direct floral annotations**, and **documented within-genus floral-category residuals**. The last is NOT a biological estimate of in-situ adaptation: literature trait annotations and genus-specific species identities differ across locations. These three signed vectors add exactly, but squared errors and Brier advantages do NOT decompose additively without cross terms. The new scoring module exposes an evaluation-only function with a synthetic exact-identity test.

## Functional-diversity observable distinct from categorical predictive skill

The source endpoints test whether botanical Direct **category frequencies** can be forecast. As a separate exploratory functional-diversity measurement, compute nominal categorical Rao/Gini–Simpson Q=1−Σ p_k² from *the same Direct flower-category vector* for each model and compare against both observed plug-in Q and finite-sample unbiased Q=n/(n−1)×Q_plugin. The implementation is categorical_simpson_diversity_diagnostic in src/fd/two_stage_floral_forecast.py, with synthetic estimator/ontology tests. This readout uses unit distances between nominal categories; it does **not** replace the original 496 recorded colour×form categorical state-set Jaccard, 17-D KDE or a continuous Gower/PCoA morphology volume, and is not an evolutionary test. Unbiased Q is highly uncertain for small Direct species counts (even may exceed the maximum theoretical population Q for low n), so retain sample-size sensitivities, no trait completeness cross-filter and never substitute Q gain for the proper Brier/log-loss primary gate. **No original GIFT categorical-Simpson effects are yet computed by this code.**

Original full 13-endpoint macro-forecast has also been staged separately as [island DRAFT PR #333](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/pull/333), covering all ten single Direct endpoints and three genuine same-species combinations across three macroregions and both status scopes; its real-data numerical outputs are not incorporated into the existing pilot study unless source CI and anonymized artifact verification succeed.
