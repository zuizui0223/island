## Two distinct questions that MUST NOT be conflated

### A. Exact **in-sample, within-region, species composition algebra** on geographically separated island pairs
For island i, let p_i(c) be directly documented source species-category frequencies, w_ig the original recorded carrier proportion of genus g, and q_g(c) the original distinct-accepted-species joint trait frequency within genus g from the original *broad-region* pool, **including focal island species**. Define m_i(c)=sum_g w_ig q_g(c); e_i(c)=p_i(c)-m_i(c). For EVERY original pair i,j:
||p_i-p_j||² = ||m_i-m_j||² + ||e_i-e_j||² + 2(m_i-m_j)·(e_i-e_j), EXACT algebraically.
The first term is the geometry of a region/genus composition *reconstruction*, the second documented within-genus deviation, and the third the interaction/cross term. The terms are **not mutually independent**, cross term can be NEGATIVE and components need not sum into two positive "percent explained" shares. NEVER report a causal percentage of adaptation/assembly. No positive-only decomposition, invented fixed-volume claims or phylogenetic independence.
