No artificial splitting to increase group count. Source can support a separate validation of mapped/checklist records, not replacement of the predeclared primary.

Bastidas-Urrutia et al. (2025), [JBI 10.1111/jbi.15038](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.15038), describes 17,771 BirdLife island-range matrix columns and a 2,399-island final filtered data set. Its data availability directs readers to the component databases, not a deposited range-only island × species matrix; the final study intersects eBird with BirdLife, excludes island-only species, etc. Do not substitute it under the current no-eBird and native/breeding map contract. The MAS data catalog lists [BirdLife range maps](https://macroecology-society.github.io/data-catalog/posts/BirdLife_ranges/) at 5 km and 2020 vintage; actual access and original data license must be confirmed, and resolution/geometry are not certified equivalent to PR66. Do not obtain restricted data from unverified mirrors. Official BirdLife [dataset information](https://datazone.birdlife.org/dataset-information) remains the primary route.
