## Pre-frozen traits and observed versus region-genus-relative comparison

Original sector outcome is the **fraction** of REAL Direct-recorded accepted plant names with: zygomorphic symmetry; tubular-or-bell_campanulate form; open_radial form; original deepest recorded tube-depth state; strict SC (excluding SI/mixed); strict autonomous reproductive capacity (excluding delayed/mixed); and one **real SAME-accepted-species** floral_form×floral_symmetry bilateral-tubular/bell combination. The recorded joint open_radial×actinomorphic category and documented absence of any specialist joint profile are separately labelled secondary, not silently added to the original seven primary tests.

Every sector island has >=5 high/medium original species-Direct trait carriers **for that particular endpoint** (not all traits), independently typed geology, valid original physical mainland distance, area, latitude, and 10° physical block. Within region, compare oceanic−continental using block-equal weighted island-level regression with covariates original `log2(1+distance_to_continent_km)`, log island area, **fraction of source botanical species with Direct trait**, log Direct plant carriers, absolute latitude. The effect unit is **percentage points of recorded sector carriers**, not an evolutionary probability. 999 block-resampling 95% descriptive intervals, CR1 cluster-robust t and separate first-rank GIFT candidate native source group reference are computed, not independent phylogenetic replicates.

The *in-region-genus-relative* response is the **same island measured sector share minus the analytically expected source-region within-genus categorical share** given actual genus-species carrier counts, using the original full geographically region-matched measured species pool (not just 425 geologically typed islands). This reference **includes focal species** and is an in-sample taxonomic composition sensitivity, NOT independent mainland historical ancestry or measured lineage selection.

## Scientific statement and publication boundary

This new result **FALSIFIES a simple claim that independently geologically oceanic islands consistently have fewer bilateral/tube special structural floral category records after documented opportunity, source genus and area/isolation controls**. It does NOT establish biological absence, no local selection, no pollen limitation or no true island pollinator transitions. The exact geological origin is known for only a selected 425/4,505 original island floras, original type/region source opportunities are imbalanced (160/43 tropical), and the source species-wide trait values are not local phenotypes.

**Main paper claim retained:** actual global floral categorical functional assemblage composition, original species/genus turnover, and region- and source-controlled tests of WHERE documented floral trait state space differs, instead of spurious 17D continuous KDE volume. Island geologic origin is a rigorously bounded **substudy**, never used to shrink the botanical global population to only typed islands. Deep, selfing, true plant-pollinator service, source genealogy and phenotype adaptation remain independent unanswered mechanisms.
