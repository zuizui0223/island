## Why the new audit differs from the previous positive-only analysis

The previous strict-original source result confirmed **10,399** positive site visitor×flower count rows with the **same accepted botanical species**, study/network/day and nonzero original flower count, representing **four GIFT islands /four source studies /174 plants /665 unique original flower survey keys**. However, positive visitor links cannot by themselves tell us which other botanical flowers were offered and not documented as visited.

We now anchor every original source floral count site on actual, exact GIFT island accepted species-positive visitor/flower observations, using the **same Study ID × Network ID × Site ID × calendar DAY × raw flower-count Units × GIFT island**. Additional potential flowers come from the published full EuPPollNet Flower_counts original field census, including species with no supported original positive visitor relation. We require original site GPS to be UNIQUE for that study/network across the full European source records, exact original botanical accepted species membership of the same GIFT island, correct source Site ID and date, original numeric flower count greater than zero, and a nonduplicate published flower-survey key. No centroid, inferred synonym, guessed local visitor guild or source-unit conversion is permitted.

## Interpretation and pending mechanism gate

The global botanical primary finding remains island floral functional category recurrence under taxonomic turnover, geographically heterogeneous true direct floral trait pairing, and genus-source assembly; this analysis does NOT reduce the botanical study population to four island communities. The new positive result is about the **availability of study/site/date-linked flower-resource *candidate choice sets***, not directional insect selection or ecological adaptation.

Next test is ORIGINAL study-method and source-effort documentation: determine whether the source survey recorded **all** potential flowers and visits in the same time-window, by animal guild, with comparable physical flower count units.
