## Question, distinction and parent population

Earlier Main findings (different estimands) stand: 7/13 original Direct endpoints show improved other-region genus-conditioned flower prediction **when the actual focal genus identity is known**, versus a genus-blind external prior; the naïve oracle-free donor species-genus mixture offers 1/13 globally versus an external genus-blind prior but **0/13 versus strong local-region category frequencies**. The original geography-based GIFT genus-composition models improved whole-flora recorded genus prediction slightly in three regions but did **not** previously demonstrate improvements for flower-category forecasts.

This new ORIGINAL 4,505-botanical-flora-parent study uses the predicted source genus weights from other island floras to forecast independent Direct flower categories, **without reading the target island actual genus roster into prediction**. It evaluates two *preselected* endpoints: floral form and true same-accepted-species primary flower colour × floral form. All source comparison models are scored on the same complete eligible Direct plant carriers, not selectively on matching genera. Both all-recorded and literal-known-native floristic scopes are retained; the latter is a nested documentation-selected sensitivity, not an independent study.

After original 10° physical-block and first candidate mainland-source entity restrictions, it further excludes EVERY accepted species name in the focal physical block and candidate source group from **both** GIFT island whole-flora genus donors and external-region flower species donors, even if an excluded name is recorded again on a distant donor island. The target whole-flora accepted name roster is used to define the holdout, not to set genus prediction probabilities. Unseen botanical genera share one explicit 0.02 source-prediction unknown-genus bin; a genus lacking permitted external flower congener states receives the external genus-blind category prior.

**Proper-score criterion**: positive paired multiclass Brier and log-loss improvements over the strong **target-region genus-blind Direct trait-frequency prior** with exploratory 499-resample 95% intervals positive under BOTH 10° physical-block-equal and first-source-entity-group-equal clusters. These intervals are not multiplicity adjusted and the grouping does not make studies/evolutionary histories independent.

## Scientific ceiling and next discriminating test

**Survives:** taxonomically conditioned floral-function category information remains geographically contingent and sometimes predicts real held-out botanical island flower composition better than strong regional category means. Global/regional nonuniformity is an empirical positive finding about prediction heterogeneity.

**Does NOT survive:** general superiority of two-stage floral composition forecasts in northern and tropical island floras; extra source/environment genus weighting over simple region-pooled genus supply under the strong all-status checks; a universal simple-flower island syndrome; within-lineage island adaptation, bird/butterfly/Sphingidae pollinator shifts, pollen-mediated fitness, observed mainland source ancestral phenotypes or causal guild turnover.

**Next source-sensitivity (not yet executed):** the pilot external congener flower-profile builder currently restricts externally learned profiles to genera also documented with local-region source Direct traits. Train profiles on **all eligible external genera** on identical focal island plant carrier sets and compare paired against this *constrained local-genus-support* model. Then vary unknown-genus probability and test all remaining 11 prespecified Direct endpoints, reporting negative cells as well as positives. This prevents attributing a regionally positive score to guild evolution or geographic source refinement without a direct matched test.
