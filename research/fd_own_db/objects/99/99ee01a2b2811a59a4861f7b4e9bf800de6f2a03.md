## Reason for the test

The original NHM numerical corolla tube length from 11,262 accepted taxa and the species-wide Direct deep-tube category are mutually related, but may reflect shared botanical study references, unequal regional source coverage and closely related plants. The previous 425-island tropical model measured +0.1080 Brier improvement for predicting Direct deep-tube category using actual numerical lengths of **different accepted source congener species** over a matched-source pooled-donor baseline. Removing the top five outcome-blind most documented genera still gave +0.0560 on a selected n274 cohort, but a stronger validation must distinguish NHM **named source corpus** from the same-corpus numerical training data.

For each of the FIVE *original* NHM corolla-length corpora **p**, hold out all its numeric donor values from calibration and fit the logistic numeric-cm→Direct-deep mapping ONLY on the other four original named corpora. For every tested accepted botanical genus, exclude the **ENTIRE genus's original Direct depth states** from that fitted calibration, including any focal island plants. Predict island species from different accepted GIFT candidate mainland *congeners* whose numerical lengths come from the withheld source p. Use BOTH original mainland source definitions: nearest five geographic source regions and climate-nearest ten of geographic fifty. Evaluate Brier loss against pooling the EXACT SAME heldout provider's numeric source donor plants, on the same focal island accepted plant species. Include each original candidate island with unresolved traits in the population denominator; no zero imputation of inaccessible flowers or unsupported provider calibrations. Source-provider-disjoint does NOT mean independent original botanical publications or field population observations.

## Scientific claim ceiling and next validation

**Supported:** Numerical flower tube length is associated with taxon-specific floral architecture in observed GIFT native candidate source pools; across FOUR different original NHM named corpus sources, distinct mainland congener lengths conditionally predict tropical original island species' Deep class better than pooling exactly those same donors. This weakens the argument that the whole-combined NHM source result is an artifact of only ONE source corpus. Each numerical calibration excludes the chosen donor corpus and the tested botanical genus itself.

**Not proven:** equivalent island and mainland genotype/flower-size phenotypes, unbiased native-only morphospace selection, homogeneous floral syndromes, bird vs butterfly vs moth effective pollen service, dispersal limitation versus establishment, or island local floral adaptive evolution. The four positive tropical corpus comparisons are on DIFFERENT eligible species/island cohorts. Original trait documentation balance is 0–3 islands per corpus and overall 8/425; a positive bootstrap interval for selected records is NOT an independent provider/observer causal interval.

**Next independent evidence needed:** the BHL×FloraChina exact SAME-island and SAME-plant test described above is now complete, but strict native n24 and total provider-record coverage-balanced n0 fail identification. New accepted-species native-island population morphology, genuinely independently sampled mainland relatives and plant-specific visitor effectiveness are needed before any ecological claim.
