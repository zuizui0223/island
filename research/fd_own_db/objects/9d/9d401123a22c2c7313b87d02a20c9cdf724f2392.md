## 2. Predefined predictive outcomes and negative controls

Original target outcome is each island's directly recorded species-level 5-category flower-primary-colour proportion on the actual source accepted plant species in that island. The candidate model estimates the **full category frequency distribution** from externally published NHM source-only species in each original island's union of five geographically closest GIFT mainland candidate native plant floras. Source colour-category counts are regularized to global external native mainland flower-colour category proportions using a fixed `alpha=20`. No genus-conditional oracle is required for the primary source-only composition prediction, avoiding the earlier restriction to 3–4% congeneric donor coverage.

The base comparator is the original global independent NHM GIFT mainland species colour-frequency prior. A stricter geography-specific negative control selects **two alternative original GIFT five-source assignments** per target island, from its same broad original geographical regime but DIFFERENT physical 10-degree blocks, with original mainland-source entity-set Jaccard <0.8 and both (i) distinct externally colour-described source donor **species count** and (ii) externally colour-described donor **genus richness** within 0.5–2× of the actual source. Selection ignores the focal original island flower-colour outcome. The same original target species and category labels are scored under actual and controls by strictly proper multicategory negative log loss and Brier; one island is one primary score vote.

A positive `placebo_loss − actual_source_loss` means real nearest geographic GIFT source assignment is more predictive of the original Direct island flower-category proportions than the two evidence-matched source alternatives. Physical 10-degree block and first-nearest-GIFT-source cluster equal resampling are computed separately with 1,999 conditional draws. They are NOT independent biological source histories or prospective inference p-values; actual source identities are candidate mainland floristic opportunity, not proven ancestors.
