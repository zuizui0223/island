## What is measured directly, and what is not

This analysis REPLACES prediction-score-first reporting with directly observed botanical **trait composition percentages** among distinct GIFT species lists and actual Direct category statuses. Main original source population remains **all 4,505 GIFT island botanical floras**. The source observations have 10 separately Direct traits; seven biologically predeclared morphology/reproductive sectors were directly tested without imposing the historical 928 triple-complete n30 condition:

1. Zygomorphic floral symmetry, vs other real documented symmetry states.
2. Tubular or bell-campanulate flower form, vs other real documented form categories.
3. Open radial floral architecture.
4. Deepest documented true floral-tube depth; never equated to tubular form.
5. Strict self-compatible category among SC/SI source Direct species only.
6. Strict autonomous selfing capacity vs directly recorded absent capacity; delayed/variable evidence is excluded from this strict binary and not recoded as zero.
7. True SAME-SPECIES floral form×symmetry bilateral-tubular/bell category; no synthetic cross-species combinations.

Native and endemic are **not synonyms**. The original archived taxon statuses use three SEPARATE columns `origin_status`, `endemic_status`, `floristic_status`. Strict `native_nonendemic` requires native AND nonendemic AND the consistent original floristic category; strict `endemic` requires native AND endemic AND the consistent floristic category; `introduced` requires explicitly recorded introduced origin. Any native with unresolved endemism is not put into the native-nonendemic control, and origin-unresolved plant records are not placed into either a native or introduced control.

**Critical detection/sample-bias evidence:** Original full island×plant accepted flora has **1,039,757 records**, of which **869,784 (83.65%)** lack resolved native-versus-introduced origin status. Original strict source categories (island×species, NOT distinct species radiations): **118,429 native_nonendemic**, **17,444 strict endemic**, **19,442 introduced**, **14,658 native with unresolved endemicity**, and **869,784 unresolved origin**. These nonrandom source-status/trait-quality filters substantially change the research sample and preclude treating a plant missing from the strict status tier as absent from the actual flora.

### Three different direct source contrasts

- **Native nonendemic minus source candidate not documented on island**: real fraction among strict native_nonendemic directly coded island plants minus real fraction among independently native-listed mainland candidate GIFT plants from the island's five geographically closest candidate mainland region lists, excluding every accepted botanical species name already on the island's FULL flora. This represents observed **plant composition sorting conditional on those source checklists**, NOT actual arrivals, propagule supply, establishment survival or extinction.
- **Stricter candidate-overlap native comparison:** limit island-side strict native_nonendemic plants to species ALSO appearing in the same five nearest source region lists; retain mainland candidate names absent from original full island flora. This is a more source-comparable **potential plant opportunity** comparison, still sensitive to mainland source listing size, plant range, native/floristic literature and geographic source mismatch.
- **Introduced minus same mainland candidate**: original introduced plant island direct category fraction minus mainland candidate-only fraction. It is a species assemblage of documented introduced/possibly horticultural records, NOT a direct contemporary propagule-release/naturalization experiment.
- **Endemic minus native_nonendemic on SAME physical island**: raw sector share, plus STRICT SAME-island SAME-botanical-GENUS category contrast. The latter estimates interspecific status-associated trait differences, NOT evolution or adaptive trait change. Source-label invariance within genus is an information limit.

Original primary unit: each physical **island** with >=5 Direct plant species in the relevant focal native/intro status and >=10 candidate source-only species where required; source-island contrast is an island mean. Report equal original 10° spatial-block cluster estimate and independent first-original GIFT-native-source-region grouping from 999 conditional resampling draws. All 7 sectors×5 source regions×status contrasts are exploratory, not multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory tests. A recorded absence is an *absence from the sampled checklist*, not true biologic failure to disperse or survive.

## Strict ENDEMIC-associated morphology and the actual information bottleneck

Raw **within-island** endemic-minus-native_nonendemic comparison usually fails >=30 island support: northern midlatitude has only **15** symmetry-qualified islands, tropical only **29**, and most other traits far less.

A separate **SAME-island SAME-genus** original comparison can qualify in 31 tropical botanical floras (19 10° blocks) for floral SYMMETRY. However its prior seeming nearly-zero proportion contrast was mostly a structurally uninformative label-matching effect: within those data there were **8 island×genus comparison units** where any Direct bilateral category state varied, comprising only **5 DISTINCT botanical genera**. After the exact unique-genus power audit in [successful original rerun #43712051851](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/43712051851), this is marked **NOT IDENTIFIABLE (<10 distinct actually variable genera)**, not measured no adaptation. Northern midlatitude has **0/150** recorded shared genus×island symmetry units with any difference between original endemic and native nonendemic categories and fails power likewise.

Other morphology is more variable but geographically underpowered: northern midlat tubular/bell has **22 variable original island×genus units across 19 distinct genera** but they occur in only **8 eligible physical islands /7 independent blocks**; tropical tubular/bell has **15 variable island×genus units across 15 distinct genera** but only **9 islands /8 blocks**. Strict SC and autonomous source status matches have fewer genuinely independent variation opportunities. This is the actual biological/floristic record evidence to motivate new same-genus native/endemic local population measurements and phylogeny.

**The distinction:** a particular endemic accepted species may differ from another native accepted species within its genus, but this is not observed independent repeated post-colonization trait change, ancestral direction, selection strength, effective pollinator switch or adaptive seed-set gain. Existing full phylogenetic backbone exact tip matches do not themselves identify actual sister taxa or preserve a true dispersal/arrival timeline.

## Next source listing-breadth falsification

A key unresolved confound is that species appearing in many GIFT native mainland source checklist regions may be better sampled and biologically widespread. A new frozen [original GIFT native source-breadth matching design](../../docs/DIRECT_FLORISTIC_SOURCE_BREADTH_MATCHING.md) compares source-listed strict native-nonendemic and not-recorded candidate mainland species on equal categories of how many distinct original 748 mainland GIFT regions list a plant (`1`, `2–3`, `4–10`, `11–30`, `31+`). It does **not** genus-match the first raw candidate sorting contrast, because genus replacement is the direct compositional outcome. Complete source original results belong in a subsequent reproducible addendum if/when they succeed; range listing frequency is **NOT** independent plant natural-range area or propagule pressure.

## Inference ceiling and manuscript decision

The direct observations support a **region-dependent difference in the assembly of documented island flower categories relative to plausible mainland GIFT candidate botanical source lists**, with morphological sectors shifting nonuniformly. The result is not a measured arrival/establishment/survival probability or an island-evolved adaptive pollination syndrome. Unlike predictability-first prior work, the outcome is an interpretable original botanical percentage-point contrast, but source checklists may omit taxa and in fact most original plant origin status is unknown. A positive/negative interval among many posthoc sector contrasts is exploratory and source-specific; no multiplicity-adjusted confirmation is implied.

**Next identifying data:** independently observed propagule-arrival vs introduced status and naturalization evidence (including intentional introduction/cultivation); plant local native/endemic provenance, independently derived geography, external source plant trait phenotypes with same local population measurements, true phylogenetic sibling/ancestral state reconstruction; and, for the mechanism, pollen supplement and local plant-visitor pollen effectiveness. The project cannot infer these by rescaling simple richness/FD or by rerunning an identical genus predictive model.
