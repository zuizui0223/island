## The major scientific correction

The main thesis is NOT that island flower functional space has a smaller volume, a 17D Gaussian KDE, or that an abstract kernel neighbourhood uniquely identifies real pollinators. The measured response is **which actual morphological category proportions vary with physical island isolation**. Individual island floras are the independent unit for descriptive response modelling; botanical species can occur on multiple islands and 10° spatial blocks do not guarantee independent species histories.

The original main population remains **4,505 island floras**. ## Original region-specific predeclared six directional hypotheses

Each original focal island contributes ONE observed phenotypic sector proportion among its species-Direct recorded plant members; requires ≥5 Direct documented plants on that axis only. Original category meanings:
- **bilateral_zygomorphic**: exact floral_symmetry zygomorphic, NOT all symmetrical flowers or proven bee pollination;
- **tube_or_bell**: exact floral_form either tubular or bell_campanulate, NOT measured deep tube;
- **open_radial**: exact floral_form open_radial;
- **deepest**: exact tube_depth_class deep.

Regressor is **log2(1 + original continent distance in km)**: coefficient is the *absolute observed sector proportion* change when (1 + km distance) doubles. A 0.01 coefficient means **1 percentage point** (not a relative 1% change). The original weighted linear-proportion model contains an intercept and the source covariates log1p(island area), original directly documented trait/carrier fraction, log(original n Direct carriers) and absolute latitude. Each physical 10° spatial block contributes equal total regression weight; CR1 group-sandwich t uncertainty and 999 full physical block-resampling draws are reported. A separate first-ranked GIFT source-entity grouped estimation is sensitivity. No model is a causal separation of climate, ancestral mainland source, genetic history or actual flower visitor behaviour. No pseudo-independent dyad or numerical resampling replication p-value.

## Strict original-native and high-latitude falsification

**Taxonomically verified native-status sensitivity is neither randomly assigned nor uniformly documented.** In the original *tropical strict native-known* floras, only **127** directly documented symmetry n≥5 islands with sufficient isolation remain (43 physical blocks); the zygomorphic proportion slope is **+0.813 percentage points** with block interval **[−0.359,+2.001]**. This is a **different original island/taxon subset and does NOT reproduce** the all-recorded negative slope. Do not interpret the sign change as an effect of introduced species: both original flora eligibility and botanical species composition changed.

**High latitude is NOT proven invariant.** The overlapping original >=60° absolute-latitude nested sensitivity has **653** zygomorphic n≥5 islands (62 blocks) and **506** deepest-tube n≥5 islands (45 blocks). Descriptive block-equal observed slopes are **−0.675 [−1.389,−0.098]** percentage points per doubling for zygomorphic and **−0.424 [−0.759,−0.070]** for deepest tube. Both are exploratory nested multiple comparisons; genus-relative physical-block slopes are closer to zero and no equivalent-to-zero hypothesis has been established. Thus the proposed no-highlatitude-gradient interpretation is NOT empirically confirmed by this cross-sectional data.

Southern-extratropical original n≥5 Direct floral form sector gradients also show exploratory raw tubular/bell **−1.260 [−2.340,−0.329]** and open-radial **+0.978 [+0.317,+1.680]** percentage points per distance doubling (157 island floras/37 original blocks). Within regional genera residual slopes remain −0.370 [−0.626,−0.103] and +0.304 [+0.085,+0.588] percentage points, respectively, but these are **NOT in the predeclared primary six-test family**, the small regional native roster and multiple posthoc analyses remain material. Do not promote as confirmed replicated adaptation.

## Scientific interpretation

**What is newly established as a legitimate observed source category association:** independent original GSHHG isolation measurements and actual single Direct flower structural category shares identify a **geographically direction-specific recorded floral *assemblage composition* gradient**, strongest in the measured all-recorded tropical zygomorphic fraction. This advances beyond an uninformative continuous KDE or even scalar categorical Rao Q.

**What is NOT established:** a genuinely missing specialist pollination niche, lost bee visitor guild, historical flower category extinction, endemic local adaptation, within-species evolution, mainland-island direct pollen fitness, or universally decreased high-latitude functional complexity. Original genus composition is a strong rival and the strict native-only source changes the result. Area, lat and source Direct trait documentation are statistically adjusted only within source sampling opportunities and cannot guarantee unbiased missingness.
