- Global original population: **4,505** independently archived GIFT botanical island floras, with ten independent Direct botanical traits, not a mandatory 3-trait-complete 928-island subset. Original GSHHG geography covers 8,264 physical island entities; 4,504 of 4,505 GIFT plant floras geographically join.

## Real biological sectors

Exactly two separate main morphological axes and two reproductive outcomes, with original vocabulary frozen:
1. bilateral: floral_symmetry == zygomorphic; NEVER call "symmetrical" universally zygomorphic or use inferred pollination guild.
2. tube-or-bell: floral_form in {tubular, bell_campanulate}; deep-tube is a separately recorded ORDERED tube_depth_class == deep. Do not conflate tubular architecture with depth.
3. open radial: floral_form == open_radial.
4. SC versus SI: self_incompatibility in {SC,SI}, excluding mixed/variable; SC does not demonstrate autonomous self-fertilization.
5. autonomous vs absent: autonomous_selfing_capacity in {autonomous,absent}, excluding mixed/variable/delayed and any nonbinary categories.
6. true SAME-SPECIES joint bilateral-tube/bell and open-radial-actinomorphic profiles, using only floral_form AND floral_symmetry recorded on the very same accepted species. Species missing either trait never supply artificial cross-product categories.

For each trait, analysis is per island's proportion of real DIRECT labelled accepted species in the named sector. A "missing" sector means "not documented among source-direct eligible species", NOT biological absence. Secondary report also counts explicit absence of the real bilateral-tube/bell form×symmetry sector conditional on >=5 directly observed same-species carriers.
