The strict bat taxonomy assigns family to 1,382/1,428 range names, with 21 unresolved and 25 diet-family conflicts. Remaining exclusions may select regions or lineages. No animal group demonstrates robust confirmed causal pollinator filtering. The all-four-guild common source intersection is at most seven islands even after MDD matching.

## Methodological conclusion, not a biological causal result

The strongest defensible finding across these sensitivity analyses is that the apparent guild-trait association is not transportable without attention to taxonomic classification, source coverage and fixed comparison populations. The data do not establish that taxonomy is the actual causal mechanism, nor that island pollination syndromes are absent. Coefficient sign consistency on overlapping panels is insufficient.

The next test is the original protected GIFT4505 species-Direct analysis (island draft PR #348), using fixed regional genus x evidence-grade expectations and species-coherent permutations, independently reproduced animal overlap, and Holm correction. This is post-pilot exploration, not a held-out confirmation. Plant genus conditioning is not a phylogenetic model or evidence of population adaptation. Distinct source records may not measure effective pollination.
