## One externally observed real visitor-guild association

For **true same species** open radial form × actinomorphic symmetry × shallow tube, within actual source publications positive for BOTH Bombus and Syrphidae:

| Evidence scope | Comparable publications / studies with ≥1 true motif-positive visited plant | Mean documented motif frequency, Bombus MINUS Syrphidae, original study-equal [95% descriptive publication bootstrap] |
|---|---:|---|
| Positive flower visitor/pollen evidence grades 1,2,3 | **99 / 41** | **−0.036917 [−0.051898,−0.023130]** |
| **Direct original flower visitor observation**, both pollinator and plant survey coded all | **60 / 22** | **−0.033032 [−0.050643,−0.017428]** |
| Original positive observations restricted to genus names present in both guild groups | **91 / 18** | **−0.006138 [−0.010066,−0.002607]** |
| Pollen carriage / confirmed grades 1 or 2 | 10 / **4** motif-positive publications | NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_MOTIF_POSITIVE_STUDY_SUPPORT |
| Direct confirmed grade 1 alone | 4 /2 | NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_INDEPENDENT_STUDIES |

**Interpretation:** among European GIFT-island-listed plant species with true Direct flower traits, open shallow radial architecture is more frequent among documented hoverfly-visited plants than among Bombus-visited plants in the SAME source publications. This is biologically compatible with different floral access channels, not with a confirmed *pollination* syndrome transition or island evolution. Publication-level comparisons do not control within-publication site/time availability, floral abundance, detection or multiple studies' common botanical lineages. Genus conditioning reduces the magnitude; source evidence and observed island genus composition are both selective. This contrast is post hoc within a wider panel, so the unadjusted descriptive bootstrap intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory p values.

## Nonidentification is substantive, not an assumption of no syndrome

- **Sphingidae vs Bombus, white×radial×tube/bell×deep:** 19 sources mention both broad guilds, but **0** with ≥2 scored plants per guild for this actual four-axis recorded phenotype. No Sphingidae-vs-Bombus deep-tube guild effect can be estimated using this strict profile.
- **Butterfly vs Bombus, yellow_orange×radial×deep:** 24 trait-qualified within-publication comparisons, but only **6** distinct publications with any positive motif; therefore NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_MOTIF_POSITIVE_STUDY_SUPPORT. The numerical preliminary point estimate is not promoted as established guild affinity.
- **Bombus vs hoverfly, blue_purple×zygomorphic×deep:** 46 eligible studies but **zero** positives for this rare 3-axis Direct combination. The apparent exact zero is **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE** rather than proof that bees and flies have equivalent ecological effects or that the morphology is absent.
- Actual DoPI v2 original evidence grades are sharply unbalanced: Sphingidae original records **466**, only **3** grade-1 directly confirmed and **27** grade-2 pollen carriage; butterflies **21,456**, grade-1 **27** and grade-2 **527**; Bombus **202,958**, grade-1 **2,658** and grade-2 **18,964**. Grades 3 are positive visitation only, grade 4 is robbery/nonpollinating, and source grade NA/out-of-range is excluded rather than assigned 0. These are source data RECORD counts, not independent true effective interactions.
- The DoPI evidence is European, primarily insects; no reliable tropical bird pollination-calibration cohort or island-local guild absence exists in this dataset. Original GloBI records can overlap underlying DoPI literature; study-level original provenance dedup remains to be checked. No web-derived insect abundance or old GBIF nondetection is treated as guild loss.
