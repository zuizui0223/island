## What this changes scientifically

The source-tested southern two-stage flower forecast still has a numerical gain versus a poorly calibrated **local-region genus-blind** source. But an external-region **genus-blind** categorical prior already performs substantially better than that local baseline; the extra advantage of geography→genus→flower prediction over this external blind prior is much smaller. The **direct paired uncertainty confirms the reference baseline reversal**, it does not identify which mechanism caused it.

A priori geographic source-ranked GIFT entities are geographic candidate donors, not measured mainland ancestral flower phenotypes or propagule establishment probabilities. Regional Direct phenotype documentation, source botanical status, taxonomic composition, source sample support, category ontology and regional trait frequency calibration could all contribute. There is no local guild switch or effective pollination experiment here. Do **not** describe the baseline difference as a causal share of the southern original stage-2 gain, a demonstration of adaptive convergence, or a globally replicated island simplification. This is an original **floristic source-frequency transferability** contrast.
