## Identification and what was truly held constant

On EACH original island, restrict to the exact SAME accepted plant species with independently high/medium species-Direct categories of all THREE axes: **floral symmetry, floral form, true tube depth**. Define two separate real same-plant indicators: actinomorphic × deep, and tubular/bell × deep. Use the exact SAME different-accepted-species **potential mainland source-only donors with all THREE axes Direct** to estimate BOTH endpoints, excluding every accepted plant occurring anywhere on the focal island's entire original flora from both source pools. Both geo5 and climate10 source modes share the identical focal target species, with genus donor support required in BOTH modes. The source predictor uses observed island botanical genus membership, NOT a model of what genus arrived or became established.

The supplementary deep-only stratum requires a true Direct deep tube on both the focal and source donor species, then compares the symmetry and form categories holding the recorded deep state fixed. Status scopes: all-recorded / independently native / native-nonendemic. Actual genus donor gates: at least one or two other accepted mainland donor species per genus. The analytical categories comprise **240** nested panels, NOT 240 independent discoveries. A support gate requires at least 30 supported islands, 10 independent geographic 10-degree blocks and 10 actual first GIFT source groups; only **24** panels satisfy it. One of those has zero pooled baseline risk in more than 1% of bootstrap resamples and correctly lacks a ratio estimate, leaving **23 baseline-normalized numerical panels**.

**Prediction model:** The source-genus-weighted Brier error is compared with the exact same source candidate species *pooled across their matched genera*. On precisely the same accepted target plants, raw gain = Brier(pooled source)-Brier(source genus), and the endpoint's **relative Brier Skill Score (BSS)** = gain / Brier(pooled source). To avoid selectively omitting low-error islands, the BSS is the ratio of full physical-block-equal mean gain to physical-block-equal mean pooled Brier. Then compare BSS(actinomorphic×deep) minus BSS(tubular/bell×deep). Uncertainty: 999 whole geographical-block bootstrap resamples, independently repeated grouping by first GIFT mainland source entity. All are **descriptive, exploratory conditional intervals** rather than preregistered or multiplicity-adjusted tests.

## Severe documentation and causal-identification ceiling

**Only 2/145** original tropical all-recorded eligible islands and **17/1,049** northern-midlatitude islands have within-10-percentage-point island versus BOTH potential mainland source modes' actual three-Direct-character documentation fractions, even after conditioning on the supported genus sets. Thus, although the same-species, same-source-donor, baseline-frequency-normalized descriptive signal is positive, it is **NOT demonstrated under an adequately powered strict documentation-balanced sample**. Results could be inflated or distorted by nonrandom botanical phenotype recording, source/provider overlap and selection on congener documentation. Source provider and archipelago were not excluded as entire folds. Botanical genus composition is known in the forecast, not predicted from colonization opportunity.

Flower morphology categories are original accepted-species descriptions and do NOT report local island phenotypes versus ancestral populations. Neither visitors nor effective pollen transfer are observed in this model. **Do not claim island-specific adaptive change, guild replacement, bird/butterfly/hawkmoth/Bombus specialization, colonization causality, or a universal narrowing of floral space.** The global 4,505 island taxonomic assembly and functional beta-diversity are the manuscript mainline; this stringent matching result is a supplementary, hypothesis-generating support analysis. The former uncalibrated 17-dimensional KDE volume is not a substantive result.

**Falsifiable continuation:** first, obtain genuinely provenance-independent plant morphology / source documentation and do out-of-archipelago / source-provider held-out predictions; second, only then ask if independently observed and effort-qualified guild availability predicts the remaining floristico-functional deviation. A missing pollinator record is not evidence of ecological absence.
