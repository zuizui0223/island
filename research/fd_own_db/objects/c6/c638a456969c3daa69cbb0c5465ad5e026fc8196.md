## Change from earlier binary bipartite null

The [earlier 2-chain ×40-snapshot result](README.md) and original run #44213550467 used valid checkerboard swaps preserving EACH real accepted plant's observed degree and EACH REAL insect species' observed botanical host degree, inside the exact original publication × published location × year × month. Those are only observed positive visitor-species links, not true biological non-visits or field pollen deposition.

This stricter sensitivity uses **FOUR independently seeded chains with EIGHTY snapshots per chain**, a maximum across all four source chain means of **0.20** recorded guild count instead of 0.35, and exact unchanged plant/insect degrees after every sampled swap. The source network motif is withheld as NOT_IDENTIFIABLE if swap opportunities are too scarce or chains disagree. This is a **diagnostic**, NOT a proof of Markov chain uniform mixing.

Across 16,757 published positive source site/month networks, source inspection identified **14,208** with insufficient bipartite opportunity, **1,888** with no adequately documented direct motif-positive/negative botanical species comparisons, **36** with source chain disagreement, and **one** >400-edge oversized site network. Within selected 4 morphological axes (open radial, tubular/bell, zygomorphic, and directly deep tube), the source paper publication key (not individual edges or plant × date observations) is the unit for each raw source estimate and for the 999-replicate bootstrap. **These four were chosen after earlier 27-motif exploration and are not an independent prospective preregistered hypothesis family.**

## Ecological inference and manuscript ceiling

**Observed:** Open radial flowers among European positively documented accepted GIFT-listed botanical plants have lower recorded insect visitor-guild repertoire than other original Direct flower morphologies within the same source studies/sites/months, after controlling each plant's distinct observed visitor-species count and each insect species' observed degree. The result is negative across most source papers and robust to the specified simultaneous source-study uncertainty and leave-one-paper-out exercise.

**NOT OBSERVED:** Actual botanical flower abundance, visit intensity, independently sampled same-site visitor guild non-detection, true insect preference, legitimate pollen deposition, effective pollination, original island-local visit community changes, ancestral/island within-species trait evolution, plant genus/phylogeny-fixed effect (the source has severely insufficient *within-genus morphology variation*), or any true tropical bee→butterfly/bird/hawkmoth syndrome shift. We cannot infer that open flowers are intrinsically specialized; genus/source and survey biases remain.

A strict bipartite degree-fixed randomization differs from a causal guild-choice experiment. Alternative degree-preserving Curveball implementation or longer chains would be a valuable method-specific robustness check if mechanistic claims were contemplated, but do not substitute for field plant × visitor / pollen transfer experiments. [Network degree-null methodological source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6072652/). Visitation and effective pollen transfer must be separated; [King et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12074) directly documents why flower visitation alone is an inadequate surrogate for effective pollination.

**Manuscript decision:** keep the 4,505-island functional beta, mainland candidate source, true paired floral architecture and signed genus accounting PRIMARY. Use these European DoPI original results only as an auxiliary floral access versus documented visitor-guild capability/limitations test; explicitly state open-radial source counterexample to the assumption that physically open flowers automatically have more documented visitor guilds.
