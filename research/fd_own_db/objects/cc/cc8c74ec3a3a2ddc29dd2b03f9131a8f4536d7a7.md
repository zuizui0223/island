# 2026-11-15 — Across-region genus-trait transportability, name-disjoint source test

**Design frozen before checking the new original experiment.** Exploratory, not preregistered or a test of mainland population adaptation. The full ORIGINAL 4,505 GIFT botanical island flora population is retained and audited across 2,205 northern-midlatitude, 1,558 tropical, 317 southern-extratropical, 424 northern-high-latitude and one original geography-unresolved flora. Ten separately high/medium species-Direct floral/reproductive categories and three real same-species two-trait endpoints (13) are analyzed. The old 928 triple-trait complete case is not the population.

### Question
Is category morphology of a genus from **other nonfocal regions** sufficient to predict the composition of the focal island's documented plants of that genus, or do genus-specific category profiles change depending on regional source community / recording practice?

### Strong name and geography exclusions
For each target physical GSHHG broad region R:
- A **local-exclusive donor species** must be recorded in R but NOT in ANY different defined GSHHG region's original botanical full-flora/strict source-native list (depending on study source scope).
- An **external-exclusive donor species** must be recorded in at least one other independently labelled GSHHG region and NOT on ANY flora in R.
- Species names occurring in both R and elsewhere are excluded from BOTH candidate donor pools. A plant only recorded on the one geography-unresolved island is not an independent geographically assignable region source.
- Regardless of donor classification, **every accepted species name in the target island's ENTIRE original botanical flora (not only the measured endpoint or original-native subset)** is removed from all local/external training and prior contributions. Outside-region donor species are disjoint from all target-region original floras by construction.
- Original species-wide Direct trait labels originate from the existing island-oriented research ledger and are NOT independent mainland field morphometry; full plant-name withholding is NOT bibliographic source, phylogenetic or trait provider independence.

### Equal-genus information
For a genus g represented in the focal island and having >=1 distinct Direct source donor species in BOTH the local-exclusive and external-exclusive candidate pools after target full-flora withholding, use `m_g=min(n_local,n_external,20)` source species from each side, chosen deterministically by original ACCEPTED taxon name hash, not by observed floral trait category, source score or original target phenotype. This matches **genus identity and per-genus donor sample count** exactly across the two models. Other focal genera with no balanced local+external donor data are not silently considered biological absences: both conditional models fall back to the SAME source-only category prior. The common prior is computed from external-region-exclusive Direct donor names, independent of original focal region's target species. Source genus probability uses 5 prior pseudocounts; prior has Jeffreys 0.5 per complete real observed category. No unmeasured cross-products or multiple independent island species traits are created.

**Two explicit endpoints on identical original target floras:** (a) absolute island-averaged improvement of actual original species-Direct frequency-vector D² when predicting with balanced local-exclusive genus donors instead of balanced external-exclusive congeneric donors (higher = regional specificity); (b) average per-plant proper categorical log loss and Brier gain on just the original directly recorded species in focal genera with >=1 donor in both reference regions. Report denominator/retained original island and carrier proportions. No effect estimate is reported if fewer than 30 supported focal island floras or 10 independently indexed original physical 10° blocks. The island-level all-genus metric includes unresolved genera at identical prior so no false zero is injected. Unmatched source opportunity is reported as source knowledge absent.

Physical block equal weighting and 999 outcome-blind reassignments of original 10° clusters yields descriptive 95% resampling ranges, separate first-GIFT-original mainland source grouping if source field is available. Monte Carlo re-selections, 4 geographical regimes, the overlapping all-status vs strict original-native scope, the 13 correlated trait endpoints and source blocks are NOT independent evolutionary histories. Every supported region and endpoint is shown with positive **and negative** effects, not just winners; pooled generality is evaluated only after complete regional/source support census. Actual region source record counts, donor numbers, matched genus fraction and the proportion of target genera with actual multiple documented category states must be published.

### Claim ceiling
A transferable genus category profile indicates source-recoded categorical conservation / study metadata generalization under known genus membership. A local model advantage indicates **conditional region/source-recording-specific trait predictability**, NOT within-species adaptation, colonization success, response to altered pollinator guilds, pollen effectiveness, or a historically independent phylogenetic source. A zero result with poor matched donor congener coverage means *not identified*, never biologically absent.
