## Two EXACT finite-population nulls

**Null A — all source Direct plant species exchangeable within geographical listing-breadth bin.** For each island/bin, combine accepted names from native and candidate lists. With N=n+s source names and K documented sector-positive states, randomly label exactly n as native and s as source-only, without replacement; exact native-positive count Y~Hypergeom(N,K,n). Then `E[D_b]=0` and `Var[D_b]=(1/n+1/s)^2 n*(K/N)*(1−K/N)*s/(N−1)`. All bins are disjoint by construction. This preserves original available source species set, observed native and mainland Direct count per bin, source listing breadth and recorded trait states. It does NOT preserve original genus representation, phylogeny, botanical provider effects or actual seed arrivals. As a separate direct taxonomic outcome, compute EXACT expected number of shared/only-on-native/only-in-candidate genera under these same random source species assignments, without inventing trait states.

**Null B — same source and breadth, PLUS actual botanical genus membership counts.** Inside every island/bin/genus, fix exact n_native_g and n_candidate_g accepted original botanical plant counts, exchange the actual Direct 0/1 category states of DIFFERENT species within that genus without replacement. For genus g with N_g total Direct source species, K_g sector-positive, native n_g, `E[D_b]=Σ_g (n_g/n−s_g/s)(K_g/N_g)`, and `Var[D_b]=(1/n+1/s)^2 Σ_g Var_Hypergeom(N_g,K_g,n_g)`. Candidate and source name species numbers, breadth bins, taxonomic genus representation and full source categorical label vector remain fixed; **this is an in-sample association null**, not independent genus ancestry or local adaptation. A positive/negative `D_obs−E_genus` is within-genus category association conditional on observed genus membership, but may be uninformative if almost all source genera have only one observed category state.

**Expected genus presence under Null A.** For every observed source genus present in at least one matched breadth bin, the exact chance of being ABSENT from the randomly selected native sample is the product over bins `P_b(Y_genus_native=0)=∏_{j=0}^{m_gb−1}(s_b−j)/(N_b−j)` (zero if the source-only group is too small); the exact chance of being ABSENT from source candidates is `P_b(Y_genus_source=0)=∏_{j=0}^{m_gb−1}(n_b−j)/(N_b−j)`. Sum these probabilities over actual source genera to get expected candidate-only/native-only counts; `E[shared]=G_total−E[source-only]−E[native-only]`. Compare observed **actual** genus counts with these exact finite-source richness/availability expectations per eligible island. Do NOT use a uniform profile-count k null; source taxonomic occurrence and GIFT breadth are controlled directly.

**Recording opportunity audit.** Conditional on actual source-status sets, measure the fraction of accepted plant names with an original high/medium Direct category among (i) all source-listed original strict native-nonendemic and (ii) all source-only GIFT candidate mainland species. ## Scientific ceiling
