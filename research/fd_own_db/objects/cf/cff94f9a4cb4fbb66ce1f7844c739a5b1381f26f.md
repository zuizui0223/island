## Original source study flower-count Units are heterogeneous

Among the 11,041 qualified positive source records, original exposure units are not the same physical measure. Examples with documented source-row totals: `Average flowers per species-site-day` **4,523**, `Number of flowers/square meter` **3,138**, `% flower cover` **1,870**, `Number of sub-inflorescences/square meter` **1,030**, `Number of spikes/square meter` 238, `Number of stalks/square meter` 162, `Number of heads/square meter` 29, `Number of inflorescences/square meter` 10, `percent cover of flowers or inflorescences per 60m2` 41. Raw counts cannot be pooled as the same number of flowers or one common ecological exposure variable. Their totals are counts of **positive visitor rows carrying such unit**, not number of independent flower-count plots.

## Scientific conclusion and what would be needed next

1. **Positive original data-quality finding:** original EuPPollNet flower availability can be linked to the GIFT island accepted plants' actual field visitor sources with source-preserving site and date, but available denominators rapidly shrink from 13 positive-citation islands to **4 quantitative-flower-source-supported islands**. This is an empirical identification boundary, not ecological absence.
2. Original flora phenotype remains **species-wide species-Direct** (not population-measured island-local flowers); the flower survey units are heterogeneous and efforts to count insect visits differ by source study. No source-island negative visitor observation or effective pollen deposition has been measured.
3. An apparent abundance-normalized pollinator preference effect should NOT be fit globally or used as a biological conclusion from the 11,041 rows. The true independent population is at most four GIFT island communities with source count coverage, and each genuine flower×guild category has only one or two original independent study sites and one or two directly scored botanical species.
4. New empirical data needed: independent contemporaneous plant×visitor observations **with source-measured local flower availability and visitor non-detection/effort**, ideally paired across independent mainland source vs multiple oceanic and continental islands, with pollinator family and effective pollen transfer/fruit/seed set or repeated plant population traits. Public source aggregators may overlap original papers and cannot replace this gate.

**Study positioning:** main result remains global 4,505 GIFT botanical functional-category recurrence, region-dependent real paired flower architecture and genus source-composition. This supplementary original European flower-supply audit documents a clear boundary before attributing any island syndrome to pollinator replacement.
