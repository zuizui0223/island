## Why the earlier source-marginal null was not sufficient

The [previous source-original positive-guild documentation opportunity test](FIXED_EFFORT_POSITIVE_GUILD_PORTFOLIO.md) fixed each plant's number of positively documented distinct visitor *species* and the site/month number of positive edges for each visitor *guild*. However, a particular **insect species** can be documented with many plant taxa, whereas another visits only one. Guild-label permutations can break this visitor identity-degree structure and manufacture an unrealistic reference. A stricter fixed–fixed, binary bipartite ecological network source reference was therefore implemented.

For each identical original publication × published site/grid × year × month:

1. Keep real GIFT-island-listed accepted plant IDs, real independently resolved DoPI visitor binomial IDs, original positive plant×insect edges and visitor guild taxonomy. Every original species-wide GIFT Direct morphological profile retains its own observation mask. Reject unresolved animal labels such as `Bombus sp.` and duplicate species-author spellings.
2. Randomize actual binary plant×visitor-species EDGES by valid 2×2 checkerboard swaps only: **each plant's positively documented insect species count and EACH visitor species' positively documented plant degree remain EXACT**. Species membership, guild labels, number of recorded links, site, study and month never change. No false ecological non-visits are created; rearrangements are only a hypothetical conditional network benchmark.
3. Two independently seeded fixed-degree chains from the original source site, each 25×edges attempted swaps as burn-in and **40 snapshots** separated by 4×edges proposed swaps; count accepted actual swaps, check every node degree after every realization, and withhold a site-motif estimate if fewer than max(10, edges/2) swaps per chain are accepted or if chain mean contrast disagreements exceed **0.35 recorded guild categories**. Sites with genuinely unchangeable small bipartite matrices are **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE**, not evidence of no guild preference.
4. Source study-clustered aggregation: each qualified site/month's **observed versus swapped-expected difference** in the recorded insect-guild-count of motif-positive minus motif-negative botanical species (>=2 botanical species in each state, >=2 actual positive visitor insect species per plant). Average source sites within publication, then source publications equally, and bootstrap entire original publications 999 times. No preregistered hypothesis, global multiple-testing correction, cross-species/family exchangeability, or true field-site floral abundance model.

The full source archive has **676,900** original DoPI positive/nonpositive literature interaction rows; **193** source paper keys contribute eligible published site/time evidence; **16,757** source study-site-year-month positive visitor network units were considered, with **14,208** failing the basic bipartite opportunity requirement, **1,888** not supplying both directly phenotyped motif states with >=2 plants each, **one** oversized network and **one** with between-chain disagreement. These are nested source *network eligibility checks*, not original GIFT islands excluded from the main 4,505 botanical population.

The four PRE-SELECTED FOR THIS SENSITIVITY (after earlier source patterns were observed) one-axis Direct morphology outcomes are open radial, tubular/bell, zygomorphic and deep tube. This is intentionally exploratory, NOT independent preregistered replication.

## Scientific conclusion and limits

**Retained observed association:** Among European positively documented study-site/year-month plant visitors for botanical taxa present somewhere in global GIFT island floras, open radial flowers do NOT show greater recorded insect-guild breadth; the source-documentary deficit remains negative after fixing **both** plant and insect species positive-link degrees. This counters the naive assumption that a physically open flower must have more observed positive visitor guild categories.

**NOT identified:** true pollinator preference given local flower supply, actual field-hour detection-corrected guild richness, efficiency of pollen deposition/seed set, historical island pollinator regime shifts, in situ same-species island floral trait evolution, independence from plant botanical lineage composition (previous [strict genus-equal source tests](GENUS_EQUAL_WITHIN_STUDY_AUDIT.md) and [positive co-visit genus-null tests](POSITIVE_MULTI_GUILD_COACCESS.md) remain sparse), and tropical bird/butterfly/hawkmoth morphology-specific selection. In particular, the present binary configuration reference preserves degree sequences but **does not preserve which visitor species choose which botanical trait**, which is the conditional association being screened, nor does it account for plant abundance, rewards or detectability. The null is not an observation of "if the pollinators disappeared" or "the flowers would evolve".
