## A. Novel source result: categorical accuracy and NOMINAL functional-diversity accuracy are different axes

For each scored real GIFT island, the model predicts a genuine plant categorical distribution p from geographically forecast genus mixture and other-island congeneric species-Direct flower/mating states. Compare **proper categorical multiclass Brier + log loss** versus the strong LOCAL regional genus-blind category-frequency prior (positive interval required for *both* proper losses and *both* physical 10°-block/first candidate-source group 499 bootstrap intervals). In a SEPARATE same-island response, compare the model's predicted **nominal categorical Rao/Gini–Simpson Q=1−Σp_k²** against the observed **finite-sample iid-adjusted Q_hat = n/(n−1)(1−Σ(n_k/n)²)**, requiring improved **absolute Q prediction error** under BOTH independent source-group exploratory intervals. Keep plugin Q as a source-sample-size sensitivity, never conflate nominal unit-category distance with mixed-trait Gower or a 17D KDE hypervolume.

## D. Meaning for a functional island biogeography manuscript

**Supported:** GIFT botanical floristic category-source transfer has geographically heterogeneous performance across taxa and traits, and some true species-Direct categorical nominal diversity source forecasts improve over a strong region-local reference. Two particularly restricted exact source-prediction cases (tropical symmetry, Southern flower colour) survive local/external/pooled genus categorical reference tests, matched-cohort corrected nominal Q, matched original donor sample size, both proper scores and two geographically grouped exploratory intervals.

**Not supported:** globally invariant island floral simplification, true pollination-syndrome switching, causal island adaptation, phylogenetic above-null functional convergence, colonization versus adaptive divergence, or well-calibrated continuous flower-morphology trait hypervolume. All scored botanical trait observations are species-wide projected to GIFT island floras, source candidate regions are geographical and flower categorical donor species are OTHER GIFT islands, not an observed ancestral mainland historical transition.

**Main limitations:** original parent 4,505 island floras but exactly 59/78 source-panel geography qualified; source-native Southern 0/13; three source name-hash samples correlated; sources selected by botanical Direct trait documentation; original proper loss intervals exploratory and unadjusted for 39 correlated all-recorded region×trait tests; Q unbiased n/(n−1) requires iid species observation model not warranted by nonrandom plant checklist photography/publication; nominal category distance differs from additive Gower/mixed trait morphological distance and from 17D hypervolume. Keep all negative and ambiguous cells in supplemental material. Claims about effective bee/bird/butterfly/moth pollination require independent original biological visitor/pollen/fitness measurements.
