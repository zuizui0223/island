## Direct trait source availability is another nonrandom gate

High/medium single-species botanical Direct documentation varies sharply by endpoint. Original all-recorded across three regions, **cleistogamy** has ZERO scoreable source-qualified floras despite 4,505 GIFT original botanical population. This is a donor documentation/genera-overlap limitation, **not** demonstration that island plants do not exhibit cleistogamy.

The mean source-trained genus probability not represented by any permitted genus donor is approximately **7.7% northern**, **7.1% tropical**, **16.2% southern** in the all-recorded floral-form-scored cohort. In the **nested known-native** original floral-form cohort, these mean missing-source-genus proportions are approximately **20.2% northern** and **14.8% tropical**; the South has no sufficient native donor cohort for such a calculation. These are differences among **different eligible cohorts**, not paired counterfactual effects of choosing native status, nor biological dispersal failures. Larger genus-source support gaps also do not establish pollinator scarcity.

The original Direct floral-form cohort has on average around **148** directly recorded botanical plant species per scored North island, **62** Tropical and **89** Southern in all-recorded, compared with **94** North and **66** Tropical in native-only nested source-qualified subsets. The huge spread in source island Direct trait record counts is a separate reason to require proper-score, grouping and finite-sample diversity tests rather than just comparing raw category volume or point estimates.

## Mechanistic interpretation still unidentified

Native-only source exclusion preserves original species-status labels rather than assigning phylogenetic island provenance. Even strict native records do not identify endemic phenotypic **change**, because identical species-level Direct traits are projected across island occurrence checklists. Distinguishing **arrival/establishment selection** from **adaptation after establishment** requires true confirmed common mainland/island native accepted-species contrasts, independently measured local phenotypes or anchored ancestry and effective visitor/fitness data. The current [source all13 proper-score result](README.md), [direct all13 local-vs-external CI](ALL13_DIRECT_PAIRED_REFERENCE_CI_20261120.md), and [no true joint floral architecture×assurance predictive advantage](ARCHITECTURE_ASSURANCE_TRUE_JOINT_AND_GENUS_NULL_20261120.md) remain valid **only for their explicitly documented source-eligible floristic target cohorts**.

The simultaneous [three-subsample equal-N source donor sensitivity](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/pull/340) is designed to test whether geographical local-vs-external floral source advantages survive matching the numbers of training-source species. No evidence result from that still-running source experiment is being incorporated in this 2026-11-20 support census.
