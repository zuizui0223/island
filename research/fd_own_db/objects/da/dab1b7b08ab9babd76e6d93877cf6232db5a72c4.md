## Test and strict original-model identity

Prior [NHM flower corolla tube-length genus-heldout model](../20261116_nhm_numeric_tube_source/README.md) predicts original island `tube_depth_class == deep` from DIFFERENT accepted species in potential mainland GIFT source regions. Every target genus is **excluded from the numerical-length→categorical-depth calibration**, so the original target genus Direct ordinal depths are not directly fit in its own model. GIFT candidate source accepted names that occur on the focal island are removed. Source modes: geographically nearest five and climate-nearest ten; both evaluated on **identical focal island Direct plant names**.

The new source-validated implementation reconstructs the SAME island×accepted-plant target Brier score using private genus sufficient statistics only (focal Direct n and Deep n, source NHM numeric donor count, source genus checklist breadth and genus-specific calibrated Deep probability). Compare (i) original species-equal genus-specific prediction vs the **identical donor species pooled**, (ii) genus-equal Brier improvement within each island, (iii) genus-specific vs a genus-equal donor-pooled reference, (iv) rank original genera by **documented focal carrier numbers only**, and exclude the top one or five, or each island's most documented genus. Both source pooled references are **recomputed after removal**. Other sensitivity gates: effective focal genus count >=3; two source genera with >=2 independent accepted NHM numeric donor species in EACH original source mode; source/island Direct documentation differences <=10 percentage points.

**Original exact numerical replication:** all **22** prior valid original geographic-block × source-mode Brier gains across 30 region×native-status×NHM-tier panels are identically reconstructed to max absolute difference **4.1633×10⁻¹⁷**; original island eligibility and cluster counts identical. ## Scientific claim boundary

**Positive supported:** independently published species-level NHM **centimetre corolla tube length** predicts original Direct ordinal tube-depth on a selected, source-qualified tropical island floristic community when potential mainland source congener GENUS is known. The positive predictive improvement is **not solely dependent on the five most frequently recorded genera**; genus-equal weighting and outcome-blind genus removals retain it under both original source choices. This strengthens genus-associated **functional trait conservatism/source-pool assembly**, not a unique bird/butterfly/hawkmoth pollination syndrome, evolution of local island flower depth, or causal establishment filtering.

**Still unidentified:** unbiased native-only floral selection, source-independent provider robustness, effective plant-specific pollination, bird/bee/Lepidoptera guild takeover, historical pollinator loss or adaptive within-lineage island morphology. Source genus and Direct/NHM annotation opportunity is highly nonrandom, and <=10pp documentation overlap yields **only 8 tropical all-recorded islands**. Further reweighting or regression cannot fill truly unrecorded traits without untestable assumptions. Do not claim the 95% block intervals represent a fully ascertained island flora effect.
