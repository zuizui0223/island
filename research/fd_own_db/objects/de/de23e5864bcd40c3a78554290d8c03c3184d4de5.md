## Decision and explicit nonclaims

1. Changing a global genus pool to a region-local in-sample genus pool does **not** abolish the long-distance northern-midlatitude or southern-extratropical descriptive residuals, but they are dominated by geographies separated by >2,000 km and share botanical/source histories.
2. Strict source-native regional residual crosses zero under block deletion; the earlier native/all matched geographic result remains a major falsification of a general, provenance-invariant adaptive-filtering claim.
3. The small tropical 500–2,000km n=318 signal is a **post-hoc specific spatial-scale candidate**, not a new confirmed ecological mechanism. It must be stress-tested with **non-reused spatial blocks** and out-of-source/taxon matching before further discussion.
4. These Direct flower colour/form profiles remain **species-wide fixed labels**, not actual within-species phenotypic evolution or plant-pollinator interaction effectiveness. No independent ecological colonization, pollinator decline, historical visitor switch, or fitness consequence follows.
