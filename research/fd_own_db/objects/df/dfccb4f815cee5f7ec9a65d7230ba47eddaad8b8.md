# 15 November 2026 — Independent published textual flower traits: source-only donor and original 4,505-flora evidence gate

## Purpose and population

The FD main analysis is the original **4,505** GIFT botanical island floras, not the 928 all-three-trait-complete islands or selected 215 source-matched focal islands. Ten original species-direct flower/reproductive traits and three genuine two-trait combinations remain independently scoped to every eligible island. The observed colour×form assemblage overlap even without shared plant species is a recorded floristic composition result, not effective pollination or trait evolution.

Previous strict heldout GIFT original source [audit](../results/20261115_global_island_functional_beta/STRICT_GIFT_OUT_OF_NAME_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) found that **zero** globally independent, directly documented flower colour+form species remain in GIFT native mainland lists after excluding all accepted names occurring on any original 4,505 island floras. The original species-wide Direct ledger is not an independently measured mainland donor phenotype catalogue.

To test an **independently published primary flower description source**, use the Natural History Museum (London) [plant-traits](https://github.com/NaturalHistoryMuseum/plant-traits) CC BY 4.0 NLP/description dataset, original Git commit `4843c22bb2fc91018d9828dcddebf067c83b1097`, `output/raw/ocr-traits/combined.csv`, git blob `e1cfd2d5c518c2061ec91d1a9e8b64fc7ca69389`. The README reports ~59,369 botanical species, ~888,328 automatically extracted traits, with 13,315 flower-colour, 14,146 corolla-colour, 8,759 petal-colour, 1,124 flower-shape and 146 flower-symmetry trait observations. It aggregates BHL biodiversity literature, eFloras (including Flora of China/North America/Pakistan) and Ecoflora descriptions. These are independent **publication source records**, not independent *field measurements of corresponding GIFT mainland source plant populations*; a species description might be drawn from the same taxonomic publication as the original Direct ledger.

## Frozen source-specific identification gates

1. **External dataset identity and rights:** download the actual original CC BY 4.0 `combined.csv` from the immutable NHM git commit, not moving `main`, verify the original git blob SHA before parsing, record SHA256 in the anonymous output and cite the repository. Do not publish the full original source CSV to the protected island analysis artifacts; the independent input is public under its original license.
2. **Exact taxon matching:** use only exact, strictly formed two-token accepted botanical binomials to compare external `taxon` and original GIFT `accepted_species` or `work_species`. Multi-rank names, authorship strings, synonyms and unverified spelling replacements are **not** silently mapped to the original accepted taxon. A WFO link may help a later strict identifier crosswalk, but cannot be assumed to match the original CoL accepted species automatically.
3. **External phenotype quality:** primary flower colour only from an unambiguous normalized single-colour category in external `flower colour`. Reject colour descriptions containing multiple distinct hues (e.g. red and white), indeterminate text or suspected OCR corruption; never force them into the first familiar colour. The `corolla colour` and `petal colour` expansion is separately labelled as a *sensitivity* and only accepted if all interpretable source component fields agree. Flower shape and symmetry each use their own narrower standardized vocabulary, not imputation from colour or observed pollinator genus. Conflicting species descriptions across independent NHM source rows are withheld, not majority-inferred.
4. **Direct validation:** measure the exact species-level overlap and agreement of external primary/expanded NHM flower colour, flower shape and symmetry with the original high/medium *species-direct* trait ledger. Report raw overlap, agreement, conflicts and unresolved/ambiguous fractions without turning disagreement into any biological local evolution inference. These documents may encode different flower organs, flowering phases, taxon concepts, or literature sources even when names match.
5. **Truly external source-only donor feasibility:** construct the union of **ALL accepted species on ALL 4,505 botanical island floras** before looking at external flower outcome values. Exclude that union from **ALL 748 GIFT 3.2 native mainland source entity lists**. Among the remaining mainland-listed taxon names, count externally described single-colour, shape, symmetry, and complete colour+shape species and genus groups. The constraint must hold for all original island plants, not merely the subset of 215 or 2,324 with Direct flower records.
6. **Preserve globally useful scope:** measure independent source-only donor congeneric coverage across ALL original ≥5 Direct flower-colour islands (3,132) and separately the original 215 endpoint islands selected independently by geography, plant species incidence, ≥10 two-trait Direct records and ≥10% floral documentation. Do not narrow the main study to a small complete-case joint-shape subset simply because shape documentation is sparse. All nonobserved traits are missing, not absent/monomorphic. External independent text categories should be held separate from the original *island-derived* species-wide labels in every model feature.
7. **Identification ceiling:** if external source-only genus coverages are too sparse or external-vs-Direct flower colour agreement fails, do not fabricate a new mainland adaptation coefficient or pollinator-syndrome prediction. The correct empirical output is a full-4,505 provenance-aware external-source opportunity and mismatch analysis, with a recommendation to expand independently documented colour or directly measured flower architecture before considering fitness or lineage adaptation.
