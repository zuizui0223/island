Reported original sampling methods are **Transect: 3 study-years; Random_census: 1; Focal_observation: 1**. The population represented by the **10,399** species-validated positive flower/visitor rows includes heterogeneous field protocols, and source original flower count units vary (flower cover percentages, density per m², heads, inflorescences, spikes, etc.). Pooling raw rows or flower counts as a single quantitative observation process is invalid.

**Important distinction:** the published original metadata fields Hymenoptera_percent and Lepidoptera_percent are percentages of animals in *documented interactions*, **not automatically verified that both taxa had equal survey opportunity or that their absence on a flower was observed**. Likewise a study type of 'Transect', 'Random_census' or 'Focal_observation' does not by itself verify that all unvisited flowers and all visitor guild nonvisits were observed in the same time window. Both original field sampling coverage and original flowering plants outside positive visitor relations must be verified against each study's protocol to measure relative guild preferences.
