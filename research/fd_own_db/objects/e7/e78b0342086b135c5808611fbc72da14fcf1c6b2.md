# Direct island floristic assembly: source sorting, endemic divergence, and anthropogenic introductions

**2026-11-15 original-source observational protocol, created BEFORE the new global direct three-stage outcomes.** This is exploratory and no adaptation or colonization-causality claim is preregistered.

## Whole study frame and independent source categories
- Keep all 4,505 GIFT original protected botanical island floras; geography crosswalk 8,264 entities and 4,504 region-assigned botanical floras. One original unassigned geography flora always remains in the population and original evidence denominator. Independently verified oceanic/continental geology is known for only 425 original botanical floras; never substitute distance_to_continent_km for geology or extrapolate geology to the other 4,080 islands.
- Original flora has three **SEPARATE status columns**: `origin_status` in native/introduced/unresolved; `endemic_status` in endemic/nonendemic/unresolved; `floristic_status` from source. Strict `endemic` = native AND endemic AND floristic_status endemic. Strict `native_nonendemic` = native AND nonendemic AND floristic_status native_nonendemic. Exclude any contradictory record. Any native with unresolved endemism stays unknown, **not** native_nonendemic. Introduced = original origin_status introduced, but no guarantee about seed introduction rate, residence time, naturalisation or invasive ecological establishment.
- Ten individual high/medium Direct species-wide traits (each separately eligible) and three real same-species joint pairs are retained without a triple trait completeness filter. Morphological sectors of interest: zygomorphic, tube/bell, open_radial, deepest tube; breeding system separately strict SC (SI as comparator), strict autonomous fertilization (absent as comparator); all other statuses are undocumented or physiologically different, not false negatives. Source species-wide morphology is not a local population adaptation record.
- For each island, original five ranked GIFT geographically nearest **native mainland source candidate** region lists are candidate species **opportunity**, not measured dispersal, observed introductions, seed arrivals, ancestors, true absence or proof of an exhaustive mainland source. The only rigorously observed `absent` fact is absence from the original island's recorded botanical checklist.

## Layer 1: potential source-pool versus native-nonendemic records (whole-flora sorting)
Use actual GIFT candidate native mainland species lists from the island's ORIGINAL five source regions. Compare frequencies among independently strict native-nonendemic island plant names versus distinct candidate native mainland-listed plant names **NOT recorded on that island in its FULL original GIFT botanical flora**; require both groups with >=5 true Direct trait records, >=10 original source-only trait-coded candidate names for the relevant trait. Exclude entire original island species from source-only comparator; no pseudoavailability for taxa outside the GIFT five-source list. Report whether direct island plant names are themselves represented in the candidate mainland lists and the matched mainland source scope, as a sampling diagnostic. The direct effect is source-trait proportion difference `p(native_nonendemic on island)-p(not documented on island but native mainland GIFT candidate)`. This is an OBSERVED source/flora trait sorting contrast, NOT an identifiable establishment probability. No genus conditioning for the **primary whole-assemblage** contrast: changing genus composition is the very sorting feature in question. Separately report within-genus diagnostic only where informative, never erase the raw genus-sorting signal and call it adaptation.
- Optional vegetation/occurrence breadth control: measure each accepted candidate species' original number of native GIFT mainland entities and observed GIFT island floras, independently of trait category. Controls for global recording opportunities, but is NOT true range size/arrival probability; do not condition on island spread as if it were a pre-arrival variable.
- Zero documented candidate species does NOT mean genuine island non-arrival or mainland absence.

## Layer 2: native-endemic versus native-nonendemic difference (within-island & genus)
For exactly the SAME island and accepted genus with both original strict status groups and Direct trait documentation, contrast sector proportions in endemic accepted species versus native_nonendemic species. Give one matched island×genus unit equal weight and distinguish unique accepted species, unique genera and independent physical 10° blocks. Report for each trait/region:
1. raw actual source endemic minus nonendemic frequency among Direct plant records in all original qualified islands (show denominator before testing);
2. difference among BOTH statuses observed within the SAME original island×GENUS (identify and count **informative genera with ≥2 actually different Direct category states**);
3. alternative endemic versus name-distinct mainland GIFT source congener discordance or sister taxa **only if genuinely qualified** (not presumed ancestors).
A within-genus **zero caused by zero variation** is an identification failure, NOT evidence that endemic evolution did not occur. A nonzero difference is *lineage-associated interspecific morphological divergence*, NOT proof of island-specific direction, ancestral state, pollinator-mediated adaptation, observed allele selection or repeated phylogenetic independent transitions.

## Layer 3: introduced species (human delivery, not removal of all dispersal barriers)
Compare original independently labelled introduced island species against strict native_nonendemic and all native directly documented species, AND against source-only GIFT native candidates as a descriptive cross-source range. Introductions include unknown propagule pressures, time since introduction, intentional planting, cultivation and source-taxonomic differences. Do NOT treat every introduced plant as naturally established with equal sampling or importation probability. Do NOT infer humans fully remove the dispersal barrier, naturalisation success, or a distinct contemporary establishment mechanism absent confirmed stages.

## Statistical uncertainty and reporting gates
- Main unit original island, not duplicated island×plant records; for within-genus comparisons first original island×genus, then independent 10-degree spatial block and unique-genera diagnostics. Original source/status and morphological trait documentation may be highly uneven. Report original all-4505 and region counts, status/trait response completeness and source-first signatures before any significance.
- >=30 original eligible islands from >=10 10-degree physical blocks, with >=5 directly labelled island names per relevant group and >=10 candidate source-only names when included; matched endemic/nonendemic genus condition additionally >=10 unique informative genera AND >=10 physically distinct blocks for a cross-island confirmatory interpretation. If fails, display descriptive support, **do not manufacture a zero effect or a significant test**.
- For matched-island source contrasts, report island-equal mean differences and 999 geography-block-equal bootstrap CI, separately first-nearest GIFT source group sensitivity and leave-one original block deletion when feasible; these are conditional observational uncertainty, not evolutionary replicate p-values. Use all supported 4 main regional source regimes and separately native/global cohort; 425 source geology-typed islands may be further *nonrandom* sensitivity.
- A one-focal-species source-wide Direct record cannot establish within-island plasticity and a genus is not an observed sister lineage. Reuse of the same donor source species/folio across islands, source botanical provider, source flora incomplete taxon recognition and native/endemic classification are rival drivers.

## Direct research claim ceiling and strongest next steps
The directly measured outcome is `trait composition among flora associated with candidate mainland opportunities / strict island statuses`, or `interspecific endemic-associated trait differences in support-qualified genus pairs`. No direct arrival/establishment/survival/ancestral transition/visitor effectiveness has been observed. The proposed 1,160 exact species-tree matches is unverified until confirmed with source phylogeny ledger; matched terminal tip alone is not an actual sister-tip comparison. A supported pattern can motivate phylogeny reconstruction, truly independent population-specific mainland/island flower-morphological measurement, propagule/seed-source arrivals, and realized plant–pollinator fitness observation. Main manuscript PR remains DRAFT.
