# 15 November 2026 — Original GIFT source out-of-name validation gate for global floral functional beta

## Inferential target

The main original botanical population contains **4,505 actual island floras**, with ten separately species-Direct recorded floral/reproductive traits and three actual two-trait combinations. For flower primary colour × floral form, **2,324** all-recorded islands each have at least five original species-direct high/medium single-state records on the **same accepted plant species**; the historical 928 triple-complete ≥30 cohort is only a sensitivity.

The 15-Nov equal-10/10%-documented northern-midlatitude 500–2,000 km 456-pair sensitivity retained a posthoc positive **block-disjoint (12 pairs)** regional-genus expected squared flower-profile frequency distance residual, but the original region-genus reference included the focal plant species. This is a **falsification gate**, not a license to claim selected convergent island flower phenotype evolution.

A genuinely new question is whether the original **native mainland GIFT 3.2 potential source flora** predicts focal directly documented categories **after removing every evaluated focal island plant name across ALL mainland source regions**. The outcome is proper probability prediction of documented joint colour/form categories, not island-mainland phenotypic change, actual functional visitor matching, or lineage-colonization probability.

## Independent information and non-independence

The original GIFT source has **748 candidate mainland regions**, about **1,666,186 region×name rows**, with original `geo_k5` rank-1 through rank-5 geographic source assignments. Each island's GIFT source set is a plausible historical **opportunity**, not a verified ancestral dispersal history.

The directly recorded phenotype ledger is globally **one fixed category state per accepted species**. Even if a tested species name is excluded from the mainland donor checklists, a distinct donor species may have received its Direct flower trait label from another island observation; this is **not independent mainland flower trait measurement**. The entire original accepted plant list at each test island must be excluded from every mainland source entity, not only the two-trait-recorded target plant list.

Three explicitly overlapping, posthoc support cohorts, none an independent replication: all-recorded 2,324 ≥5 Direct flower colour+form floras; original source-native 327 ≥5 Direct paired-trait floras; and the original northern-midlatitude n≥10 and ≥10%-documented all-recorded potential short-distance target universe. Retain all 4,505 island botanical floras in the original population denominator and show trait-specific attrition without silently treating missing Direct labels or unverified plant statuses as absences.

## Numeric design and potential falsification

1. **Freeze source:** original pinned SHA-256 checks for Direct trait ledger and accepted island botanical flora, independently audited GSHHG geo/region crosswalk, and original 748-region/1,666,186-row mainland GIFT source. Record actual SHA-256 of mainland/assignment files. Reject source count drift rather than silently changing cohorts.
2. **Single proper outcome:** observed accepted-species complete flower-primary-colour × floral-form state (one real joint observed category, no synthetic cross-product and no PCoA KDE volume). Each distinct source donor species counts ONCE globally and ONCE in the union of its five potential source entities regardless of repeated GIFT region-list occurrences.
3. **Strict taxon-name exclusion:** for cohort S, remove the UNION of **all accepted plant names** recorded on the cohort's original full island floras from EVERY mainland GIFT source region before constructing priors or genus/source models. Separately report what remains after excluding **all species in all 4,505** observed island floras. If there are no source-only species-direct complete colour/form donors, report **NOT IDENTIFIABLE** rather than hallucinating prediction scores.
4. **Model family fixed before reading original response:** prior from globally available eligible direct-pair GIFT species with 0.5 pseudocount/category; genus-specific posterior with 8 prior-pseudocounts; source-only donor category mixture shrink 8 to global; and source-enriched genus prediction that reweights local within-genus category deviations while holding the **global total genus donor count fixed**. The source-enhanced model must reduce to the global genus model when all or none of a genus's training species are in the nearest-source pool. Check the strict categorical negative-log-likelihood and Brier loss on exactly the same focal plants, one island vote each. This is a **posthoc conditional genus oracle** (focal genus already known), not a prospective genus colonization predictor.
5. **Support gates:** report source-known genus fraction, percent of exact evaluated target plant names that would have leaked without holdout, fraction with five-nearest geographic source donor congener, count of original source regions with direct trait donor evidence, target plant n, independent 10-degree block count, and exact donor profiles that remain. Evaluate scoring if and only if a genuine source name-disjoint and finite observed trait model exists. Any source-inclusive score must be compared with a source-placebo matched for donor support before attributing specificity to geography.
6. **Interpretation gates:** no p-values from repeated island/plant species sharing, bootstrap/Monte Carlo duplicates, five donor source regions as independent ancestors, or per-island scores treated as evolutionary replications. A positive GIFT-local score does not establish a pollination syndrome, net colonization success, true reproductive fitness, or adaptive floral evolution. A nonpositive or unsupported score does not biologically refute trait selection or recipient-site pollinator shifts.

## Reproducibility and data-rights policy

The original protected source runner is `island/scripts/fd_source_name_heldout_macro_preflight.py`; synthetic regression tests `tests/test_fd_source_name_heldout_macro_preflight.py`; standalone original source CI `island/.github/workflows/run-fd-source-name-heldout-macro.yml`. Only aggregate source-opportunity counts, holdout coverage, proper scoring summaries and digests may be published in `fd`. No original exact island IDs, full accepted species names, genus IDs, native source entity lists, exact coordinates, or per-target plant predictions are exported.

### Stop rule

If the original source association survives strict name holdout only under very poor independent genus donor support or only with a narrow selected cohort, retain **descriptive global floral-functional turnover** as the primary result, and do not promote a source-independent ecological trait filtering or selection mechanism. Prioritize new independent directly recorded mainland flower morphologies and verified native plant provenance before rerunning the evolutionary inference.
