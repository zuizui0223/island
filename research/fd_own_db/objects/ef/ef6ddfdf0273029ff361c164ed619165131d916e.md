# Main figures — source-verified aggregate draft

All five SVGs are editable vector graphics generated by `scripts/render_integrated_manuscript_figures.mjs` from `figure_data.json`. No protected identifiers, invented coordinates or new model fits. All data come from the archived numerical reports and run 45364977926's exact 12 contrast records. The source-cohort map is deliberately deferred: no coordinates are required to show the numerical support, and provisional bird crosswalks should not be depicted as confirmed polygon matches. The current figures are aggregate-data draft panels, not final typeset journal plates.

- [Fig. 1 — Floral recurrence](figures/fig01_recurrence.svg). Original 4,505-island parent; 2,324 floras have at least five genuinely same-species colour × form records. Frequency overlap across nested island pairs is descriptive; the genus-disjoint null squared distance is lower than observed. No excess evolutionary convergence implied.
- [Fig. 2 — Geographical reassembly](figures/fig02_geographical_reassembly.svg). A: all four regions for actinomorphic × deep. B: three tropical deep-tube combinations (n=666 symmetry/depth; n=598 form/depth), with original intervals recovered from run 43857181963's verified report. C: total and between-/within-genus covariance differences. D: both joint and covariance contrasts in 96 documented-native tropical floras. E: original alternate-source, first-source-group and annotation-balanced estimates, plus the nested 123 animal-matched subset. All comparisons are conditional and correlated, not independent replication. Symmetry and form categories can overlap; joint frequency is not covariance or conditional probability P(radial | deep). No direct measure of specialization or causal partner shift.
- [Fig. 3 — Potential partner geography](figures/fig03_partner_geography.svg). Bird–bee matches provisional, animal source supports not equal denominators. Distance-specific coefficients plotted as points; only available mainland resampling ranges given in text. Distance variables are correlated and modeled separately. No demonstrated guild replacement.
- [Fig. 4 — Dietary differentiation](figures/fig04_dietary_differentiation.svg). Historical public name-unit phenotype scores. All 12 planned nested contrasts, including unsupported and unavailable-range cells, retained. Whiskers are exploratory group-resampling percentile ranges, not calibrated CIs. BH q shown for tubular family/genus tests, with all 24-test counts; held-out MSE for every scope. Higher coverage subset gain is not general to lower threshold.
- [Fig. 5 — Two-sided specificity](figures/fig05_two_sided_specificity.svg). Same jointly scored original 30-island target plant cohort, candidate mainland comparators. Exact genus-grade expected coefficients and observed-minus-expected residuals; separate source definitions. Native-supported 20-island restriction is made before roster change. Four nominal passes in one common depth cohort, none Holm; all 460 feasible conditional origin bounds cross zero. Neither causal matching nor native trait response is established.
