# Strictly geography/source-held-out genus versus genus-blind predictions across original GIFT 4,505 island floras

**Original numerical rerun pending. Contract frozen 19 November 2026.** This is an observational botanical conditional prediction study, not an independent mainland ancestral trait or pollinator adaptation experiment.

## Scientific question and cohort

The earlier 4,505-island, 13 actual High/Medium Direct single/true same-plant paired endpoint study reported that other-region distinct accepted species of a known botanical genus help predict island phenotypes. But it evaluated external region genus versus local region genus; the new decisive test asks whether external-region genus-conditioning does better than a **genus-blind externally sourced category prior** after stricter geographical training exclusions.

Source original 4,505 accepted GIFT island floras; 10 single traits + 3 true SAME-accepted-species observed pairs, two nested all-recorded/native statuses, four independently assigned GSHHG macroregions, three completely refit training exclusions (same physical 10 degree block; same first potential mainland source entity; union of both). Original 312 support cells retained (13 × 2 × 4 × 3), 178 previously verified both-group supported cells. Do not replace the full population with 928 triple-trait-complete islands.

## Critical source-bias control

Remove all accepted species recorded anywhere in focal island AND other original GIFT islands in the same 10 degree block or original first candidate mainland source entity from BOTH local and external genus donor training AND the shared external-region genus-blind source prior. Region-exclusive source species cannot be present in both local and external regions; for each genus balance exact accepted donor species count and use the same categorical shrinkage. Refit source genus composition after exclusions. Score baseline and strict source models against EXACTLY SAME original target accepted plant species restricted to still-supported genera; source support attrition cannot be relabeled no biological effect.

## Proper-loss and spatial validation

For each surviving original region/status/trait/mode, take external genus-vs-genus-blind **proper multiclass Brier loss reduction AND proper log-loss reduction** and require BOTH original 499 bootstrap group-equal 95% lower bounds above zero in BOTH independent physical block and first GIFT potential candidate mainland source grouping. Original minimum 30 matched GIFT islands and 10 groups in each scheme. Negative score retained; uncertain source group or loss disagreement is not a positive finding. Compare three nonoverlapping north midlatitude, tropical and southern extratropical source macroregions: only a trait passing all 3 with both proper losses/both source clusters constitutes a conditional geographic transportability candidate. Northern high latitude stays independent sensitivity.

A positive genus-over-blind signal says **genus-associated categorical phenotype information is predictively portable** conditional on KNOWING which botanical genera exist on the focal island. It does NOT predict colonization (genus roster is provided), ancestral mainland phenotype (all donor plants occur in OTHER GIFT island floras, sharing same Direct trait ledger), independent phylogenetic trait evolution, island-local flower phenotype shift or bird/butterfly/hawkmoth effective pollination. The separate original GIFT mainland candidate source test provided zero completely name-novel Direct colour×form phenotype donor species outside all 4,505 floras. Endpoint dependence, native documentation selection and exploratory non-multiplicity-adjusted bootstraps bar universal confirmatory claims.
