## Reading

- **E1 cannot separate the explanations.** Under lineage co-occurrence E1 is positive in 75–100 % of replicates, as large
  as under trait matching. The 24 Nov 11/11-positive pattern is therefore uninformative about mechanism on its own.
- **E2 separates them at any number of archipelagos.** Under co-occurrence E2 stays at its nominal 2–5 % false-positive
  rate even with 60 archipelagos; under matching it is detected.
- **The number of archipelagos sets power, not identification.** For the weaker matching effect (strength 0.5), E2 is
  detected in 64 % of replicates with 6 archipelagos and 98 % with 15. The existing 30-island, 5–6-archipelago cohort
  already applied a plant genus × grade null and found the tubular association no larger than expected, which is the
  co-occurrence pattern, but with limited power. Range maps raise power and make the bird-genus null and the clade
  replication possible; they are not needed to define E2.
- **What the simulation leaves out.** Archipelago-level confounders that vary within archipelagos (elevation, habitat,
  island age) and favour both specialist birds and tubular species within plant genera would bias E2 upward. Genus
  composition is known exactly here; in GIFT the expectation is in-sample and uses Direct-scored species only.
