## Nulls, negative controls, and conceptual boundaries

1. **No biological negative records.** Missing WOL edges mean undocumented, never absent pollinator guilds. A nominal positive bird, butterfly, Sphingidae or bee edge shows an original documented network animal, not successful pollen transfer.
2. **No reconstructed floral syndromes.** Flower symmetry and depth, or form and depth, must be HIGH/MEDIUM species-Direct and recorded for the SAME accepted plant species. Plant phenotypes are literature species-wide, not specifically remeasured at the source WOL site.
3. **No pseudoindependent provider replication.** WOL, GloBI and Mangal aggregate literature with possible overlapping studies. Source-original study ID and date deduplication is required before claiming independent confirmation.
4. **No selected 928 global replacement.** Preserve the original 4,505 parent, 4,499 verifiable geographic subset, original Direct morphology masks and explicit WOL site/plant evidence masks. Known-native and endemic GIFT statuses remain distinct from missing and introduced.
5. **No causal dispersal/selection.** GIFT native potential mainland regions are not observed ancestors. Original island-vs-mainland within-population traits, documented insect community effort and effective seed/pollen performance are unavailable.
