The principal FD response is flower **colour × FORM joint functional category** distribution among the original **4,505** island botanical floras; **2,324** original floras contain ≥5 high/medium single-state species-Direct accepted plant species known for BOTH colour and form on that SAME original plant species (130,451 original island×plant Direct paired-trait carrier observations). The old **928 three-trait-complete/30-plant flora sample is supplementary** and MUST NOT replace the global population.

We ask whether external **mainland-listed NHM text descriptions** provide sufficiently independent same-species colour+form data to fit a mainland-source-conditioned reference for those 2,324 island floras. All source-only donor names must be: (i) exact accepted plant binomials in a verified GIFT native mainland source-region list, (ii) absent from the union of ALL names on the original 4,505 island floras, and (iii) unambiguously assigned one original source primary flower colour AND one original source described flower form on the **same species**, without reconstructing synthetic cross-product states or treating organ-colour alone as primary whole flower colour.

The text corpus is a separate published trait-extraction data source, not a newly observed mainland population, not a source ancestor, not proven independent underlying publication and not pollen-effectiveness data. Zero records mean documentation opportunity absent, NOT monochromatic, shape-free or pollinator-free flora.
