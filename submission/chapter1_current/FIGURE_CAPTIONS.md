# Main figure captions

## Figure 1 | Global scope and corrected geographic exposure
(A) Global distribution of the **8,264 corrected island units** used in the plant analysis, grouped by the four predeclared geographic strata. (B) Global distribution of the 1,248 unique sites in the independent GloPL pollen-supplementation dataset. (C) Summary of data coverage across the island trait database and GloPL. (D) Geography-audit summary: 1,113 spurious island zero distances were repaired using source-matched GSHHG 2.3.7 coastlines, one Eurasian continental split component was excluded, and 996 true continental GloPL zero distances were retained.

## Figure 2 | Database construction and analytical workflow
GSHHG geography, GBIF island floras and source-audited trait evidence were integrated into the global island trait database spanning reproductive assurance, flower colour and floral structure. H1 and H2 form the island-trait workflow. H3 uses independent GloPL pollen-supplementation experiments, and H4 links the H2 species scores to GloPL through exact species matching.

## Figure 3 | Constraint–response triangle and inferential boundary
H1 separates a positive global-average classic-island direction (H1a) from regional heterogeneity (H1b) and from the stricter, unsupported requirement that every region independently support the same direction. H2 decomposes reproductive assurance from additional floral accessibility/colour responses. Independently, H3 tests experimental pollen limitation against isolation, and H4 tests whether the H2 reproductive-assurance and accessibility states are associated with lower current pollen limitation in exact-species overlap. The dashed historical edge from past pollen limitation through selection, sorting or persistence to present-day trait composition is not directly identified.

## Figure 4 | Raw measurement-axis audit and floristic-origin divergence
The first four panels show direction-free raw-state reorganization within reproductive assurance, floral structural complexity and flower-colour composition in each geographic region under all-analysis and Direct-only evidence. These high-dimensional omnibus results are retained as descriptive phenotype audits and are not the formal test of the classic island-syndrome direction. The formal H1 instead uses the frozen seven pre-oriented indicators as a one-dimensional equal-domain score with finite-cluster inference; its global-average direction is positive but regionally heterogeneous, and strict four-region recurrence is unsupported. The fifth panel shows cosine similarity between source-backed native and source-backed introduced tropical isolation-response vectors. Reproductive-assurance vectors are nearly opposed (cosine = -0.758 all-analysis; -0.853 Direct-only), illustrating provenance-specific assembly trajectories without identifying a causal introduction effect.

## Figure 5 | H2: conditional pathway decomposition
(A) Corrected isolation coefficients for reproductive assurance and for generalized accessibility and plain colour after conditioning on reproductive assurance. With finite-cluster t references and the same frozen BH family, primary selfing-adjusted accessibility is supported in northern high latitudes (q = 0.00243) and the tropics (q = 0.01913); the tropical Direct-only estimate remains positive but is not FDR-supported (q = 0.1267). (B) Confirmatory selfing-adjusted raw colour-state responses. (C) Representative FDR-supported colour-conditioned architecture responses from the corrected analysis: a northern-midlatitude yellow/orange × large-bee-form decline, a northern-high-latitude blue/purple × deep-large-bee-tube decline, a tropical Direct-only yellow/orange × butterfly-deep-tube increase, and a southern-extratropical yellow/orange × bird-form decline. These descriptive trait combinations do not identify realized pollinators. H2 is a conditional decomposition, not a causal mediation test.

## Figure 6 | H3 and H4: ecological pressure and functional bridge
(A) In the corrected GloPL analysis, pollen limitation increases with geographic isolation (beta = 0.09191, SE = 0.03806, finite-publication two-sided p = 0.01594); the no-zero and supplemental-only sensitivities are shown alongside it. The offshore-only robustness slope remains positive (beta = 0.22031, finite-publication p = 0.02459). (B) Exact-species H4 associations for the literal Direct-only H2 reproductive-assurance score (beta = -0.29830, finite-publication p = 0.00417) and generalized-accessibility score (beta = -0.29566, finite-publication p = 0.02334). (C) Primary atomic-trait sensitivities. Negative H4 coefficients indicate association with lower current pollen limitation; they do not establish historical mediation.
