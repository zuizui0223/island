# Main figure captions

## Figure 1 | Global scope and corrected geographic exposure
(A) Global distribution of the **8,264 corrected island units** used in the plant analysis, grouped by the four predeclared geographic strata. (B) Global distribution of the 1,248 unique sites in the independent GloPL pollen-supplementation dataset. (C) Summary of data coverage across the island trait database and GloPL. (D) Geography-audit summary: 1,113 spurious island zero distances were repaired using source-matched GSHHG 2.3.7 coastlines, one Eurasian continental split component was excluded, and 996 true continental GloPL zero distances were retained.

## Figure 2 | Database construction and analytical workflow
GSHHG geography, GBIF island floras and source-audited trait evidence were integrated into the global island trait database spanning reproductive assurance, flower colour and floral structure. H1 and H2 form the island-trait workflow. H3 uses independent GloPL pollen-supplementation experiments, and H4 links the H2 species scores to GloPL through exact species matching.

## Figure 3 | Constraint–response triangle and inferential boundary
Geographic isolation is associated with a positive but regionally heterogeneous floral/reproductive direction (H1) and, independently, with experimental pollen limitation (H3). H2 conditionally decomposes the plant response, and H4 tests whether the island-associated reproductive-assurance and accessibility states are associated with lower current pollen limitation in exact-species overlap. Together these associations form the constraint–response triangle. The dashed historical edge from past pollen limitation through selection, sorting or persistence to present-day trait composition is not directly identified.

## Figure 4 | H1: global directional tendency with regional heterogeneity
(A,B) Regional estimates of the predeclared classic island-syndrome directional score under all-analysis and Direct-only evidence. Error bars show ±1.96 cluster-robust SE for display; inference uses finite-cluster Student-t and cluster sign-flip reference distributions. Northern mid-latitudes are positive but unsupported, whereas northern high latitudes, the tropics and southern extratropics are supported in both scopes. (C) Random-effects global-average scores using Paule–Mandel heterogeneity and modified Hartung–Knapp inference: all-analysis estimate 0.0691 (one-sided P = 0.0256) and Direct-only 0.0635 (P = 0.0238). Regional heterogeneity is supported (Q P = 0.000867 and 0.0232; I² = 0.819 and 0.685). Thus H1 supports a positive global-average direction but not strict four-region recurrence. (D) Descriptive tropical source-backed native–introduced raw-state cosine similarities; reproductive-assurance vectors are nearly opposed (−0.758 all-analysis; −0.853 Direct-only). Raw-state omnibus tests are not used to establish the directional H1 claim.

## Figure 5 | H2: conditional decomposition under finite-cluster inference
Corrected isolation coefficients for reproductive assurance, generalized accessibility and plain colour after conditioning on reproductive assurance where applicable. Titles report finite-cluster BH-FDR q values for generalized accessibility. Accessibility remains supported in northern high latitudes and, in the primary all-analysis scope, the tropics; the Direct-only tropical estimate remains positive but is not FDR-supported. Southern plain colour is also supported. H2 is a conditional decomposition: persistence after adjustment means measured selfing does not statistically absorb the association, not that direct pollinator selection or causal mediation has been identified.

## Figure 6 | H3 and H4: ecological pressure and functional bridge
(A) In the corrected GloPL analysis, pollen limitation increases with geographic isolation (β = 0.09191, SE = 0.03806, finite-publication two-sided P = 0.01594); no-zero-constant and supplemental-only sensitivities are shown alongside it. The separate offshore-only robustness analysis also remains positive (β = 0.22031, P = 0.02459). (B) Exact-species post-hoc H4 associations for the literal Direct-only H2 reproductive-assurance score (β = −0.29830, finite-publication P = 0.00417) and generalized-accessibility score (β = −0.29566, P = 0.02334). (C) Atomic-trait sensitivities. Negative H4 coefficients indicate association with lower current pollen limitation; they do not establish historical mediation.
