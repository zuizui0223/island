## Scientific spine

1. **H1 — traitwise recurrence and exceptions.** Seven traits are fitted separately in four regions. Self-compatibility increases with isolation in all four regions in broad All and WCVP-compatible All analyses. Other selfing, colour and structural responses are region dependent. H1 uses pointwise two-sided finite-cluster t inference; no pooled/domain score and no family-wise significance claim are active.
2. **H2 — partial separability.** Measured reproductive assurance does not absorb all floral-accessibility response. FDR support for selfing-adjusted accessibility is strongest in northern high latitudes and tropical all-analysis; tropical Direct-only is not FDR-supported.
3. **H3 — ecological pressure.** Experimental pollen limitation increases with corrected geographic isolation (beta = 0.09191; finite-publication p = 0.01594), with a positive offshore-only robustness gradient.
4. **H4 — functional compatibility.** Exact-species reproductive-assurance and generalized-accessibility scores are associated with lower current pollen limitation (finite-publication p = 0.00417 and 0.02334).

## Current interpretation

The paper does **not** claim a universal floral phenotype. Its strongest ecological interpretation is:

> **Reproductive function recurs more consistently than floral phenotype. Island isolation is associated most repeatedly with reproductive-assurance traits, while detailed floral expression varies among regions; the same isolation gradient is independently associated with stronger pollen limitation.**
