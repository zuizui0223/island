# Main figure captions

## Figure 1 | Global scope of the study
(A) Global distribution of the 8,265 islands included in the plant analysis, grouped by the four predeclared geographic strata. (B) Global distribution of unique sites in the independent GloPL pollen-supplementation dataset. (C) Summary of data coverage across the island trait database and GloPL. (D) Distribution of distance to the nearest continental boundary across analysed islands.

## Figure 2 | Database construction and analytical workflow
Island geography, GBIF island floras and source-audited trait evidence were integrated into a frozen global island trait database spanning reproductive assurance, flower colour and floral structure. H1 tests syndrome recurrence, H2 conditionally separates reproductive assurance from pollinator-facing floral change, H3 independently tests pollen limitation against geographic isolation, and H4 exact-matches species-level H2 scores to GloPL.

## Figure 3 | Inferential structure
Geographic isolation is tested directly against the floral island syndrome (H1) and experimental pollen limitation (H3). H2 decomposes the floral response into reproductive assurance and an additional pollinator-facing component. Dashed arrows denote hypothesized historical causal links from pollination constraint to trait change; these are not directly identified. H4 tests whether the island-associated H2 trait states are associated with lower current pollen limitation.

## Figure 4 | H1: recurrent floral/reproductive island syndrome
(A) Standardized isolation coefficients for the seven atomic H1 responses in the primary all-analysis scope. Asterisks mark nominal p < 0.05 for individual coefficients; the formal H1 inference is the seven-response multivariate test. (B) Descriptive mean coefficients for the three biological domains. (C) Joint multivariate q-values in the all-analysis and Direct-only scopes.

## Figure 5 | H2: pathway decomposition
(A) Isolation coefficients for selfing_core and for generalized_accessible and plain_colour after conditioning on selfing_core. (B) Selfing-adjusted raw colour-state responses. (C) Selected FDR-supported raw colour-conditioned architecture responses. H2 is a conditional decomposition, not a causal mediation test.

## Figure 6 | H3 and H4: ecological pressure and functional bridge
(A) The primary global GloPL isolation coefficient. (B) Exact-species H4 associations for the literal Direct-only H2 selfing_core and generalized_accessible scores. (C) Primary atomic-trait sensitivities. Negative H4 coefficients indicate association with lower current pollen limitation; they do not establish historical mediation.
