# Main figure captions

## Figure 1. Recurrent H1 domain orientations across four geographic strata

Descriptive mean standardized isolation coefficients are shown for the three H1 biological domains—reproductive assurance, colour, and floral accessibility/generalization—in each of the four predeclared geographic replication strata. Circles show the all-analysis evidence scope and crosses show the Direct-only sensitivity. Positive values indicate movement in the predicted island-syndrome direction. These domain means are descriptive summaries; the formal H1 inference is the seven-response multivariate Wald test within each geographic stratum. All four strata retained supported joint response vectors in both evidence scopes, while the colour component was weakest in northern mid-latitudes.

## Figure 2. H2 separates reproductive assurance from selfing-adjusted floral change

Isolation coefficients are shown for the reproductive mechanism score (`selfing_core`), generalized floral accessibility after conditioning on `selfing_core`, and plain colour after conditioning on `selfing_core`. Models additionally control for island area and climate. Generalized accessibility remained positive in all four geographic strata after conditioning on reproductive assurance, with FDR-supported effects in northern high-latitude and tropical islands in the primary evidence scope. Plain-colour responses were more context dependent, with the strongest selfing-adjusted positive response in southern extratropical islands. The analysis is a conditional decomposition and is not interpreted as causal mediation.

## Figure 3. Independent pollen-limitation pressure and the H4 functional bridge

Model coefficients and 95% confidence intervals summarize the independent H3 pollen-limitation gradient and the two literal H2 species-score bridges used in H4. H3 shows that experimental pollen limitation increases with geographic isolation in the global GloPL analysis (beta = 0.0794, SE = 0.0377, two-sided p = 0.0354). In exact-species post-hoc functional triangulation, higher `selfing_core` is associated with lower current pollen limitation (beta = -0.2971, SE = 0.1035, p = 0.0041), as is higher `generalized_accessible` (beta = -0.2960, SE = 0.1294, p = 0.0222). Because these coefficients arise from different model families and predictor scales, magnitudes should not be compared directly; the figure emphasizes direction and uncertainty.

## Figure 4. Supported colour-conditioned architecture responses

Isolation coefficients are shown for FDR-supported raw colour-conditioned architecture effects retained in the v14 H2 evidence layer. In northern high-latitude islands, specialized/deep architecture declined among blue/purple flowers. Tropical Direct-only evidence showed increased yellow/orange coupling to a deep-tube component. Southern extratropical yellow/orange flowers showed mixed form and tube-depth restructuring. These responses demonstrate context-dependent reorganization of colour–architecture coupling and are not observations of realized pollinator identity.
